▸ **Goal:** re-plant them in the church, give Joe the first word, and count the cost of the night.

The doors exploded. # MOVEMENT 2 — The Battlefield *(the graveyard by daylight — 25 minutes)*

▸ **Goal:** the promised morning investigation — battle-signs, the retreat trail, the crest payoff — ending at the north gate with Urwin's counsel. Dread up, weapons away: nothing attacks up here.

🖼 ![[Scene - The Battlefield Morning.webp|500]]

📖

> [!readaloud]
> The graveyard by daylight is an argument that nobody won. Headstones toppled, turf ripped in long claw-furrows, black feathers and blacker ichor freezing into the mud. The bodies you dragged out are where you left them. The bodies you *didn't* kill — the other faction's dead — are simply gone.

🎲 **Investigation** (let them work; feed by check):
- **DC 12 Investigation — battle-signs:** two engagement lines, neither centred on the church. The church was an *obstacle* in someone else's battlefield. The heavier force gave ground all night, north.
- **DC 15 Survival — wolf tracks:** the wolf pack came, fought, and left *in formation* — no scatter, no scavenging. Dominated, coming and going. (Confirms the Arcana 25 read; reward the Strahd-controls-wolves theory. **Say no more than the tracks say.**)
- **DC 15 Perception — the wight's trail:** black-fletched arrows, and a scorch-line where something hit it hard. A dragging footprint trail — **north, through the gate, into the old quarter.** *(It didn't make it out. They'll meet it in Room I.)*
- **The retreat trail** *(free, once they look north)*: everything that survived went the same way — through the north gate of the churchyard, into the **old quarter.**

🎲 **★ Donovich's crest tour — and the key to finding the crypt ★** — walking the family plots (his promised tour), he stops at a leaning row of stones. Last night's nat-1 becomes this morning's grace:

> [!readaloud]
> "Forgive me — last night, by candlelight, I could not place it. In daylight there is no mistaking." *(his thumb traces the worn crest — then holds it beside the seal from the party's letter)* "**These are von Zarovich graves.** Cadet lines. Cousins. The family's quiet dead." *(a pause)* "The crest on your letter is the same tree, my friends. A different branch."
- This **confirms the two-bloodlines theory at last** — they built it themselves across two sessions; hand them the win.
- **And it gives them the search key:** the old quarter holds a dozen leaning tombs, but only one family raised *mausoleums*. *"If something sleeps under the old quarter, it sleeps under **that tree.** Look for the crest. I will come as far as the door."* **Donovich walks with them** — he's tonight's guide to the right crypt, and his lore matters at the door.

🎲 **The north gate — Urwin's counsel.** He walks them to the gate and no further (the Keepers watch the surface; someone must). Quiet, plain, certain:

> [!readaloud]
> "The old quarter's been closed longer than I've been alive. Whatever she's dug up down there, it was buried by people who knew more than us." *(a beat)* "And the wolves that bled her dead last night — they answer to the castle. The Count doesn't want her here either. Being *somebody's* pawn is the cost of standing on this board. Come back up alive and we'll argue about whose."

> [!gmonly] Seed the Harbinger
> Somewhere in this movement, far off toward the old quarter: **stone-on-stone. A blade, dragging. Circling.** One beat, then gone. Don't comment. They know that sound — and they think they scared it off once.

# MOVEMENT 3 — The Old Quarter & the Dig Camp *(20 minutes)*

▸ **Goal:** find the crypt. The old quarter is a search, not a signpost — short, moody, and rewarded — ending at the mausoleum and the camp of the dead diggers outside it.

🖼 ![[Scene - The Old Quarter.webp|500]]

📖

> [!readaloud]
> Past the north gate the graveyard gets older. Headstones lean at drunken angles under black dripping yews; a roofless chantry stands hip-deep in brambles, its carvings weathered to whispers. The retreat trail scatters here — claw-furrows and drag-marks fanning out among the stones like water finding drains. Somewhere among these tombs, the dead went to ground.

🎲 **Finding the right crypt** *(a short search — three ways in, any one suffices)*:
- **Follow the trail — DC 13 Survival:** the heaviest drag-marks converge on the far north end, behind a screen of yews.
- **Follow the crest — free with Donovich:** he moves stone to stone, tracing crests with his thumb — and stops at the one that matches. *"Here. The same tree."*
- **Follow the work — DC 12 Investigation:** fresh cart-ruts, deep ones, all running to and from one point. Nobody carts spoil from a grave they haven't opened.

📖 *(the mausoleum)*

> [!readaloud]
> Behind the yews, the old quarter's one true building: a **mausoleum** of black-veined marble, half-swallowed by bramble, its roofline carved with a thorn-vine that circles the whole structure like a crown. And in front of it: **industry.** Churned mud. A hand-cart. Tools stacked neat as surgery. A firepit, cold. Bedrolls for a dozen — none slept in. The camp of a work crew that went inside and didn't come out.
>
> The door itself is a single slab of stone, and it is **shut.**

🎲 **The camp** (feed by check):
- **DC 12 Medicine / Investigation** on two corpses half-buried in the spoil: worked to death and then *dry* — bloodless. On their arms: **crude number sequences and gang marks.** The same prisoner tattoos as the spawn from the siege. (Reward the theory: *the servants are made from prisoners — and so was the labour force.*)
- **DC 12 Investigation — the dig:** they started a **shaft** behind the mausoleum — pit props, ropes, a wheelbarrow still half-full — then *abandoned it unfinished.* Whatever way in they found instead, it wasn't a shovel. *(This is the bypass — Movement 4.)*
- **DC 15 Perception:** among the boot-prints, one set that is *wrong* — barefoot, too long, walking weight with no stride variation. It has circled this building **many times.** The freshest circuit is from *this morning.*

⏭ **The door.**

# MOVEMENT 4 — The Blood Door *(the mini puzzle — 20 minutes)*

▸ **Goal:** the way in — a door that asks *who you are*. The solution is Lucian. Give the table time to work it; give Joe the moment when they do.

🖼 ![[Scene - The Blood Door.webp|500]] *(this is also the puzzle prop — leave it up and let them study it)*

📖

> [!readaloud]
> Up close the mausoleum door is a masterwork: a single slab, no seam, no hinge, no lock. Carved across its face, a great **tree** — the same crest Donovich showed you, root to crown — and wound through its branches, a **thorn-vine** in relief so deep the barbs stand free of the stone. At the heart of the tree, where the trunk splits: a shallow **chalice basin**, stained dark, a channel running from its lip into the carved roots below.
>
> Beneath the basin, letters in old Barovian. Father Donovich reads them slowly, twice, before he'll say them aloud:
> *"THE TREE DRINKS ONLY OF ITS OWN. KNEEL, KIN, AND BE KNOWN. ALL ELSE FEEDS THE ROOTS."*

🎲 **Reading the door** (feed by check — every clue points the same way):
- **DC 12 Investigation — the stains:** the basin and channels hold blood in layers. Many old spatters — panicked, flung, *wasted* — and around the threshold, two more drained corpses with prisoner tattoos, tangled in the carved roots as though the stone itself pulled them close. *All else feeds the roots* is not a metaphor. But over the old mess: **one clean pour**, recent, that ran the full channel to the roots. *Someone's blood worked.*
- **DC 13 Arcana (Dallas's moment):** the ward isn't keyed to undeath, faith, or force — it's **blood-heraldry**, old and proud: *the door is a herald. It doesn't ask what you are. It asks who.*
- **Donovich, quietly:** *"A family door. I have read of these. It will know its own to the last drop — however far the branch has bent."*
- **Jasper's notebook beat:** if he sketches the mechanism (he will), his monster-hunter eye adds: the thorns aren't decoration — they're the *mouth*.

🎲 **Offering blood — run each attempt as its own beat:**
- **Any ordinary blood** *(a wrong answer, not a punishment)*: the thorns flex — actually flex — and drink. **1d6 Necrotic** to the donor as the vine takes more than offered. The channel runs, reaches the roots… and stops. And somewhere below the ground, a **bell tolls once.** Low. Patient. *(Each failure = one toll. Three tolls and the remnant below is awake and ready — see Room I.)*
- **★ Lykta** *(dhampir — if she tries)*: the thorns drink and *hold* — a beat too long, tasting. Her whispers hear it plainly: a voice of dry stone asking, without language, ***"Name yourself."*** Then rejection, almost regretful: kin of the night, not of the tree. 1d6 Necrotic. The bell does not toll for her. *(Pass Nat the moment: the door didn't refuse her. It just didn't recognise her.)*
- **★★ LUCIAN ★★** — **hand Joe private note #1 the first time he comes within arm's reach of the door:**
> *Note #1 — "As you step close, the carved thorns move. Slowly — a creak of stone — turning toward you, the way flowers turn toward sun. Nobody else sees it. Something in this door knows your blood is near."*
- **When his blood touches the basin** *(the thorns drink **deep — 2d6 Necrotic**, a real cost)* — read it:

> [!readaloud]
> The thorns drink — and this time the stone *pulls*, greedy, taking more than was offered. The channel floods. The carved roots light ember-red from the ground up, and the fire climbs the tree branch by branch, crest by crest, until the whole door is a burning genealogy — and then the door **kneels**: it sinks, grinding, into the earth, and the cold breath of the crypt rolls out over your boots like something exhaling for the first time in a century.

- **Then hand Joe private note #2:**
> *Note #2 — "As the door drinks, the old Barovian above the lintel resolves — and you can read it, though you never learned how: **'WELCOME HOME, BLOOD OF THE LINE.'** Nobody else can read it. Share this, or don't. It's yours."*

> [!gmonly] Playing the Lucian beat
> Don't gloss it. Let the table sit in what just happened: *the vampire's family tomb recognised their warlock.* Donovich looks at Lucian a half-second too long and says nothing. If the table asks Lucian directly, that's his scene — Joe decides what he admits. **Do not explain it yourself**, and do not let anyone say "von Zarovich" *at* him — the door said *kin*, the rest is S3's (*"I'll give you back your name"*). This moment is the whole reason the puzzle is a blood puzzle.
>
> **Retro-payoff coming:** Milos (Movement 5) saw Lyssa open this door *"with a cut of her palm — it knew her."* When the players hear that, they'll do the arithmetic on Lucian themselves. Let them.

🔀 **If they never try Lucian** *(the fallbacks — the puzzle must not become a wall)*:
- After the **third toll** of the bell, or ~15 real minutes, Donovich connects it: *"It wants the family. Unless one of you is hiding a crest, we need another way."* → **the abandoned shaft** behind the mausoleum (Movement 3). A DC 12 Athletics scramble, ropes and pit-props, dropping into **Room B** — noisy, filthy, and the remnant below is *fully alerted* (the Room I fight becomes a prepared defence, no surprise possible).
- **Brute force:** the slab shrugs off everything — but the *thorns* don't. Burning or hacking the vine clear takes 10 minutes, deals a parting **4d6 Necrotic** lash to whoever finishes the job, opens a crawl-gap… and tolls the bell continuously. *(And the vandalism is noted below: Lyssa's S3 greeting gets colder.)*

⏭ **Down the stair.** Donovich stays at the threshold — *"I mind the door. It's what I'm for."* Weapons out.

## L — The Warden's Alcove *(off the rotunda — best found after the fight)*

> [!readaloud]
> Off the great chamber, a quiet alcove holds two stone biers, side by side like an honour guard's post. One carries the pieces of an ancient harness — dead plate, black with age — laid out with ceremony, and a sword-shaped absence in the dust where something rested for a very long time and now doesn't. The other bier is empty, and the dust says it always has been. The plinth inscription is worn by centuries, not chisels. What's left of it reads: *"…SWORN PAST DEATH…"*

🎲 **Detail:** the empty bier was kept for a body that never lay down — the warden who swore past death never got to rest. Found *after* the fight, this room reframes the kill: they didn't slay a monster, they **relieved a sentry**. If Lykta's death-whisper beat landed in **K**, this is where it makes sense out loud. Nothing to loot; everything to understand.

## M — The North Chantry *(the ravens' way in)*

> [!readaloud]
> A cross-shaped chantry, its vaulting half fallen — one thin crack of grey daylight leaning through the rubble like a plank propped against the dark. Black feathers drift across the fallen stone. And very faintly, from somewhere up that crack, you can hear ravens — the first ordinary sound the crypt has offered you.

🎲 **Detail:** the crack is too narrow for a person, wide enough for a bird — this is **Urwin's route** if the emergency reveal fires in **K**, and how the Keepers have known more about this crypt than they've said. A PC who calls up it gets, after a moment, one answering *craw*. Sef's raven-feather charm (Jasper's pocket) feels warm here.

## N — The Sealed Stair *(the close)*

🎲 **On the way here:** if they spared the wight in **I** — **now** they find it on the entry stair. Two pieces. One stroke. Cauterised black. Nobody says anything.

🖼 ![[Scene - The Sealed Stair.webp|500]]

> [!readaloud]
> The last vault ends at a door that isn't a door: a single slab, twice head-height, carved edge to edge with the raven-and-sun — and it has been **forced.** Not smashed: *unmade* — the wards chiselled out in the same methodical hand as the names, then the slab levered aside with engines. Beyond it, stairs spiral down into air that is colder, older, and faintly **green.** Far below: candlelight. And something breathing — wet, patient, too many lungs.
