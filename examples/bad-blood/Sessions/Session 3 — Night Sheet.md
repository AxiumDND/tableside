## 🕯 The passage *(no combat — ~15 min)*
Sentinels usher, never fight (*"She is expecting you."*). Attacked → they melt away, **rejoin the Trap later at the worst moment.** Lykta only: the voices aren't the spawn's — *"You hear us. Fifty years, and you are the first."* Lens up = threads of light, every flame → the wall.

## 🎭 The talking *(3–4 min — do not rush the silence)*

![[Scene - The Inner Sanctum.png]] ![[Lyssa von Zarovich.png]]

1. **The naming** — she confirms everything airily; never apologises.
2. **The REVEAL** — Strahd invited them; pest control for the family tomb. Same breath, Choir to Lykta: ***"She does not lie. Not in this."*** *(Letter raised? "He answered with silence.")*
3. **★ The OFFER — Lucian ALONE:** his name + her side, priced at the party. Deliver to Joe in character, **then WAIT.** Others bargain → *"You have nothing I want."* (Eyes skip off Lykta too fast.) Accept/feign → *"Start with the little medium."* — she buys exactly **one round** of a feint (turncoat strike = Advantage).
4. Talking done → hand on the iron doors: *"And these are my children. Let's see how they feed."*

## 🔓 The Binding *(3 locks — track with 3 coins/dice)*
- **Anchor-stone ×2** *(carved slabs, mid-room)*: Chime adjacent (auto) · holy water (auto) · one radiant hit (AC 15).
- **The seal** *(sarcophagus, far corner)*: opens when both stones down — **or instantly: Lucian's blood + Signet on the lid** (1 action). Pocket fallback: his death-blood.
- **Choir to Lykta, top of each round (always true):** R1 *"The sun-stones. The stones are the locks."* · R2 *"The bell you carry was cut for our funeral. Ring it."* · R3 *"The blood beside you is a key. The wall knows him."*
- **First crack → Lyssa's tell, loud:** ***"THE STONES — kill the medium, NOW."***

## 🌑 The Unchaining *(3 rounds, overlays the fight — read-aloud + image in [[Session 3]])*

![[Scene - The Unchaining.png]]

Top of each round: **everyone** in the room **DC 15 Wis** or 10 (3d6) psychic *(yes, it can finish dying PCs — that's the design)* · spawn drop dead (R1) · mind flayers **−10 necrotic/round, Mind Burst stops recharging** · vats burst · **★ LYSSA CANNOT LEAVE — killable** · Warden alive? **released too** (second release whisper to Lykta).
**End of R3:** cold pull of air toward the mountains → silence → to Lykta only: ***"We have you."*** Never explain it.
