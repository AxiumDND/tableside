# MOVEMENT 0 — Cold Open *(two figures in the mist)*

▸ **Goal:** Sef arrives with Jasper — Urwin's ravens found him and she went out to get him. Pick up exactly where 0.5 ended, then get moving fast.

📖 **The opener — read it cold, lights down, ambience up.** *(Embedded here so you don't flip files at minute zero; the same recap lives in [[Session Recaps & Aftermath]]. Show the siege image as you start.)*

> [!readaloud]
> Last time: a letter none of you could trace — a job, a road, and a bottle of wine so dark it drank the light. Then a fog that rose the *wrong way*. Lucian kept his head as it swallowed the camp. Dallas threw light into it — and swears he saw faces pulling back from the glow. No stars overhead. Not the same trees. Lykta asked her cards whether to trust the path opening in the mist, and the cards said *yes* — and the wine had turned black as treacle. So you drank it, ash-sweet and sickly, and the fog leaned away in one direction. You left a vial by Jasper's pack with a note — *"if we are not here, drink it and follow"* — and you walked.
>
> The path ended at an iron gate flanked by statues of kings too old to name — and at the thing that guards it. Tall as three of you. Patient. Dragging a runed blade that sang against the stone. Your steel barely slowed it; only radiant light made the runes along that blade gutter. You took the high ground, and it stopped, looked up at you for a long moment, and stepped back into the fog like a servant called away. It never spoke. It never hurried.
>
> Beyond the gate: black pines, a bruise-coloured sky, and a castle on the mountain that watches and never comes closer. A man named Urwin found you in the dark — grieving, plain, and certain, a raven never far from him. He named this land **Barovia** and warned you not to cast your voices beyond the valley: *he* will be listening. Lucian talked him into a contract on the spot — per person, per hour, plus damages — and Urwin, heaven help him, agreed.
>
> He walked you through a village that doesn't come out at night to the only lit building left: a church, its symbol of the dawn worn almost smooth. Father Donovich, white-haired and stubborn, preaching quietly over broken pews. Families sheltering in the undercroft — children pale and too quiet, not enough life in them, two of their number already three days buried. Lykta put a small light into a child's hands and goodberries into the priest's, and the old man wept. Something at the altar caught your eye — you never got the chance to ask. And when Lykta reached out for her whispers, she heard *nothing*: a soul-shaped hole where a presence should be.
>
> Then her scout came back from the dark with the shape of the night: wolves and dead things ringing the cemetery, drifting inward from every side, water finding its level. Urwin whispered to a raven, and the raven flew east. Father Donovich said only: *"Prepare yourselves."*
>
> That was an hour ago — or three days. The Mists keep no honest clock. This is now:

> [!readaloud]
> You're in the bell tower looking out, rain fine as ash on your shoulders. The cemetery edge is thick with shapes — not charging yet, still massing, drifting in from the tree line and the Mist like water finding its level. Below you the church breathes light through its shutters. It is the only warm thing for a hundred miles.
>
> And then: two figures, coming fast along the road from the east.
>
> One is tall, stumbling, gear clinking — running like someone who hasn't slept. The other is smaller, moving differently: quick and certain, cutting between the headstones like she knows exactly where each shadow is, pausing to let a cluster of shapes drift past, then moving again. She's *steering* him. And she's winning.
>
> *(to the table, out of the read-aloud:)* Dallas, Lykta, Lucian — you know that run. That's Jasper.

> [!tip] Table note — the first five minutes
> Don't ask for rolls yet and don't open the map discussion. Let them shout down the tower, wake the church, throw the gate — momentum first, mechanics later. The session starts *moving* and should feel like it.

🎲 **Sef Martikov** and **Jasper Alderwick** ([[PC — Jasper Alderwick]]), running for the gate together.

At the end of Session 0.5, Urwin spoke to a raven — he sent Sef out into the Mist to find the missing fourth member of the party. She went. She found him. She's been guiding him back through the graveyard edge for the past half hour, in raven-hybrid form where needed, on foot where not, using her knowledge of the village's dark corners to stay ahead of the massing creatures. > [!readaloud]

She's breathing hard but her eyes are clear. Urwin meets her across the room and she just says: "Found him, uncle. Ravens had him east of the old mill."
>

🔀 **Jasper's arrival — what happened to him:**

He was scouting ahead when the Mists came in. The road vanished; so did the party. He wandered — hours or longer, impossible to say — until he stumbled on the party's original campsite. Their fire-ring, their boot-prints. And left for him: a vial of black wine and a note in Lykta's hand: *"We are alright. Follow if you can. Drink this first — it helped us find the road."*

**The wine question — leave it open.** Don't tell David whether Jasper drank it; ask him in character when the moment comes. Either way works: if he drank, the path to the village simply appeared through the grey; if he didn't, the Mists held him longer until Sef found him regardless. The vial may still be in his pack. Let the party notice.

After the camp, Sef found him east of the old mill — sent by Urwin's ravens, quick and certain, in raven-hybrid form some of the way. She said: *"Your friends are with my uncle. Come now."* They moved together through the village outskirts as night drew in, fought through a cluster of undead on the approach — nothing that broke them, but enough to arrive with blades already drawn. Sef knew the gate latch in the dark. She got him through.

📖 *For David — Jasper's first look inside, just before the reunion:*

The church is small and crowded: villagers on the pews with bundles at their feet, a priest moving between them with a taper, ravens lining the rafters like a second congregation — and every single one of them is looking at *you.* And there, by the altar — alive, whole, arguing about something in low voices — the three people you last saw when the road still existed.

**The scholar's question (Glen / Dallas):** the Mists are the biggest arcane phenomenon Dallas has ever stood inside, and now there's a witness. **Sef, watching:** she's on her bench through all of this, listening. Her own question lands in Movement 1 — but if the wine debate gets going, she shrugs: *"Asked him the same thing on the road. He didn't answer me either."*

# MOVEMENT 2 — The Siege

▸ **Goal:** three waves of mounting pressure. Each wave has something that specifically reaches the snipers — the gallery isn't safe, the walls aren't safe, the air isn't safe. *(The bell tower is Lucian's problem tonight — see the ★ note at the top.)*

> [!tip] Running the waves
> A breath between each wave (10–20 seconds — repositioning, not a rest). Keeper allies: each round one downs a wounded ghoul or Helps a PC. Scale by adding/removing **ghouls only** — never touch the spawn, wight, or prototype numbers.

> [!gmonly] ★ The Wereraven Reveal — emergency tide-turner ★
> Urwin and Sef fight as humans throughout the siege — until the party is getting overrun. Watch for the trigger: a PC goes down, the villagers are seconds from being reached, or a wave is about to break past the barricades unchecked. **That's the moment.**
>
> Urwin shifts first. No announcement. Mid-sentence if needed. A ripple runs along his arms, his shadow on the wall blooms into something vast and winged, and then he's something between a man and a raven, tearing through a ghoul with his bare hands. Around the room, the two Keeper allies follow. The players suddenly understand what they've been fighting beside.
>
> If the situation is truly desperate — Sef is present and conscious — she shifts too. She's younger, faster, and absolutely furious. *"Uncle said stay inside."* She did not stay inside.
>
> **In raven-hybrid form**, use the Keeper Wereraven stat block just below, but give Urwin +4 HP (35 total) and let him act with genuine aggression rather than support. Sef uses the same block at full stat. Their reveal can single-handedly stabilise a Wave 2 or Wave 3 collapse. Use it once — **don't save it until the session's over**, but don't spend it before the party actually needs it.
>
> **If the party never gets overrun:** the reveal happens quietly at dawn — Urwin shifts to raven form to send the post-siege message, and the players see it in the grey morning light as a quieter moment rather than a combat twist.

📖 *If the reveal triggers, read:*

> [!readaloud]
> Urwin doesn't shout a warning. Mid-stride a ripple runs up his arms — and his shadow on the wall blooms into something vast and winged before *he* does. Feathers where a man was; a shape between a raven and a fury tearing the nearest ghoul apart with its bare hands — and it's wearing Urwin's eyes. Across the nave the two quiet cousins change the same way, and you understand, all at once, exactly whose church you've been defending.
>
> *(If Sef shifts too:)* A second shadow blooms beside his — smaller, quicker, furious. "Uncle said *stay inside,*" she snarls, in a voice with too many notes in it — and she hits the nearest spawn like a thrown knife.

# MOVEMENT 3 — The Lady in the Rain

▸ **Goal:** the villain in the flesh. Non-combat. One minute of quiet after an hour of noise.

🖼 ![[Scene - Lyssa on the Rooftop.webp|500]] ![[Lyssa von Zarovich.webp|250]]

🎲 When the prototype falls, cut the ambience dead. Read — then show the scene, then the portrait.

> [!readaloud]
> The prototype folds — and the sound goes out of the world. No ghouls. No drums. Not even the rain. Just your own breathing, candle-smoke, and somewhere under the floorboards a child crying very quietly. Then you realise the rain *hasn't* stopped. You just can't hear it any more.

*(Beat. Show the rooftop scene, then:)*

> [!readaloud]
> On the roof of the chandler's across the churchyard — untouched by the rain soaking everything else — a pale woman in old, fine clothes stands with her hands folded, watching the way a scholar watches an experiment. "Oh, that was *good,*" she says, and somehow you hear it perfectly. "Better than I'd hoped. The Count's little feather-folk, and four strays from beyond the Mist — and you *held.* How interesting."
>

And that I've very nearly finished what he never had the imagination to start." *(a glance at the graveyard — if Sef fell, her eyes linger and nothing moves in her face)* "Do try to survive the week. I'd hate to waste a good test." And then the rooftop's empty, and the rain comes back.

🎲 She does not fight. She assesses and withdraws. She is always the one who leaves.

🔀 **If they attack her** (a spell, an arrow, the Shard's light): whatever they send *arrives where she was*. The rain closes over the space like she was never in it — then her voice, from nowhere and everywhere at once, faintly delighted: *"Oh, and* ***spirited.*** *Good."* No roll, no contest. She was never within reach; the party should leave this minute knowing they never had her.
🔀 **If Dallas flies at her on the broom:** he reaches an empty rooftop and a patch of dry slate in the shape of nothing at all — already cooling. From the tower, Lucian saw her leave *(don't say how — that's for Joe's recap too)*.

**Sef's fate — don't rush past either:**

> [!readaloud]
> *(If she lives:)* Sef is sitting against the churchyard wall where someone dragged her, white as candle-wax, two dark punctures at her collar already bruising black. When Urwin reaches her he doesn't say anything — he just holds her, and his hands are shaking, and she lets him for exactly three seconds before: "I'm *fine,* uncle." She is not fine. She's grinning anyway.

> [!readaloud]
> *(If she died:)* Urwin walks to where she lies in the wet grass and kneels, and the rain keeps falling, and nobody — not the Keepers, not the priest, not one raven in the eaves — makes a single sound for a very long time. When he finally lifts her, he does it the way you'd lift something that weighs nothing at all.

> [!gmonly] What Lyssa's words carry without explaining
> "The Count's little feather-folk" — she knows the Keepers exist. "His cousin" — she's blood-connected to Strahd and is *proud* of it. "Four strays from beyond the Mist" — she knows exactly who the party is. "A good test" — they are being evaluated as pieces. Both Strahd and Lyssa see them as instruments. The party should feel this landing without knowing yet what it means.

> [!gmonly] ★ Joe's keepsake — Lyssa and the tower ★

# MOVEMENT 4 — The Morning After *(the faction war lands)*

▸ **Goal:** physical evidence in the graveyard + Urwin's full explanation + Larnak's arrival. By the end of this movement, the players understand the bigger picture.

🎲 **First, the tower empties.** As the grey light comes up, boots on the stair:

> [!readaloud]
> Lucian comes down from the bell tower into the candle-smoke — scorch marks up one sleeve, a long scratch over his eyebrow, every spell slot spent and wearing it like a good coat. He looks around the wrecked nave, at the bodies, at all of you still standing. "Anything happen down here?"

One line back from whoever wants it, then he's part of the group again for the aftermath. *(He saw Lyssa's cameo from the best seat in the valley — that stays in your pocket for Joe's Session 2 recap. Don't play it out now.)*

## The graveyard investigation

🖼 ![[Scene - The Morning After.webp|500]]

🎲 Dawn graying the sky. Curious PCs search the graveyard. DC 12 Investigation / Survival:

> [!readaloud]
> Beyond the area where your fight was — east section, past the old yews — there are bodies that aren't yours. Ghouls and two spawn, yes. But three of them have wounds you didn't make: massive wolf-jaw prints, crushing the skull. One has claw-gouges across its back, pre-mortem, from something that was never inside the church. And then — half-hidden under a toppled headstone — a dead wolf. Not one of Lyssa's undead. A real wolf, killed by vampire claws. Both sides lost people out here tonight. This was a battle within a battle.

🎲 DC 15 Survival / tracking: the wolf pack tracks lead *away* from the church into the Mist — they never approached the building. The pack was large. Dire wolves at minimum, and something bigger directing them.

## The orders *(★ the "— L." prop — don't let them miss it)*

🎲 Searching the bodies (automatic if they search the wight or either spawn):

> [!readaloud]
> Inside the wight's rotted coat, wrapped in oilcloth against the rain, a single folded page. The hand is elegant — educated, feminine, utterly composed. It reads: *"The work at the old churchyard is nearly ready. The new servants take to the change well — better than the last. Keep the feather-folk busy — and the strangers too — until I send word. Leave no clever corpses. — L."*

🎲 This is the arc's key physical clue — see [[Handouts & Props]]. It confirms: the siege was a *distraction with a schedule*, the old churchyard is where the real work is, and the elegant hand has an initial. If they show Urwin, he goes very still: *"Feather-folk."* (pause) *"She knows what we are."*

> [!gmonly] Clue discipline
> Don't explain "— L." — Lyssa named herself on the rooftop minutes ago; let a player make the connection out loud. If nobody searches the bodies, Urwin's kin find the page at dawn and bring it to him. It must reach the party's hands before they leave.

## Urwin explains

🎲 He's already named the faction war between waves (Beat 3, above). Now he fills in what the party's found:

> [!readaloud]
> *(looking at the dead wolf)* "The Count's pack found one of hers. Cost them. But they made her pay for it." *(quiet)* "He doesn't know we're here. He doesn't care about the village — he never has. But whatever she's doing in the old places of this valley, digging up things that should stay buried — she's doing it without his permission. In his domain. And the Count does not share."
>
> *(he looks at the party)* "You're caught between two things that have been old since before this church had stones. So are we. The difference is: you can leave." *(a pause)* "Once the job is finished."

## Ernst Larnak arrives

🖼 ![[Scene - Ernst Larnak Arrives.webp|500]] ![[Ernst Larnak.webp|250]]

🎲 Too conveniently — a figure steps through the broken door. Dry. Immaculate. He steps over a body with the ease of stepping over a puddle.

> [!readaloud]

"But not *unexpected,* for those of us who pay attention." *(a gold coin spins toward whoever's nearest — too much for a conversation)* "The old churchyard at the village's edge has been *ever* so busy lately. Diggers in the dark. Someone ought to put a stop to it." *(the smile)* "Someone *capable.*" *(a folded paper appears between two fingers: a contract, absurdly formal)* "**Two and a half thousand gold** says it's you. Rest today — you've earned it. But go at first light tomorrow. Her schedule and mine do not *wait.*" *(he sets the paper on a broken pew, already turning)* "Do be discreet." Gone into the fog before anyone fully decided to stop him.

🎲 **[[Ernst Larnak]]** is Strahd's spy — here because Strahd's wolves just reported on the siege. He knows everything because the Count's animals watched the whole thing. He's pointing the party at Lyssa. Don't explain this — let the wrongness sit.

> [!gmonly] Larnak makes the offer NOW *(changed — three-session plan)*

> 🔀 **Lucian talks contracts** *(if Joe were here — he isn't tonight)*: Jasper is the Scribe — let David read the contract instead. Larnak signed without reading, which should be *more* unsettling than haggling.

🔀 **"Who are you?"** → gives his name, warm as a door left open in winter. Never answers what he does. *"A friend of order."*
🔀 **They try to grab him:** he simply isn't where they reach. He's a Spy and chooses when to be findable.

> [!gmonly] The growing picture
> Lyssa told them to "tell the Devil his cousin sends regards" — she expected them to report to someone. Now a man connected to that someone is pointing them at Lyssa. The party are pieces. The arc's whole revelation is the slow answer to: *whose pieces, exactly?*

# MOVEMENT 5 — Close *(the march to the crypt)*

## The rest, and the road *(montage — 10 minutes, then cut)*

🎲 **Level up to 7 first**, then montage the rest: the party sleeps through the daylight hours — the only safe ones Barovia has — while the Keepers watch and the church is boarded and mended. One beat each, player's choice: sharpening, praying, reading the "— L." orders again, sitting with the Brauns. **Lucian goes back up the tower to sleep** — out of pure stubbornness. If Sef lived, she spends the day loudly not resting. Long rest complete; spells and slots back.

🎲 **Urwin's counsel** — the next dawn, walking them to the east wall:

> [!readaloud]
> "The old yard's been forbidden ground since before my grandfather. Not for the dead in it — for what the dead were *keeping.*" *(stops at the wall)* "My ravens won't fly over it since the digging started. And there's an older story: a warden that comes for grave-robbers. If any of that's true, take the light with you." *(he looks at the Shard-bearer)* "Take the Dawn. And come back."

📖 *Then the march — read it slow, and cut on the last line:*

> [!readaloud]
> Past the east wall the graveyard gets older. Headstones lean at drunken angles under black dripping yews; a roofless chapel stands hip-deep in brambles, its carvings weathered to whispers. And in the middle of it: **industry.** Churned mud. A hand-cart. Tools stacked neat as surgery. One grave stands open — its stone slab not lifted but **shattered from beneath** — and stone steps descend into a cold that has nothing to do with the morning.
>
> Somewhere behind you, back toward the Mist, faint but unmistakable: stone-on-stone. A blade, dragging. *You're standing on the ground it circles.*

⏹ **CUT THERE.** Don't let anyone go down the stairs — Session 2 opens on the grave's lip.

Dawn tomorrow — with the light."* If they still insist, let them take exactly three steps into the cold before the session ends anyway: same cliffhanger, now with the door at their backs. Never run S2 content tonight.
