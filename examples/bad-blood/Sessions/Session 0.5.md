> [!tip] How to use this
> Each beat has: **▸ Goal**, **📖 read-aloud** (say it or riff it), **🎲 run it**, **🔀 if the players…**, and **⏭ move on when…**. Tonight is **mostly Session Zero** — the descent and the fight are a short, atmospheric capstone, not the main event. Go slow and warm early; flip cold and quiet the moment the Mists rise.

# MOVEMENT 0 — Session Zero
▸ **Goal:** the party leaves the table knowing each other, sharing history, chasing a goal — and you leave it holding their fears and watch order.

🎲 **Run it** (work down the list, keep it conversational):
1. **Safety & tone.** *"There'll be gore — it's a horror game — but I run it like a PG-13 film: creepy and bloody when it counts, never gratuitous. Anything lands wrong, say the word and we ease off, no fuss."* Quick lines-and-veils / X-card check.
2. **Re-introductions.** *"For the others, and for me — tell us who you are, and what you look like by firelight."* (Two dhampirs at this fire — [[PC — Lucian Radu]] & [[PC — Lykta Endrino]]. Let them clock each other.)
3. **Bonds.** *"How do you know these people? Give me one thing you've been through together."* Push for a thread between **each pair**; Natalia + Lucian's shared heritage is a freebie.
4. **The quest.** *"Before tonight — what were you all travelling toward? A job, a debt, a place you're trying to reach?"* **Write it down.** Barovia will frustrate it, and Urwin's offer will echo it.
5. **Fears.** *"One thing your character is afraid of. No real-world phobias unless you're happy to."* Note each — payoffs in [[Dread & Omens]].
6. **The watch.** *"Who's taking which watch tonight?"* You'll use it in a minute.

> [!tip] Don't apologise for a long Session 0. A warm, unhurried hour here is *exactly* what makes the cold open and the months of dread to come actually bite. Leave a gap where David's PC will slot in next week ("there's usually a fourth of us…").

⏭ **Move on when** the party feels like a *band*, and you've got fears + watch noted.

# MOVEMENT 1 — The Last Camp
▸ **Goal:** flip the genre. Lull them, then snatch the floor out — they wake stranded in Barovia.

📖 **Cold open — say this first, to the *players*, before any in-world business.** Slow, quiet table, lights low. Cut hard from it to the fire.
> There is a valley that does not appear on any map.
> You won't find the road that leads there, because there is no road — only a wrong turn taken in fog too thick to argue with, and the fog closing politely behind you. The mountains lean inward here, like listeners. The pines grow black and close. The sky holds a colour somewhere between dusk and a day-old bruise, and it holds it always — the sun stopped being welcome here a long time ago.
> They call it Barovia. The few who remain speak of it the way you'd speak of a sickness in the family: quietly, and never after dark. They keep their shutters latched and their wine the colour of old blood, and when a stranger walks out of the mist, they don't ask where from. They've learned that the mist *gives*, sometimes — and what it usually wants back.
> Because the land here is old, and it is patient, and a long time ago it learned the taste of people.
> None of which you know yet. Tonight you're somewhere else entirely — somewhere ordinary, and warm, and almost over.

🎲 Cut to black, hand out **[[Letter — The Invitation]]**, let a player read it aloud. Then show [[Scene - The Last Camp.webp]] and open on the fire.

📖 **The fire:**
> The road's been long, the last inn a full day behind you, so you make camp where the trees thin — a ring of stones, a small fire, the horses hobbled close. Crickets. Woodsmoke. The easy quiet of people who've decided to trust one another with the watch. Settle in. Pass the wineskin.

🎲 If you didn't spend it all in Session Zero, take one last warm beat — a shared memory, a small kindness — *then* take it away.

🎲 **The Mists take them.** Drop the music to **silence.** Pick the deep-night watcher. Show [[Scene - The Mists Rise.webp]].
> [Watcher's name], it's your watch. The fire's burned low. And the fog comes — but not the way fog should. It doesn't roll in off the hills; it *rises*, out of the ground, out from between the camp-stones, too white and too quiet. The fire shrinks to a dim orange smear an arm's length away. You open your mouth to wake the others —

🎲 Let the watcher try **one** thing (shout, grab someone). Then a **DC 13 Wisdom save** — succeed or fail, the fog takes them too. The others can't be roused. *Story, not a fight.* Cut to black.

📖 **Waking** — show [[Scene - The Svalich Woods.webp]]:
> You wake to grey light and the smell of a woodsmoke that isn't yours. The fire is long dead. The fog is already drawing back between the trees like a tide going out — and as it clears, it takes your road with it. Black pines lean over a valley that was not here last night, under a sky the colour of a bruise. The horses are gone. The air smells of wet stone. Somewhere ahead a raven calls, once — and then, oddly, it *waits.*

🔀 **If they search themselves / the camp:** whoever the Invitation's *token* latched onto finds it — the spent vial of dark wine (or feather). **DC 15 Arcana:** a faint, *spent* conjuration, like a struck match; it won't light again. **Do not explain it.** Gear remains; horses and road do not.
🔀 **If Natalia reaches for the spirits:** too many voices, all disoriented — pressure, not answers (see [[PC — Lykta Endrino]]).

⏭ **Move on when** the dread has landed and someone asks "where *are* we?" — into the woods.

# MOVEMENT 2 — The Thing in the Mist

**Combatants:** [[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade]] · party

▸ **Goal:** a terrifying first encounter with the **[[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade]]** — the night's only fight. They **survive, not win**; Urwin's Dawn ends it. Run it to *frighten.* Full guidance + block: [[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade]].

📖 **The stalk:**
> An hour through black, dripping pines, and the woods go *wrong.* The birds stop. The wind stops. And under the silence, a sound — low, growing — a hum like struck iron, like a blade dragged over stone, getting *closer.*

🎲 A red glow swells in the fog; the hum rises to a *scream.* Then it comes:
> It walks out of the Mist like the fog gave birth to it — towering, shrouded head to foot in rotted grave-cloth over blackened iron, faceless under its hood, dragging a slab of runed steel taller than a man, the etched characters burning sullen red. It doesn't speak. It doesn't hurry. The hood turns, takes the three of you in, fixes on one — and it starts walking.

![[Scene - The Harbinger Emerges.webp]]

🎲 **Run it scary, not lethal** (block: [[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade]]):
- **Steel rings off it** — resists nonmagical B/P/S; even magic weapons feel like hitting an anvil. Say it loudly the first time.
- **★ Radiant flinches it** — where Natalia's light lands, the runes gutter (Guttered Runes). The contrast *is* the lesson.
- **Dread of the Tomb** (10-ft, DC 15 Wis/Frightened) + **Executioner's Arc** (recharge cone) — keep them spread.
- **It can't be killed here** (no Shard; Curse-Bound Rejuvenation). 2–3 rounds of dread, then the rescue.

🎲 **The ravens herald the rescue:** as it closes, ravens **boil up screaming** out of the pines — that's what brings Urwin. Cue him (Movement 3) the instant a PC is in real trouble.
🔀 **Natalia's Whispers:** she hears *nothing* from it — a soul-silence colder than a scream.

# MOVEMENT 3 — The Keeper in the Fog *(rescue, then hook)*
▸ **The motivation, solved:** not a stranger with a bounty. Urwin ([[Urwin Martikov]]) is a **grieving, faithful man** who lost friends to the tavern and two scouts after them, and has been **praying** for help he can't give. His ravens screamed; he came running — and his **Dawn-light drives the Harbinger off**, saving the party. To him they're **an answered prayer.** Earnest, a little raw.

🎲 **The rescue — his entrance.** Time it to the party's worst moment.
> Ravens — an explosion of them, screaming up out of the pines — and a voice: "**Down! Eyes shut!**" Light blooms at the treeline, warm and gold and utterly wrong for this grey place, pouring from the open hand of a stocky man in an old coat. The Harbinger *stops* — recoils, runes guttering — and steps back into the Mist, gone. The man lowers his hand, breathing hard. "…Huh. I asked for help. Didn't think I'd get it. Name's Urwin."

![[Scene - The Dawn Drives It Off.webp]]

🎲 **If they ask what it was:** the Keepers call it the **Tomb-Warden** — *"It hunts what doesn't belong, and those who rob the dead. The Dawn holds it off. For a while. Try not to go digging up graves… though where I'm sending you, you may not get the choice."* (Don't over-explain.)

🎲 The pitch — **lead with need, not coin:**
> "There's a tavern in the village — the Blood of the Vine. Three sisters ran it. Good women; I was buying the place off them. Three weeks ago it went dark, and something started *changing* them. I've lost two people who went to look. I can't send more of mine to die, and I *can't* leave them to it." *(first grey light over the pines)* "I've been praying for someone who could do what we can't. And the Mist gave me *you.*"

🎲 **Stack the carrots:**
1. **Stranded & broke** — he's the only friendly face, and he knows the land.
2. **The job they expected** — if they mention the letter or the goal they named in Session Zero, this *is* that kind of work (an uncanny dovetail — Strahd's hand).
3. **He needs them** — people say yes to being *needed.*
4. **★ The way home** — *"You don't walk out of Barovia the way you walked in. But my people know these Mists. Help me, and I swear on the Dawn, I'll help you find your road home."*

🔀 **"Why trust you?"** → *"You shouldn't. Trust that I *need* you — that's the only honest thing in this cursed valley."*
🔀 **"Why not go yourself?"** → *"I've buried two of mine already. I'm one man."*
🔀 **"Get us home — how?"** → *"I don't know yet. Nobody just *leaves.* But if there's a road out, the Keepers will help you find it. First — the tavern. Please."*

> [!tip] The cruel irony to bank: Urwin thinks the **Dawn** sent them; the party think a **patron** hired them. Only Strahd knows. It hits twice as hard in [[Letter — Strahd's Farewell]] — *"Did you never wonder who sent you down that road?"*

⏭ **Move on when** they accept (they will — stranded, broke, needed, offered a road home). Walk them to the village.

# MOVEMENT 4 — The Threshold
▸ **Goal:** end on the shuttered tavern. Don't open it.

🎲 Walk them down as the grey day sinks toward dusk — nailed shutters, faces you feel but never see, the castle that never gets closer. Stop the moment the tavern shows.
![[Scene - The Village Lane.webp]]

> The village is a huddle of grey houses with their shutters nailed shut — not one face shows itself, though you feel them behind the glass. At the end of the lane: a tavern, dark, its sign creaking on one hinge — a bunch of grapes, paint flaked to dried-blood red. *Blood of the Vine.* No light. No sound but that creak. Urwin stops at the square's edge and comes no closer. "That's it," he says quietly. "Whatever's in there… it was three good women, once. Do right by them if you can." *(he won't meet your eyes)* "I'll be near. Dawn keep you."
