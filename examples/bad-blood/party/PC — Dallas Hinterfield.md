> [!gmonly]
> **How his kit bends the arc**
> - **Fireball + Quickened Spell** is his signature. Massive in the **Session 2 church siege** (waves of servants) and useful in the open tavern floor (S1). Watch the church interior — friendly fire on the Braun family and Sef; nudge him toward **Careful Spell** (protect up to 3 allies) so a clutch Fireball doesn't cost a child.
> - **Counterspell** can defang scripted villain beats. **Lyssa's** taunt-cameo (S2) and gloat (S3) are *social*, but if she casts to escape, expect a Counterspell — let her flee another way (Shadow Stealth / Misty form) so she survives per [[Lyssa von Zarovich]].
> - **Elemental Affinity (Acid)** only boosts **acid** damage — so it rides on **Sorcerous Burst**, *not* Fireball. He can also spend a point for acid resistance for an hour.
> - **Acid & fire ignore most Barovian undead resistances** (many resist necrotic/cold, not acid/fire) — he'll feel effective. But he brings **zero radiant**; the party's radiant/light gap (see the pitch) is not his to fill.
> - **Broom of Flying** trivialises some terrain/chase beats — fine, lean into it for spectacle, but the **Mists** still pen him in (no flying *out* of Barovia — see [[The Mists — Getting In & Out]]).
> - **Misty Step + 50 ft. fly** make him very hard to pin. Melee horrors (Alenka the Relentless Slasher) should use reach/grapple or cut off escape rather than chase.

## Description

### Appearance
- **Build** — Tiny, ancient, immaculately kept; a gnome who irons his travel clothes.
- **Features** — Bright, busy eyes; ink-stained fingers; always mid-thought.
- **Carry** — A flying broom he treats as a filing system with bristles; tinker's tools; a coin purse heavier than he looks.

### Voice & mannerism
- **Manner** — Patient, didactic, kindly condescending — *"I'm used to helping out those who aren't as smart as I am."*
- **Tells** — Explains things no one asked about; narrates his own spellwork.

> [!gmonly]
> **Hooks to offer the player (blank backstory = open door)**
> - **The Sage who can't leave a riddle alone.** The arc is built on *buried sites of power deliberately woken* ([[Lyssa von Zarovich]]'s method). A 350-year scholar of arcana is the perfect lens: let him **read the Temple of Ravenkind** (S1), the siege-residue (S2), and the crypt journal's **Bluetspur** thread (S3). Feed him an Arcana/History check at each and he'll *want* to chase Lyssa.
> - **Draconic blood, ancient curiosity.** A black-dragon bloodline that's lasted 350 years implies a long, strange lineage — easy to dangle a personal reason the **dark magic Lyssa is harvesting** matters to him (a half-remembered family rumour, a stolen relic).
> - **The relic he'll covet.** The **[[Ravenkind Shard]]** (radiant) is the one thing the party badly needs and he *can't* power up with Elemental Affinity — a nice tension: the scholar who covets it but can't wield it like he wants.
> - **One thing that scares him** (per the pitch ask): suggest *being wrong*, or *senility* — a 350-year mind starting to slip is gold in a domain that feeds on dread (see [[Dread & Omens]]).
