> [!gmonly]
> **How his kit bends the arc**
> - **He's the line the party couldn't hold.** The roster flagged "no frontliner" as the party's biggest gap — Jasper closes it. Sentinel + Vex rapier + Extra Attack means anything that tries to walk past him to the villagers *doesn't*.
> - **Sentinel is built for the Sef clock (S1 Wave 2).** *Halt* on an opportunity attack drops a creature's Speed to 0 — he can literally pin the spawn that's draining Sef, or stop a ghoul reaching the undercroft. Make sure David knows the feat before the siege; it's his hero moment waiting to happen.
> - **Natural Shard-bearer.** Confirmed: **no at-will radiant** (took Mage Hand + Minor Illusion; his only radiant is 3× holy water, 2d8 splash) — so a frontline martial holding the **[[Ravenkind Shard]]** puts its Dawnlight exactly where the spawn and the prototype want to stand. If nobody's claimed it, Donovich handing it to *the stranger who walked out of the Mist* is a lovely beat.
> - **The Longbow of Warning changes your ambushes.** While he holds it, he has Advantage on initiative and **he and allies within 30 ft. can't be surprised** (while awake). Any "the spawn drops from the ceiling" beat still *works* — it just can't be a mechanical surprise round. Narrate the bow humming a half-second before the strike; it's flavour gold and fair warning.
> - **He brought vampire answers.** Silvered rapier, 3× holy water, *Protection from Evil and Good* (undead can't charm/frighten/possess the warded target — cast it on the Sef-clock target or the Shard-bearer). Let this pay off visibly; David built for this arc.
> - **Find Familiar (ritual).** Expect a scout on the battlefield. In Barovia, a familiar in the graveyard is also a *target* — and a lovely way to show the faction war beats without a Perception gate.
> - **No darkvision.** The only PC who genuinely can't see in Barovia's dark. Use it — light discipline matters again, and it pairs with his lonely-in-the-Mists arrival.
> - **Scribe + INT skills.** Arcana/History/Investigation +5 — he's a second lore engine alongside Dallas. The "— L." orders, Mirabel-style journals, the Bluetspur journal in S3: hand the documents to *him*. A scribe reads hands, inks, seals — he could be the one who notices the "— L." hand matches nothing local, or that the Farewell letter's hand is *older than any of it.*
> - **What was he doing in the Mists?** He's been in there alone since Session 0's road — hours or days, hard to say. Fertile ground: what did he see? Give David one private detail (a lit window in the fog, a voice offering directions, the grinding of a dragged blade passing close) that pays off later.
> - **Small + Nimbleness + Stealth +5:** he fights *through* the big things. Dire wolves and ogres are terrain to him.

## In the Mists — what happened to Jasper *(Session 1 cold open)*

> [!gmonly] For James — brief David on this before or at the start of Session 1. Give him the beats; let him fill in the texture with his character voice. The wine question is the one thing to leave open at the table.

**The sequence:**

**1. Separated.** The Mists came in fast — faster than the others could react. One moment the road was there, then it wasn't. The rest of the party was gone. No sound, no light, no sense of direction. Just grey.

**2. Wandering.** Time in the Mists doesn't track cleanly — it could have been hours, it could have been longer. Nothing dangerous found him, but nothing helpful appeared either. The Mists are not hostile here; they're simply *indifferent.* He kept moving because stopping felt worse.

**3. The camp.** Eventually — inevitably, the Mists being what they are — he found the party's original campsite. Their fire-ring, a scuff of boot prints he recognised. And left for him: a small vial of *something* black and wine-dark, and a folded note. The note read, in Lykta's hand: *"We are alright. Follow if you can. Drink this first — it helped us find the road."*

**4. The wine — leave this open.** *(Did he drink it? This is David's call at the table — ask him in character when Sef or the party bring it up. Either answer works: if he drank, the path to the village simply appeared through the grey; if he didn't, he kept wandering until Sef found him anyway, guided by Urwin's ravens. The vial is still in his pack either way — let him decide what he did with it.)*

**5. Sef finds him.** A figure came out of the Mist — small, fast, certain. Moving between the shadows like she'd memorised every one of them. She introduced herself plainly: *"Sef Martikov. My uncle sent ravens looking for a stray. I think that's you."* She didn't ask many questions. She started moving and expected him to follow.

**6. The road to the village.** They moved together through the edge of the village — dark, rain-slicked, the kind of quiet that means something is very wrong. Night was drawing in hard. They fought their way through at least one cluster of undead on the outskirts — ghouls or shambling dead drawn by the noise of the massing siege — nothing Jasper couldn't handle with Sef covering his flanks, but enough to arrive at the church breathing hard and with blade already drawn.

**7. The gates.** Same heavy iron gates the party came through. Sef knew exactly where the latch was in the dark. She got him through, hauled it shut behind them, and walked him straight to the nave.
