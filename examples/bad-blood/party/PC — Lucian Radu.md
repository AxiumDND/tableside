> [!gmonly]
> **How his kit bends the arc**
> - **He carries the party's radiant.** **Starry Wisp (2d8 radiant)** is one of the few radiant sources in this party (Dallas has none). Against Barovia's necrotic-resistant undead this matters — flag it to the player as his "vampire-killer" cantrip, especially vs. [[Lyssa von Zarovich]] (Sunlight/Radiant is her weakness) and the S1 sisters.
> - **Frighten effects are half-wasted here.** **Frightful Avatar**, **Bane**, and friends lean on the Frightened condition — but many Barovian horrors are **immune to Frightened** (vampires, the Nightbringer, several RTHW blocks; check [[Bestiary]]). Don't fudge it away, but warn him *in fiction* (a foe shrugs off his menace) so he learns to pivot to **Hold Person / Hypnotic Pattern / Hunger of Hadar** for control instead.
> - **Self-healing in a healer-light party.** Vampiric Bite (Drain) + Facsimile of Life temp HP + Undead Endurance keep him standing. Good, because the pitch warns the party to bring healing — he's a partial answer.
> - **Face of the party.** Deception +7, Disguise Kit, Forgery Kit, CHA 18. He's built to *work the mystery* — lean on him for reading Milos's broken testimony, the manifest's forged-vs-genuine hand (Forgery Kit!), and any Vistani negotiation. *(Larnak is cut — his too-smooth scenes with him.)*
> - **Speak with Dead** can shortcut investigation beats — pre-decide which corpses *can't* usefully answer (the sisters are too far gone; the churchyard dead may be claimed by Lyssa) so it enriches rather than skips. See [[Going Off-Script & Failure States]].
> - **Spider Climb + Misty Step** make vertical maps (the tavern's two floors, the church) his playground.

## Description

### Appearance
- **Build** — Lean, pale, deliberately elegant; old-blood bearing gone slightly to seed.
- **Features** — Yellow eyes, black hair, a too-charming smile that shows the fangs when he forgets himself.
- **Carry** — Fine clothes he can't quite afford, a disguise kit, forged papers, and the **Dark Shard Amulet** that hums against his chest.

### Voice & mannerism
- **Manner** — *"Sarcasm and insults are my weapons of choice."* Glib, quick, allergic to being told what to do.
- **Tells** — Name-drops a title no one's heard of; spends money he doesn't have.

## Personality (from the sheet)
- **Trait** — Sarcasm and insults are his weapons of choice.
- **Ideal** — *Independence.* A free spirit; no one tells him what to do. (Chaotic)
- **Bond** — *"I come from a noble family, and one day I'll reclaim my lands and title from those who stole them from me."*
- **Flaw** — Always in debt; spends ill-gotten gains on decadent luxuries faster than he brings them in.

> [!gmonly]
> **Secret leverage — the von Zarovich hook (this is the gift of these two sheets)**
> Lucian's whole character is *a dispossessed noble owed his rightful lands* — and he's a **dhampir** walking into a domain ruled by the **von Zarovich** bloodline, against a usurper-cousin ([[Lyssa von Zarovich]]) who is herself trying to seize a "deserved Barovia." Three ways to weaponise that without rewriting his backstory:
>
> 1. **★ The blood door (S2 — this is now the big one).** The von Zarovich family tomb **opens on his blood** — *"WELCOME HOME, BLOOD OF THE LINE"* — two private notes for Joe, pre-written in [[Session 2]] Movement 4. The tomb recognises him; the Harbinger's Vengeful Tracker re-keys to him; Milos kneels to him on reflex. Nobody says "von Zarovich" at him — the table does its own arithmetic, and Joe decides what he admits.
> 2. **Lyssa's recruiting pitch (S3 — she now knows).** By the time they meet, she knows *someone opened her family's door with the family's blood.* Her eyes find him: *"You understand, don't you? Blood that was owed a throne and given a gutter. We're the same, little one."* An offer to "restore" his name if he stands aside. Tempting **exactly** the Chaotic, independent, debt-ridden noble — and a clean betrayal beat if he refuses. *(Larnak's debt-leverage hook is cut with him.)*
> 3. **The patron question.** His **Undead patron** is unnamed on the sheet — Barovia is the place to answer it. Is the whisper at his back something in the **churchyard crypt**? Tied to the buried evil Lyssa is harvesting? Or is it **Strahd himself**, the valley's apex undead, who finds an indebted dhampir noble *useful*? Any of these turns the [[Strahd von Zarovich (the unseen hand)]] reveal into something that lands on Lucian's bones, not just the party's.
>
> **Restraint:** none of this should let him *actually* reclaim a title inside a 3-shot — the value is the temptation and the cost. The Mists take them home (see [[The Mists — Getting In & Out]]); leave the deed as a hook for "next time."
>
> **Mechanical note:** Form of Dread / Frightful Avatar will *visibly fail* against frighten-immune foes — frame that as the von Zarovich dead being beyond his menace, reinforcing how far above him this league is.
