**Kit that matters here**
- **Healing:** Cure Wounds, Healing Word, Healing Spirit, Lesser Restoration, Aid, Goodberry, **Chalice** starry form (heal on every heal/attack). *The party's healer.*
- **Radiant:** **Starry Wisp** (2d8), **Guiding Bolt** (always prepared, free 3/rest), **Moonbeam**, **Archer** starry form (bonus-action 1d8+3 radiant). *The party's radiant.*
- **Control/utility:** Faerie Fire, Fog Cloud, Entangle, Spike Growth, Hold Person, Moonbeam, Wild Shape scouting.
- **Melee option:** Shillelagh (+6, 1d10+3), Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense, Vampiric Bite (1d4+3, heal/buff, 3/rest).
- **Spirit Medium / Gathered Whispers:** Message at will, **Augury always prepared**, Unearthly Scream (reaction +3 AC, 3/rest), Voices from Beyond.

> [!gmonly]
> **How her kit bends the arc**
> - **She closes the two gaps the [[Player Pitch — Bad Blood]] begged for** — healing *and* radiant — almost single-handedly. With her in the party the arc's lethality math eases; you can run the published numbers a notch closer to "as written." Don't over-correct: she's *one* healer for a squishy party (combined low HP), and concentration is precious (Moonbeam vs. Healing Spirit vs. Faerie Fire — she can only hold one).
> - **Radiant ≠ sunlight (know the rule).** Her radiant always hurts undead and **shuts off the Nosferatu's Regeneration** (S3) — but it does **not** trigger vampires' *Sunlight* Hypersensitivity. Only the **[[Ravenkind Shard]]'s** Dawnlight counts as sunlight ([[Setting Rules — Barovia]]). Tell her this so she values the Shard, not just her cantrips.
> - **She hears the dead — lean into it.** Augury + Voices from Beyond + a Spirit Medium background make her your **diegetic dread antenna.** Route omens through her: the church dead during the siege (S1); the disturbed catacomb-dead and the erased family (S2); the von Zarovich crypt-dead (S3). The dead in Barovia can **warn, beg, or lie** — see [[Dread & Omens]]. Overlaps with [[PC — Lucian Radu]]'s Speak with Dead; let them corroborate or contradict.
> - **Built defensively on purpose.** Patient Defense, AC 14, Uncanny Metabolism (regain focus + 1d6+2 HP on initiative) — her player got caught by mid-rest combat in a prior Barovia game and built to never repeat it. **Reward that caution:** her instinct to bank rests *is* the survival strategy this arc rewards (see the wave-breather pacing in [[Session 1]]).
> - **In Session 0.5:** when the **[[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade|Harbinger]]** attacks (the night's one fight), her **radiant is the only thing that flinches it** — the perfect on-ramp demonstration that radiant is the arc's answer-key, right before Urwin's Dawn drives the thing off. *(Her Whispers hear only silence from it — a soul-shaped hole — which may rattle a medium more than any monster.)*

> [!gmonly]
> **Hooks to offer the player (blank backstory = open door)**
> - **A medium walking into the most haunted valley on the multiverse.** Barovia is *wall-to-wall* restless dead. Her whole concept gets louder the moment the Mists close — overwhelming, even. A fear-seed: *the dead here won't stop talking,* and some of them lie.
> - **Two dhampirs, one party.** She and [[PC — Lucian Radu]] share the half-vampire heritage — in the domain of *the* vampire. Are they kin, coven, rivals, strangers who recognise the hunger in each other? Easy bond to build at the Session 0 fire. Strahd and [[Lyssa von Zarovich]] will both find two dhampirs *interesting.*
> - **Who does she channel?** A named spirit-guide on the other end of her Whispers is a gift you can pay off — let Barovia's powers try to *impersonate* it (a false comfort from the Mists).
> - **One thing that scares her** (per the pitch ask): suggest *silence* — for a medium, the dead going suddenly quiet is the worst omen of all.
