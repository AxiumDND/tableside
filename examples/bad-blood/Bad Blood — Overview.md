## The pitch *(as designed)*

Two published Barovia mini-adventures, stitched into one short arc with a custom middle session. Outsiders are **pulled into Barovia by the Mists**, work a job that drags them into a vampire's plot to depose Count Strahd, and are **released by the Mists** once it's done — never realising the snare and the door home were both the Count's doing. The party starts as stranded investigators and ends as Strahd's deniable instrument.

> [!abstract] The through-line
> **Lyssa von Zarovich** — a distant relative of Strahd — is quietly sabotaging Barovia: seeking out old, half-buried sites of power and deliberately *stirring the evils sealed inside them*, to sow chaos that weakens the Count's grip and to harvest dark magic for her experiments.
>
> The **Keepers of the Feather** — the wereraven network led by [[Urwin Martikov]] — shelter the party; that same night Lyssa **field-tests her servants by sieging their village church** (S1), and the party clocks the faction war unaided. Following the "— L." note and the retreat trail, they find her dig beneath the old quarter — a family crypt whose door opens only to **the blood of the line** (S2) — and finally descend to her sanctuary-lab (S3). Meanwhile Strahd already knows about his upstart relative and is happy to let outsiders destroy her for him.

> [!warning] ★ How it actually ended — three things the pitch got wrong
> **1.** The party was *supposed to* never realise the frame. **They were told, in writing, by Strahd himself** — ✂️-cut [[Ernst Larnak]] and the Farewell letter were both reinstated at the table on the night.
> **2.** The party was *supposed to* be released by the Mists. **They declined.** They are still in the valley.
> **3.** Session 3 was built as a likely **TPK** with the *Long Dark* epilogue. **All four survived** and the epilogue is unplayed.
> Full account: [[Session 3 — Actual Play]].

## Cast — final status

- [[Lyssa von Zarovich]] — **☠ destroyed, permanently.** Radiant damage, in her coffin, after a mist escape the party tracked down.
- [[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade]] — **☠ killed.** His **rune blade** and a **second signet ring** left the crypt with the party.
- [[Strahd von Zarovich (the unseen hand)]] — **never seen once in three sessions**, which is now a feature. Has personally written to the party, been refused, and left three conditions on the table.
- [[Ernst Larnak]] — **alive.** ✂️ Cut on 19 July, **reinstated at the table** to deliver the letter. Un-fought, knows their names, just failed to close a job. **The cheapest recurring villain available.**
- [[Urwin Martikov]], [[Sef Martikov]] and the **[[Keepers of the Feather]]** — alive, never thanked, **wereraven reveal still in pocket.**
- [[Father Donovich]], the [[The Braun Family|Brauns]], [[Madam Eva]] — alive, untouched by the finale.
- [[The Sisters — Alenka, Mirabel & Sorvina]], [[Arik the Barkeep]] — **never met.** The tavern was never entered. See [[Tavern — Blood o' the Vine (Backup)]].
