# Village of Barovia — Reference
*Canon detail from* Curse of Strahd *Ch. 3, trimmed to what this arc needs. **We use a slice of the village; the rest is name-drop and set-dressing.** The main CoS plot (Ireena, the burgomaster, escorting her to Vallaki) is **deliberately not opened** — this is a flying visit. Full map: [[Locations]] (*Map of barovia full.webp* + the village map).*

> [!quote] Approaching the village *(read-aloud)*
> Tall shapes loom out of the dense fog. The muddy ground gives way to slick, wet cobblestones, and the shapes become village dwellings — windows staring out of pools of blackness. No sound but a distant, mournful sobbing echoing through the streets. When the fog thins, **Castle Ravenloft** looms over it all, like a lance piercing the sky.

The saddest place in the land: the people are so terrified of Strahd they rarely leave their homes. Shops are shuttered and looted; claw marks scar the walls. **No walls, no defenses** — which is exactly why the church becomes the refuge in Session 2.

## What we actually use
| Place | In this arc |
|---|---|
| **Blood of the Vine tavern (E2)** | **Session 1.** Canonically owned by three sisters **Alenka, Mirabel & Sorvina** (*CoS spells it "Sorvia"; RTHW & we use Sorvina*) with barkeep **Arik Lorensk** — exactly our cast. RTHW corrupts them into the horrors. |

| **The cemetery / old churchyard** | **Session 3.** *Note:* keep our **"old churchyard"** (Lyssa's sanctuary) as a **separate, abandoned** chapel-and-graveyard at the village's edge, distinct from the active church's cemetery (E6), so it doesn't muddy the S2 refuge. |
| **Castle Ravenloft (the Pillarstone)** | The castle "always in view," whose window goes dark at the ending. |

## Name-drop only *(don't open these)*
- **Burgomaster's Mansion (E4) — Ireena & Ismark.** The heart of the *main* campaign (Strahd wants Ireena, who carries the soul of his lost Tatyana). **We don't touch it.** If players wander there: a barred, claw-scarred mansion, a grieving young woman who won't open the door, her brother Ismark wanting to flee to Vallaki. Gently steer them back to the tavern/church — *that's* their thread. A name-drop adds depth without derailing.
- **Death House (E7)** — the haunted house. Not part of this arc.

## Handy set-dressing
- **Bildrath's Mercantile (E1).** The one open shop, run by grasping **Bildrath** (and his simple, gladiator-strong nephew **Parriwimple**). Sells basic gear at **10× price**, never haggles — a profiteer in hell. Useful if the party wants to kit up before the finale. *"If you want it badly enough, you'll pay for it."*
- **Mad Mary's Townhouse (E3).** The source of the sobbing — a broken woman clutching a malformed doll, mourning her vanished daughter Gertruda. Pure atmosphere.
- **Occupants of an explored house (d20):** 1–3 empty · 4–8 swarms of rats (Strahd's servants, attack) · 9–16 frightened villagers (commoners + children; flee, never speak to strangers, clutch makeshift holy symbols) · 17–20 *2d4 Strahd zombies* (converge if disturbed).

---

> [!gmonly] ★ The Church is the Dawn's house ★
> Canonically the village church (E5) is a **church of the Morninglord (the Dawn)** — sunburst holy symbols everywhere, *Hymns to the Dawn* on the shelf. **This is perfect for our arc:** [[Urwin Martikov]] and the Keepers revere the Dawn, the **[[Ravenkind Shard]]** is a splinter of the Dawn, and the church the party defends in Session 2 *is* the Dawn's own house. Our priest is **[[Father Donovich]]**. Lean into the sanctuary-of-light theme — it makes defending the church mean something, and sets up the radiant relic beautifully.
>
> *(There is a deeper, tragic canon secret tied to this church and its priest. **It is deliberately left untouched here — saved intact for a possible future full* Curse of Strahd *campaign.** Don't introduce it in this three-shot.)*

## Special events *(free atmosphere)*
- **★ March of the Dead** *(every midnight — a superb omen).* An eerie green light fills the church cemetery, and a silent procession of the **ghosts of fallen adventurers** — warriors, archers, dwarves, bearded mages — rises and marches the Old Svalich Road toward Castle Ravenloft, trying and failing, again, to end Strahd. They can't be touched, turned, or spoken to. **Use it as a wordless dread beat** between scenes (see [[Dread & Omens]]). The implication for the party — *others came, and this is where they ended up* — needs no narration.
- **Dream Pastries** *(optional).* **Morgantha**, a night hag disguised as an old woman, wheels a cart through the fog selling addictive "dream pastries" (1 gp) — and collects payment, from one household, in the form of a child. A grim optional encounter if you want a side of horror; she avoids strangers and fights only in self-defense.
