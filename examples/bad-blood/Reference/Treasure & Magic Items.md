## Loot by session

### Session 1 — The Night the Church Held *(✅ played — see [[Session 1 — Actual Play]])*
- **The "— L." note** — found in the prototype's coat; in Jasper's notebook. Plot, not loot.
- **2 flasks of holy water** — Donovich to Jasper, post-siege.
- **Battlefield salvage:** the prototype's **mind-flayer remains** (a Keeper alchemist will pay 150 GP, or it's a grim trophy + a Bluetspur clue).
- **Keepers' favour** — holding their door earned deep loyalty; pays out as the Boost Kit at the end of S2 and a faction ally for any future Barovia play.

### Session 2 — The Tomb-Warden's Due *([[Session 2]] — rewritten 19 July 2026)*
- **The votive cache** *(Room I — the smashed false coffin)*: silver votive plates and grave-tokens the crew missed, **~120 GP** as a lot (more from a collector who doesn't ask questions).
- **The wight's arrows** *(Combat 1)*: **12 silvered-tip black-fletched arrows** — Jasper will want these.
- **The workshop crates** *(Room F)*: packed grave-goods and reliquaries — **~300 GP** of portable valuables (rings, coffin-plate silver, a jet mourning-brooch). **Carrying them makes you the Harbinger's quarry #2** — a feature, let them. The Keepers won't touch grave-goods.
- **★ Optional (recommended): the Gem of Brightness** — crated in Room F as a "sun-marked reliquary" Lyssa ordered shipped to her intact (*"the light in them dies slowly; do not test it"*). Moving it here (per [[Rewards & Leveling]]) gives the party the arc's magical-sunlight tool with the Shard cut. If used, replace the S3 coffin loot.
- **The Rune-Blade fragment** *(where the Harbinger fell)*: fist-sized, rune-etched, inert — keepsake/prop for the Scribe. If someone insists on the blade itself: it's a 250-lb slab of grave-steel; it never rings again, and carrying it flags them to every warden-spirit in Barovia. Let them, with that knowledge.
- **★ The Signet of the Quiet Dead** *(Room G, the emptied niche — DC 13 to spot)*: blackened-silver cadet-line signet; attunes **only for blood of the line** (Lucian). Knows the direction of the line's resting-places · Advantage on CHA checks vs Undead of the line · 1/LR *Command* (DC 15) vs such Undead CR ≤2. **Wearing it openly = declaring himself family** — Lyssa recognises it on sight in S3. Cold metal (75 GP) to anyone else.
- **★ The Chime of Last Rites** *(Room E, hidden altar cavity — DC 12)*: silver funeral chime, no attunement. 1/day: Undead CR ≤3 within 30 ft, DC 13 Wis or Disadvantage on attacks until end of its next turn. Lykta's natural tool.
- **★ The Embalmer's Lens** *(Room F, the strap table)*: crystal lens, no attunement. 1/day *Identify* · Advantage on Investigation/Medicine on corpses, wounds, necrotic residue. Her maker's-mark is on the rim — using it is borrowing her eye.
- **Scrolls** *(Room F crate)*: *Gentle Repose* + *Protection from Evil and Good*.
- **Holy water ×2** *(Room C — earned)*: restoring a font (holy water splash, blessing, or prayer + DC 13 Religion) wakes it for one minute; two flasks before it silts back.
- **The manifest** (Lyssa's hand) and **Milos's testimony** — plot, not loot.
- **The Under-Level Boost Kit** ([[Rewards & Leveling]]) — Urwin hands it over at the surface at the close: Keeper's Blessing, **2× Potion of Greater Healing** and **1× Potion of Heroism** per PC, and a **vial of holy water** each from [[Father Donovich]]'s store.

### Session 3 — Treacherous Blood *([[Session 3]])*
- **★ The purse in the coach** — **2,500 GP** beside the Farewell letter: payment, punctual and infuriating, for a contract nobody signed. *(Replaces Larnak's fee — he's cut.)*
- **Gem of Brightness** — in the Elder Evil's coffin **unless moved to the S2 crates** (recommended); if moved, the coffin holds a lesser radiant votive + ~200 GP instead.
- **Lyssa's workroom:** a **Wand of Magic Missiles** (3 charges left), 2 **spell scrolls** (*Vampiric Touch*, *Misty Step*), a locked strongbox (**~400 GP + a ruby worth 250 GP**), and her **research notes** (Bluetspur lore; sequel fuel).
- **Mist talisman to Bluetspur** (the crypt journal) and the **home talisman** with [[Letter — Strahd's Farewell]] — doorways, not gold.

> [!note] Cut with the tavern
> The old tavern loot — 500 GP each from Urwin, the **Quaal's Feather Tokens**, the tavern salvage, and the **[[Second Skin]]** Dark Gift — only enters play if the players surprise us and go in. It all lives in [[Tavern — Blood o' the Vine (Backup)]].

> [!note] What leaves Barovia with them
> Gold, the Gem of Brightness, the Rune-Blade fragment, and any **[[Second Skin]]** Dark Gift all come home. The Dark Powers' bargain doesn't dissolve at the border. *(The [[Ravenkind Shard]] stays with Donovich — never given.)*
