# Ravenkind Shard
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement — **waived: attunes on touch**, see below). The canonical item card — link here from anywhere the Shard comes up at the table.*

> [!warning] ✂️ CUT FROM THE ARC — 19 July 2026
> **The Shard is never handed over.** It was not given in S1 (the party fought the whole siege without it), and the S2 handover scene was cut in the 19 July rewrite. It stays **in Donovich's altar**, unused. The arc's radiant answers are now **Lykta and Lucian's spells**, plus (optionally) the **Gem of Brightness** moved early into the S2 workshop crates — see [[Rewards & Leveling]] and [[Session 2]].
>
> *Optional omen:* as the party passes the altar in S2's morning, the loose stone **hums** once, faintly, like a struck glass — and Donovich lays a hand on it without seeming to notice he's done it. Never explained. Sequel material — this card stays canonical if the Shard ever enters play.

> [!info] The item
> A finger-length splinter of pale, sun-warm stone, a raven-in-flight and a rayed sun worked into its face — a **shard of a true Holy Symbol of Ravenkind**. It hums faintly when undead are near. [[Father Donovich]] has kept it twenty years, behind a stone that was never mortared. [[Urwin Martikov]] knows it on sight: *"A splinter of the Dawn. The Count hates these. Keep it close."*
>
> **Attunement — waived.** Normally a short rest; here it **attunes on touch** for whoever Donovich gives it to (twenty years of a priest's prayer is the cover).

## Abilities

**🔆 Dawnlight (1/Day).** Bonus Action: for **1 minute** the shard sheds **Bright Light in a 20-ft radius and Dim Light for 20 ft beyond — and this light counts as *sunlight*.** Vampire spawn, the prototype, and every true vampire in the arc have **Sunlight Hypersensitivity: 20 Radiant at the start of each turn in the light, and Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.**

**⚔️ Radiant Rebuke.** When you hit an Undead with an attack, add **+1d6 Radiant** (no action). Uses per long rest = Proficiency Bonus (**3/rest** at the party's current level).

**🛡 Ward of the Dawn.** Advantage on saving throws against being **Frightened by Undead.**

## Running it in Session 1 (the siege)

| Wave | What the Shard does |
|---|---|
| **Wave 1** (ghouls, wolves, wight, specter) | Nothing decisive — flare-and-flinch only (read-aloud in [[Session 1]]). Radiant Rebuke still works on everything undead. |
| **Wave 2** (vampire spawn) | **First real payoff.** Dawnlight wrecks the spawn — 20 Radiant/turn + Disadvantage. Let it land. |
| **Wave 3** (the prototype) | Same hypersensitivity — devastating. It shrieks; the Keepers notice. |

> [!gmonly] The 1/day budget — decide before the table
> One Dawnlight, lasting 1 minute (10 rounds), for the whole siege. It **cannot cover Wave 2 and Wave 3** unless they run back-to-back inside 10 rounds. If the party burns it on the first spawn, the prototype comes in the dark — that's a real decision; let it stand. Emergency lever if the siege is collapsing: Donovich prays at the altar and re-kindles it **once** (*"The Morninglord is watching tonight."*). Once only.

> [!gmonly] Role in the arc *(historical — see the cut notice above)*
> It *was* the arc's **radiant answer-key**: the Harbinger rematch was designed around its Dawnlight, and radiant shuts off the Nosferatu's Regeneration in [[Session 3]]. Those jobs now belong to the party's own radiant and the **Gem of Brightness**. Its raven-and-sun mark still matters as *iconography* — the defaced chapel lintels (S2) and the corrupted sanctuary carvings (S3) are the same mark.
>
> **Provenance updated 4 July 2026:** the tavern is cut ([[Arc Plan — Four Sessions]]); the old source (the buried Temple of Ravenkind beneath the Blood o' the Vine) went with it. The Shard is now **Donovich's altar relic**. *Optional flavour:* Dawnlight recharges only at dawn — and Barovian dawn is grey and grudging, so it feels precious.
