# Rewards & Leveling

## Milestone leveling (this table's the plan)
**Level by milestone**, one level per session:

| Session | Played at | Level up… |
|---|---|---|
| [[Session 0.5]] *(played)* | **6** | **No level-up** — the on-ramp (Session 0 + the descent). |
| [[Session 1]] — The Night the Church Held | **6** | → **7** after the siege holds (dawn). |
| [[Session 2]] — The Tomb-Warden's Due | **7** | → **8** after the Harbinger falls (or is driven off) in the catacombs. **Urwin hands out the Boost Kit here**, before the descent. |
| [[Session 3]] — Treacherous Blood | **8 + Boost Kit** | Arc ends; level as you like for any sequel. |
| [[Session 4]] — Overflow | — | Contingency only — no milestone. |

> [!warning] Session 3 is run one level under
> *Treacherous Blood* is written for **Level 9**, but the party hits it at **Level 8.** That's a deliberate choice — the gap is small and the horror of being slightly outmatched suits Barovia. **Close it with boosts, not by leaving them to die** — see the **Under-Level Boost Kit** below, and the level-8 tuning in [[Encounters & Stat Blocks]].

**If you prefer XP:** the book grants **+1,400 XP each** for Session 1 and **+2,600 XP each** for Session 3 (plus normal combat XP). Award **~1,800–2,200 XP each** for the custom Session 2.

## Under-Level Boost Kit (for the Level-8 finale)
Hand out **two or three** of these before/early in Session 3 so a Level-8 party can survive a Level-9 lair. All are lore-friendly:

- **Keeper's Blessing.** Before the old churchyard, [[Urwin Martikov]]'s wereravens grant each PC a one-use boon: reroll one d20 *or* gain 2d10 temp HP when Bloodied. Refreshes once. (A warm, in-fiction "+1 level" of resilience.)
- **The Gem of Brightness, early** *(the Shard is cut — 19 July 2026: never handed over; it stays at Donovich's altar).* Move the Gem from the S3 coffin into the **S2 workshop crates** (Room F — a "sun-marked reliquary" Lyssa was about to ship out). Its beam blinds; its **5-charge Daylight burst counts as the arc's magical-sunlight answer** — vampire Sunlight Hypersensitivity, the Nosferatu's Regeneration switch-off, and the Harbinger's destruction clause. If you'd rather keep the S3 coffin loot, Urwin can gift it here instead. The party's own radiant (Lykta, Lucian) carries the rest.
- **Consumable cache.** 2× **Potion of Greater Healing** and 1× **Potion of Heroism** per PC *(plus the two Quaal's Feather Tokens — only if the tavern backup was run; see [[Tavern — Blood o' the Vine (Backup)]])*.
- **A Keeper at their back.** If **Sef survived the siege** (Session 1), she insists on coming — a wereraven ally (see [[Supporting & Minor NPCs]]; Wereraven / Scout statline) and effectively a 5th body without out-levelling the party. Keep her support-flavoured.
- **Heroic Inspiration, freely.** Start each PC with one and hand it out generously for good horror roleplay.
- **Dial the lair down** (if you'd rather not buff): run **1 Vampire Mind Flayer** instead of 2, make the **Elder Evil purely optional**, and let **Lyssa flee earlier**. See [[Encounters & Stat Blocks]].

> [!tip] Pick a lane
> Easiest at the table: **Keeper's Blessing + the Gem of Brightness (found in the S2 crates) + generous potions.** That alone lifts a Level-8 party to roughly Level-9 durability against undead without rewriting a single stat block. Don't stack *every* lever or the finale goes trivial.

## Loot & rewards by session

### Session 1 — The Night the Church Held *(played 13 July — see [[Session 1 — Actual Play]])*
- ~~The Ravenkind Shard~~ — **never handed over** (cut 19 July 2026). Donovich gave Jasper **2 flasks of holy water** instead.
- **Keepers of the Feather favour** — a faction ally for any future Barovia play; a Keeper roost and raven-borne tips. Holding their door earned deep loyalty.
- **Sef is alive and unbitten** — she was never in danger (the clock never ran); her bond with the party is fascination, not rescue-gratitude.
- **The clues** (the "— L." note on the prototype, the prisoner tattoos, the faction war, Strahd-controls-wolves, the Bluetspur/mind-flayer thread) — plot, not loot.
- **Battlefield salvage** — the prototype's mind-flayer remains (150 GP from a Keeper alchemist, or a grim trophy).

### Session 2 — The Tomb-Warden's Due *([[Session 2]] — rewritten 19 July 2026; Larnak is cut, so no contract gold)*
- **The crawl loot:** the **votive cache** in the smashed false coffin (Room I), the wight's **12 silvered-tip arrows** (Jasper), the workshop **crates** of grave-goods (Room F — carrying them makes you the Harbinger's quarry #2, which is a feature), and the **Rune-Blade fragment** where the Harbinger fell. Full list: [[Treasure & Magic Items]].
- **Optional: the Gem of Brightness** in the F crates — see the Boost Kit note above.
- **The Keepers' send-off** — **Urwin hands over the Under-Level Boost Kit at the surface, at the close** (Keeper's Blessing, potions, holy water) as the Keepers arm the party for what's below the sealed stair.
- **The manifest** (Lyssa's hand again) and **Milos's testimony** (her name) — plot, not loot.

### Session 3 — Treacherous Blood *([[Session 3]])*
- **★ The purse in the coach** — **2,500 GP waiting beside the Farewell letter**: payment, punctual and infuriating, for a contract they never signed. (Replaces Larnak's fee — the sting is that *someone* was always going to pay them for this.)
- **Gem of Brightness** — in the Elder Evil's coffin **unless you moved it to the S2 crates** (recommended — see the Boost Kit note; if moved, replace the coffin loot with a lesser radiant votive or gold).
- **Mist talisman to Bluetspur** — the dream-journal in the crypt; a doorway to the next horror, not gold.
- **★ Passage home** — the arc's true capstone "reward": with Lyssa dealt with, the **Mists open** and the party can leave Barovia (a home-keyed Mist talisman, a Vistani escort, or the Keepers' guidance). Lore-accurate, since nobody leaves without Strahd's permission — and he's choosing to give it. The sting: it's a *dismissal*, not a thank-you. Full staging in [[The Mists — Getting In & Out]] and Movement 6 of [[Session 3]].

> [!note] Cut with the tavern
> The old tavern rewards — 500 GP per PC, the Quaal's Feather Tokens, the **[[Second Skin]]** Dark Gift — only enter play if the players go into the Blood o' the Vine after all. See [[Tavern — Blood o' the Vine (Backup)]].

> [!note] What comes home with them
> Gold, the Gem of Brightness, the Rune-Blade fragment, and any **[[Second Skin]] Dark Gift** all leave Barovia with the party. Dark Powers' bargains don't dissolve at the border. *(The [[Ravenkind Shard]] stays with Donovich — it was never given.)*

## Dangling threads (for a continuing game)
- **Lyssa escaped** and swears revenge — a ready recurring villain. See [[Lyssa von Zarovich]].
- **The Bluetspur talisman** — "allies beyond the Mists" become the next arc.
- **Strahd used the party** to cull his rival — the Invitation, the Mists, and a purse of coin they never negotiated. Does the Count now know their faces? Reward and curse in one. See [[Strahd von Zarovich (the unseen hand)]]. *(Ernst Larnak was cut 19 July 2026 — his file survives for sequels.)*
