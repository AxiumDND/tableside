## The Village Church *(the Keepers' refuge — and the siege)*
**Session:** [[Session 1]]
A short walk from the tavern. The Keepers shelter the village's frightened people here, and [[Urwin Martikov]] uses it as his base — wereravens roosting in the bell-loft. The warmest, safest place left in the village, which is exactly why Lyssa attacks it. Map the **nave** (great doors, shuttered windows, overturned pews for barricades), the **bell-loft / gallery** above (overwatch), the **undercroft** below (where the Brauns shelter), and the **churchyard** outside — the battlefield of the S1 faction war, read by daylight in S2.

## The Old Churchyard — the old quarter *(Session 2)*
**Session:** [[Session 2]] *(rewritten 19 July 2026)*
The **original burial ground past the church's north gate** — where the village buried its dead before the church had stones. Leaning stones under black dripping yews, a roofless chantry hip-deep in brambles — and the one true building: a **black-veined marble mausoleum** ringed by a carved thorn-vine, with Lyssa's dig camp dead outside it (drained diggers, prisoner tattoos, an abandoned shaft). The way in is the **Blood Door**: a chalice basin and the instruction *"The tree drinks only of its own"* — it opens on **von Zarovich blood** (Lucian's). Theatre-of-mind above ground.

## Crypt Level 1 — the Crypt of the Cadet Line *(Session 2 — ✅ played 20 July)*
**Session:** [[Session 2]] · what actually happened: [[Session 2 — Actual Play]].
**★ The canonical map is now the table drawing:**

![[Crypt Level 1 — as played (Session 2).jpeg]]

As played: the **Hall of Chiselled Names** (A — sun rosette, swept paths that avoid it), the **Urn Room** (B — people-ash, names removed), the **Wells** (C — one font rekindled with holy water), the **Warden's Work** (the bisected dead — Speak with Dead: *"sent by the master of these lands"*), the **Workshop** (F — strap tables, brine jars, the Lens, the scrolls), the **Broken Span** (H — planks over the whispering dark), the **False Crypts** (Combat 1 — one Fireball), and the **★ Rotunda** (K — the toppled statue of the Warden, and the **dais-ELEVATOR**: pressure-plate mechanism, surfaces behind the tree line — it's how the siege-ending forces rose). Off the rotunda: the guard-statue chamber, and the **freshly dug hole** dropping ~70 ft into the **lower cavern** — stair-remnants, and the **very large door with the voices behind it** (the way to the sanctuary, [[Session 3]]). *Position at cut: the party at that door, the Warden descending the dais.*
