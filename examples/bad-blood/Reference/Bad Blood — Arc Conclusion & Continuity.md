# Bad Blood — Arc Conclusion & Continuity
*The arc is finished. **The party is not.** This is the one page to read before any of these PCs or NPCs appear in another game. What ended, what survived, what is still owed. Play record: [[Session 3 — Actual Play]] · live-state history: [[Tracker — Bad Blood]] · hub: [[Bad Blood — Overview]].*

> [!abstract] How it ended
> **Session 3, 27 July 2026. All four PCs survived.** They killed the **Tomb-Warden** at the great door, walked into **Lyssa von Zarovich's** sanctuary, named her, argued with her, and then killed her — twice, once as a body and once as ash in a hidden coffin, with radiant damage, because they worked out how. In the middle of it **Lykta opened the amber sarcophagus with her own blood** and something ancient came out, destroyed the remaining mind flayers, and **poured itself into her.** Afterwards **Ernst Larnak** came down the shaft with **Strahd's letter**, revealed the whole frame in the Count's own words, and opened the road home.
>
> **★★ AND THEY DIDN'T TAKE IT.** Lykta wanted the hole the mind flayers ate in her filled before she'd leave the valley; the others were still owed gold; nobody walked into the Mists. **The arc ends with four living PCs still inside Barovia, having refused the Devil of Barovia's one and only courtesy to his messenger's face.**
>
> **The planned TPK ending — [[Session 3|the Long Dark]] — never fired and is entirely unspent.**

## 2. ★★ The refusal — and what it should cost

This is the arc's real ending and its real cliffhanger. Everything else in this document is downstream of it.

**What happened:** Ernst delivered the letter, the letter named three conditions and opened the road, and the DM asked the party directly whether they took it. **Lykta said no first** — the mind flayers had permanently reduced her hit point maximum, she wanted to know if anything could give it back (*"I feel like I've lost all of my income"*), and the answer was *"not in the moment, but there'll be time"* / ***"give it time."*** The others noted the gold was still outstanding. Nobody stepped into the fog. The scene ended there.

> [!gmonly] Why "the road simply waits" is the wrong answer now
> The run guide's planned response to a refusal was gentle — *"the road simply waits, patient as its owner."* **Don't run it that way.**
>
> Strahd's letter was a **settlement**, not an invitation. A service rendered, an account closed, three terms, and a door — offered by a creature who does not do this. Refusing it doesn't pause the transaction; it **rejects the terms**, publicly, in his valley, in front of his man, by four people who have just demonstrated they can permanently destroy a von Zarovich.
>
> Read his own tone back: *magnificent · exactly as useful as I had hoped · you have proven interesting.* That is the tone of **a man putting a tool back in the drawer.** A tool that declines to go back in the drawer is not a tool. It is a **loose end** — and loose ends are precisely what [[Ernst Larnak|his people]] are on record as hating.
>
> He was patient *because they were leaving.* They are not leaving.
>
> **Suggested consequence — the door closes, and the courtesy with it.** No second letter, no coach, no more polite prose. The Mists that "know their scent now" stop reading as an exit and start reading as a fence. The wolves stop conspicuously sparing anyone. And the next thing that comes for them does not introduce itself.
>
> Keep the escalation cold rather than loud. Strahd doesn't rage; he **stops being interested in their comfort.** The horror is that nothing dramatic happens for a while — and then the road east isn't there any more.

**The three conditions are now live in a different way.** They were the price of leaving. The party didn't leave, so the party never accepted them — but they'll be held to them anyway:

1. *"Do not return. The Mists know your scent now — and they are not always mine."* → They never went, so this can't be broken. It can, however, be **revoked**.
2. *"Do not speak my name where the fog can hear it. It carries."* → **Immediately breakable, in a valley made of fog.** This is the fastest fuse in the arc.
3. *"Do not mistake an open door for a friend."* → They didn't. They mistook it for something they could keep in their pocket and use later. That is arguably worse, and he will read it that way.

## 3. The party as they stand

> [!note] Party state at the close
> ✅ **Level 8** (awarded post-session), un-boosted — the Boost Kit never reached them. No long rest taken after the Trap. **Still underground in Barovia**, in the wrecked sanctuary, with the way to the surface being the dug shaft or the dais-elevator. No surface scene has happened; nobody up top knows the war is over.

### Lykta Endrino (Nat) — ★ the carrier, and the reason they stayed
The one who changed, and the one whose need is now the party's plot engine.

- **Took the worst of the night**: repeatedly grappled and drained by vampire mind flayers — roughly **58 points of damage taken**, at least **one level of exhaustion**, and a **badly reduced hit point maximum** from Drink Sapience. **This is why the party is still in Barovia.** She refused to walk into the Mists diminished.
- **★ She opened the amber sarcophagus with her own blood and accepted what came out.** The DM's ruling at the table: *"she's so far gone she wouldn't know how to say no."* She glowed green, dropped unconscious, and woke up.
- **She has no idea what she is carrying. Neither does the player. Neither, currently, does the DM.** The single most valuable unspent thing to come out of the arc.
- **★ Note the collision:** the thing she wants (restoration) and the thing she now hosts (a vestige of Amber Temple stock) are the *same kind of transaction*. The valley's only sources of that power charge for it. She has already said yes to one of them without reading the terms.
- Prior colour to keep: hears the dead; the whispers on the broken span in S2 (*"come down to us"*); she is a **dhampir**.

### Lucian Radu (Joe) — ★ the blood of the line
- **Kin to the von Zarovich line, confirmed on screen twice**: the blood door in S2 (*"welcome home, blood of the line"*) and Lyssa's own recognition (*"you're of my line, aren't you?" — "as am I."*). Prep canon: **indirect kin, not her son**; his mother's branch is the erased one.
- Killed Lyssa's body personally (Misty Step + Eldritch Blast → ash).
- Wears the **Signet** of the old branch.
- With Lyssa dead, **he may be the last of that bloodline anyone knows of** — and he is **still standing on the family's land**, in the family's tomb, which he opened with the family's blood. He didn't leave. In Barovian terms he has not so much declined an exit as **taken possession.**

### Jasper Alderwick (David) — the hunter who called it
- Held the line all arc; opened the fight with Lyssa because he refused to trade one tyrant for another. **128 damage into her**, including the nat 20.
- Carries the **monster notebook** — which now contains a sketch of a thing nobody can identify, the Warden, vampire mind flayers, and a working method for killing a vampire in its resting place.
- Only PC without darkvision. Carries the **Longbow of Warning** and the **Embalmer's Lens**.

### Dallas Hinterfield (Glen) — the artillery
- Fireballs won two fights this arc. He was the one who flushed Lyssa out of the shadows.
- **Barovia corrupted his magic** — his Mage Hand came out skeletal in S2 and stayed that way. **Nobody has ever explained this to him**, and he is still in Barovia for it to get worse.
- The prep's seeded "gift" (magic burning clean and faintly amber near the seal) **was never played** — still available as a slow reveal.

## 4. NPC ledger — who to bring back, and how

> [!gmonly]
> **Dead and staying dead:** Lyssa von Zarovich (radiant, in her coffin), the Tomb-Warden / Harbinger of the Rune-Blade, four vampire mind flayers, everything else in the sanctuary.

### ★ Strahd von Zarovich — a correspondent, still never seen, and now refused
The biggest change to the campaign's status quo. He wrote to them, in his own voice, and **they turned down what he offered.**

He does not need to appear to punish that. The tools are all already on the board: the Mists, the wolves, the roads, the weather, and a valley in which every single door belongs to him. **The right escalation is withdrawal, not violence** — he stops making things easy, and the party discovers how much of their survival so far was permission.

He also **never answered [[PC — Lykta Endrino|Lykta]]'s letter** from S2. He wrote to the whole party instead — and got a no. Between those two facts, the correspondence is now the most interesting relationship in the campaign.

**What he knows:** their names, their faces, what each of them is good for, that one of them is kin, and that one of them is carrying something out of his valley's oldest prison that he did not authorise.

### ★ Ernst Larnak — alive, un-fought, and the messenger who was told no
Cut from the arc on 19 July, **reinstated at the table for the ending.** The party met him exactly once: smiling, dry, unhurried, delivering the letter and the way out. **They never fought him, never got a straight answer, and never learned his master's business from his mouth — and then they declined his errand in front of him.**

He is a **coward in a fight and over-confident that he's the smartest man in the room**, and he has now failed to close a job. That makes him useful twice over: as the man who returns with a colder message, and as a man with a personal problem. Sheet: [[Ernst Larnak]].

### The village of Barovia — still up there, still unresolved, still reachable
The party never went back to the surface. **Nobody has been told the war is over.**

- **[[Sef Martikov]]** — alive, owed a beat all arc, never got one. She is **still available**; the prep's fifty-years-later Sef (age 65, the Amber Temple bargain) is unspent, and its *shape* — a Barovian who goes to the mountain and makes a bargain about these four — is now much more plausible, because they're still here to bargain about.
- **[[Urwin Martikov]]** and the **Keepers of the Feather** — never thanked, never told. **The wereraven reveal is still in pocket.**
- **[[Father Donovich]]** — last seen exhausted and alive, told to save his strength.
- **The Braun family**, **[[Arik the Barkeep]]**, **[[Alenka]] / [[Mirabel]] / [[Sorvina]]**, **[[Madam Eva]]** — all untouched by the finale. **Madam Eva is the obvious next stop** for a party that wants something restored.
- ✂️ **Milos** and the manifest — never appeared. ✂️ **The Ravenkind Shard handover** — never happened. ✂️ **The tavern** — never entered, and the table was *actively curious* about it (*"Blood of the **Vine**… vines that drink blood?"*).

## 5. Live threads — ranked by how much they're worth

1. **★★ They refused the door and they're still in the valley.** The campaign's next scene writes itself: they climb out of a hole into a Barovia that is no longer being made easy for them. See §2.
2. **★ Lykta's shredded hit point maximum** — the stated reason they stayed, and therefore the party's immediate quest. Mind-flayer sapience damage isn't a long-rest problem. In Barovia the answers are Madam Eva, the Abbey, the Amber Temple, or a bargain. **All of them cost.**
3. **★ What came out of the amber, and what it did to Lykta.** Older than the crypt, spoke *"we are saved"* and *"I'm so glad I saved you,"* killed her enemies unasked, and went into her rather than into the mountains. Unpaid for, unnamed, unexplained. Prep's answer was a vestige of Amber Temple stock — keep it or replace it; the table has been told nothing.
4. **★ Lucian, last of the old branch, standing on the old branch's land.** Lyssa dead, his mother's line run, the Signet on his hand, and a claim he has physically declined to walk away from.
5. **Strahd's three conditions** — especially *do not speak my name where the fog can hear it.* Fastest fuse available.
6. **The gold that was promised and never paid.** A debt owed by Strahd, to people who refused his settlement. Collecting it is a *terrible* idea and a great session.
7. **The second signet ring**, unattuned and unidentified, off the Warden. What does the *champion's* ring do that the *family's* doesn't?
8. **The rune blade.** Prep canon: the Warden's soul lived in the runes; radiant released him. **Or did it, and is he in the sword Jasper is carrying?**
9. **Dallas's skeletal Mage Hand.** Barovia's mark, never explained, and he's still standing in Barovia.
10. **The crypt itself** — desecrated, open, a wrecked laboratory, a broken ancient prison, and a hole in the ground behind the treeline near the mausoleum that anything can now climb out of. They didn't seal it. Nobody did.
