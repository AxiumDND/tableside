## ★★ The cliffhanger — the refusal

**Strahd's letter was a settlement**: a service rendered, an account closed, three conditions, and a door. **The party turned it down in front of his messenger.** The run guide's planned answer to a refusal (*"the road simply waits, patient as its owner"*) is too gentle now — full reasoning and a suggested escalation in [[Bad Blood — Arc Conclusion & Continuity]] §2. Short version: **the door closes and the courtesy stops.** No second letter, no coach; the Mists become a fence rather than an exit; the wolves stop conspicuously sparing anyone; nothing dramatic happens for a while, and then the road east isn't there.

The three conditions, still live: *Do not return — the Mists know your scent now, and they are not always mine* · ***Do not speak my name where the fog can hear it, it carries*** *(fastest fuse in a valley made of fog)* · *Do not mistake an open door for a friend* — which they didn't; they mistook it for something they could pocket and use later, and he will read that as worse.
