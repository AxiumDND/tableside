# Ambience & Soundtrack — Bad Blood
*Mood cues per scene. I can't link specific tracks, but these describe what to search for (Tabletop Audio, Michael Ghelfi, Myinstants for stings, or your own library). Aim for **looping ambience under everything** and a **one-off sting** at each shock.*

> [!tip] Three-layer rule
> Run **one ambient loop** (sets the room), drop a **sting** at reveals/jump-scares, and swap to **combat music** only for the real fights. Silence is a tool too — kill the audio dead the instant a horror appears, then let the sting hit.

## Session 0.5 — Into the Mist *([[Session 0.5]] — played)*
- **The Last Camp (open):** warm crackling campfire + crickets → cut to **dead silence**, then a low rising drone as the Mist takes them. *(Search: "campfire night ambience"; "rising drone".)*
- **The Harbinger:** kill everything — just stone-on-stone scrape and a dragging blade; heavy dread combat only if they stand and fight. *(Search: "dread drone", "metal drag".)*
- **Urwin's rescue:** ravens screaming, then one warm chord under the dawn-light. *(Search: "crows/ravens screaming", "warm choral swell".)*

## Session 1 — The Night the Church Held *([[Session 1]])*
- **The church (the Roost):** warm hearth, low murmur of frightened villagers, rain on the roof → **ravens screaming** sting as the alarm hits. *(Search: "church interior rain", "crows/ravens screaming".)*
- **The siege:** driving combat music + rain + distant howls; ring a bell or drum for each new wave. *(Search: "siege battle music", "heavy rain ambience".)*
- **Sef's rescue clock (Wave 2):** drop the music to a **ticking/heartbeat** under the action for the 3 rounds. *(Search: "heartbeat tension", "ticking clock".)*
- **Lyssa's cameo:** cut everything to rain-only the moment she's revealed; a single cold piano/strings motif for her voice. *(Search: "cold solo piano", "villain reveal sting".)*
- **The morning after:** grey wind, dripping, distant crows — let the silence stretch through the graveyard search. **A single off-key note for Larnak.** *(Search: "graveyard wind", "single piano note".)*

## Session 2 — The Tomb-Warden's Due *([[Session 2]])*
- **The old churchyard:** wind, dripping yews, distant crows; a cold sting at the shattered grave. *(Search: "graveyard wind", "stone crack sting".)*
- **The upper catacombs:** stone-crypt drone, bone-dry acoustics, occasional settling dust. *(Search: "crypt ambience", "catacombs drone".)*
- **The Harbinger rematch (the rotunda):** the S0.5 scrape-and-drag returns first — let them recognise it — then heavy percussion combat; gutter the music each time radiant lands. *(Search: "boss battle dark", "metal drag".)*
- **The broken seal:** silence, then a low green hum from below. End the session on it. *(Search: "subterranean hum", "ominous drone".)*

## Session 3 — Treacherous Blood *([[Session 3]])*
- **The descent:** the hum grows; green-candle crypt drone. *(Search: "ritual chamber ambience".)*
- **The Trap:** Lyssa's cold motif → hard psychic-horror combat for the mind flayers; a discordant sting on each **Mind Burst.** *(Search: "psychic horror", "mind flayer ambience".)*
- **The Elder Evil:** silence → a single deep impact as the coffin opens → frantic combat.
- **The door home:** the drone finally resolves to something almost peaceful — then a single cold note as they read **[[Letter — Strahd's Farewell]]** and the castle window goes dark. *(Search: "bittersweet ambient", "ominous music box".)*

## Tavern backup — Blood o' the Vine *(cut — only if run; [[Tavern — Blood o' the Vine (Backup)]])*
- **The tavern:** dusty silence, a dripping tap, a single squeaking sound (Arik's rag). *(Search: "abandoned tavern ambience", "haunted house drone".)*
- **Alenka stalks:** near-silence with occasional metal-on-wood scrapes; sting on her first strike. *(Search: "slasher stalk", "knife scrape sting".)*
- **The temple:** deep cavern drone + faint wet chanting; heavy ritual-horror combat music for Sorvina. *(Search: "eldritch ritual ambience", "boss battle dark".)*

## Recurring motifs *(reuse to build dread)*
- **Strahd / the castle:** one cold, slow piano or music-box theme. Play *only* when his hand is felt. By Session 3 the players should recognise it.
- **The Mists:** a rising, breathy drone with faint wings. Use at both bookends.
- **The Harbinger:** stone-on-stone and a dragging blade — S0.5 at the gate, S1 dawn (circling), S2 rotunda. Same sound every time; let them learn to dread it.
