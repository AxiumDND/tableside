# Encounters & Stat Blocks

Every creature across the arc (**four-session plan** — [[Arc Plan — Four Sessions]]), with the **real CRs from *Ravenloft: The Horrors Within* Ch. 5** (the player supplied the actual stat blocks — full blocks at the bottom of this note). Common creatures cite the Monster Manual / SRD. The cut tavern's encounters are kept at the bottom as backup.

> [!danger] Reality check — these CRs are HIGH
> The RTHW horror stat blocks hit **well above** what "Adventure for Level 6 Characters" suggests. **Run them as written and a 4-PC party can absolutely die** — that's the genre, but go in with eyes open and the tuning levers below ready. The key mercy is that each session's fights are **sequential, with room to rest between them**, and several bosses are *solo* monsters (poor action economy against four PCs).

## Session 1 — The Night the Church Held (party Level 6 → 7 — the siege) *([[Session 1]])*
Three escalating waves, **offset by 2 Keeper wereraven allies** who fight beside the party (+ the hidden reveal as an emergency tide-turner). Scale by adding/removing **ghouls**, not by touching the spawn, wight, or prototype. Run with **breaths between waves** (reposition, not a rest).

| Wave | Creatures | ~Budget (5× L6) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — The Doors** | 4 **Ghoul** (CR 1) + 2 **Dire Wolf** (CR 1) + 1 **Wight archer** (CR 3, stays outside) + 1 **Specter** (CR 1, through the wall) | Hard | Ghoul **paralysis** punishes clumping; the wight and specter exist to *reach the snipers* — drop either if Wave 1 runs long. |
| **2 — The Walls** | 2 **Vampire Spawn** (CR 5) + 2 **Ghoul** | Hard | They **Spider Climb** to where the ranged PCs are. **One spawn is draining Sef** in the churchyard — the 3-round rescue clock starts here. |
| **3 — The Prototype** | 1 **Vampire Mind Flayer** (CR 5) + 1 **Vampire Spawn** + remaining ghouls | **Deadly** | **Mind Burst** (Int DC 15, Incapacitated, extends *vertically*) is the spike. The party's first taste of what Lyssa builds. |

> [!warning] Running the siege at L6
> Planned for L7 originally — the party hits it at **L6, but five strong**: Jasper (David) joins in the cold open and closes the frontline gap, and the Keeper allies offset the rest. Levers: **drop a ghoul or two per wave** for a softer table; drop the **wight or specter** from Wave 1; cut Wave 2 to **one** Vampire Spawn; the **wereraven reveal** stabilises a collapsing wave. **Don't** stack the prototype and both spawn in one wave — Mind Burst + two CR 5 spawn would be a TPK. The **[[Ravenkind Shard]]'s Dawnlight is 1/day** — it covers Wave 2 *or* Wave 3, not both.
>
> **Stat blocks:** Vampire Mind Flayer below in *Full stat blocks*; Wight archer is in [[Session 1]]; Vampire Spawn, Ghoul, Dire Wolf, Specter are MM/SRD.

If dropped *without* radiant, it becomes an S3 surface complication — watching, never a second boss. Full staging: [[Session 2]] Movement 7 + [[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade]].

## Session 3 — Treacherous Blood (party Level 8; lair built for 9)

| Creature | Count | CR | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Vampire Spawn** | 2 | 5 | MM/SRD | AC 16, HP 90, Spider Climb (no regen). Entry guardians. |
| **Vampire Mind Flayer** | 2 *(→1 for L8)* | **5** | RTHW Ch. 5 | AC 15, **HP 85**. **Mind Burst** (30-ft, Int save, Incapacitated) + grapple→**Drink Sapience** (6d6 psychic). |
| **Lyssa — Vampire Nightbringer** | 1 | **8** | RTHW Ch. 5 | AC 16, **HP 142**, fly/hover. **Flees when bloodied (~71 HP).** |
| **Vampire Nosferatu** (Elder Evil) | 1 | **8** | RTHW Ch. 5 | AC 16, HP 85, Regen 10, Blood Frenzy, **Blood Spew** 6d8 cone. Optional. |
| **Swarm of Infesting Insects** | 1 | **2** | RTHW Ch. 5 | AC 13, HP 33; Poison + nasty 1-hour after-effects. |
| ~~Ernst Larnak~~ | — | — | — | ✂️ Cut 19 July 2026 — never appears. |

> [!warning] Session 3 difficulty — the trap is the spike
> **The Trap = Lyssa (CR 8) + Vampire Mind Flayers.** Two mind flayers + Lyssa is ~XP 7,500 raw — a **Deadly+** wall for 4× L8. The Mind Flayers' **Mind Burst** can Incapacitate the whole clumped party at once; that's the lethal combo. **Hard rule for a Level-8 party: run ONE mind flayer unless you handed out the boost kit**, and have **Lyssa flee at ~71 HP** (she's a coward by design). The two Vampire Spawn guardians are a separate, earlier fight — don't merge them into the Trap.
>
> **Radiant is the answer key — but know the rule:** Barovian *daylight isn't sunlight* ([[Setting Rules — Barovia]]), so you can't hurt these vampires by fighting outdoors by day. **Magical sunlight does:** with the Shard cut, that means the **Gem of Brightness's 5-charge Daylight burst** (rule it as sunlight for this arc — it's the answer-key now; ideally found in the S2 crates) triggers each vampire's **Sunlight Hypersensitivity** (radiant every turn they start in it, + Disadvantage). Plain **radiant** (Lykta's spells, Lucian's Starry Wisp) always hurts undead and shuts off the **Nosferatu's** Regeneration — it just isn't 'sunlight.' Lean on all of it.
>
> **Elder Evil (Nosferatu CR 8 + Swarm CR 2)** is a *second boss after the Trap* — **optional, skippable, and the smart party walks away** at L8. Only run it if they're fresh and boosted.

| **Arik** | 1 | — | — | Non-combatant; collapses if touched. |

> [!warning] Tavern difficulty — read this (if run)
> As written this is **three Hard-to-Deadly fights**: a CR 8 stalker, a CR 6 + two CR 1, and a CR 8 + CR 2 finale. It is *survivable* because:
> - The bosses are **solo** (Alenka, Sorvina) — the party out-actions them.
> - Fights are **spaced**; bank a **short rest** in the cleared Office before the basement.
> - **Light/radiant and ranged** trivialise some threats (Sorvina's aura only bites in melee; Alenka recoils from sudden light).
>
> **If you want it less lethal, pick a couple:** cut Alenka's necrotic rider to 3d6; drop Sorvina's Terrifying Aura to *Frightened only* (no Paralyze); remove one Ghoul; give the party the **first short rest free**; or run Alenka purely as a stalker the party can flee/bottleneck rather than a stand-up fight. **Do NOT run all three back-to-back with no rest.**
