# Setting Rules — Barovia
*The hard rules of the demiplane, from* Curse of Strahd*. Keep these in your back pocket — players test the edges, and Barovia's edges are the whole point. Where a rule touches **Bad Blood**, it's flagged.*

---

## Lay of the land
Rolling thunderclouds, deathly stillness, dark woods patrolled by Strahd's wolves and servitors. The **Svalich Woods** climb the mountains that wall the valley — **Mount Baratok** (snow-capped) and its twin **Mount Ghakis**, with **Lake Zarovich** between them. **Vallaki** (palisaded town) sits on the lake's south side; **Krezk** (walled village, Abbey of Saint Markovia) lies west; the ruins of **Argynvostholt** (the fallen Order of the Silver Dragon) lie between them. **East of the mountains** lies the **village of Barovia** — *shrouded in mist, with no walls or defenses* — overlooked by **Castle Ravenloft**, perched on the 1,000-ft **Pillarstone of Ravenloft.**

> [!gmonly] For this arc
> Our whole three-shot is the **village of Barovia** and its surrounds. The village is **undefended and wall-less** — which is exactly why the **village church** becomes the people's only refuge (and Lyssa's target) in Session 2. Vallaki/Krezk are a day+ away; we never go there. Full regional map: [[Locations]] (*Map of barovia full.jpg*).

## The Mists *(why "just leave" never works)*
A deadly fog rings the valley and engulfs any creature trying to leave — **flying creatures included.**
- **Start your turn in the fog:** **DC 20 Con save** or gain a level of **Exhaustion** — and it **can't be removed while in the fog.**
- **No matter the direction or distance,** you get turned around and end up **back in Barovia.**
- The fog is **heavily obscured.**

> [!gmonly] For this arc
> Reinforces everything: nobody walks out. The party leaves only when **something lets them** — Strahd's permission via the home talisman, the Keepers' Mist-craft, or a keyed Mist talisman. See [[The Mists — Getting In & Out]]. *(The Bluetspur talisman in Session 3 is keyed to Bluetspur, not home — a doorway sideways, not out.)*

## Sunlight in Barovia *(the radiant rule — important!)*
By the Dark Powers' will, **the sun never fully shines here.** Barovian daylight is **bright light, but NOT sunlight** for the purpose of vampire vulnerabilities. **However — Strahd and his spawn ARE subject to sunlight *created by magic*,** and they stay indoors by day and hunt by night regardless.

> [!gmonly] ★ For this arc — the answer key ★
> Every vampire in **Bad Blood** has **Sunlight Hypersensitivity** (radiant when it *starts its turn in sunlight*). You **cannot** trigger it by fighting outdoors in the daytime — Barovian "day" isn't sunlight. You trigger it with **magical sunlight**: the **[[Ravenkind Shard]]'s Dawnlight** (*explicitly counts as sunlight* — that's the whole point of the relic), the **Daylight** spell, or similar. Plain **radiant damage** (a paladin's smite, the Gem of Brightness, a cleric's spells) still hurts undead normally and is always welcome — it just isn't "sunlight." Make this clear so players don't waste a round "waiting for dawn." See [[Encounters & Stat Blocks]].

## Alterations to magic
Barovia is its own **demiplane**, sealed from every other plane.
- **No escape magic works.** *Teleport, Plane Shift, Astral Projection, Wish* (cast to leave), banishment effects, and plane-hopping magic items **all simply fail.** The Border Ethereal is the only exception — and a creature leaving it is **pulled back into Barovia.**
- **Summoning** from other planes works normally; **extradimensional spaces** (Rope Trick, Magnificent Mansion) work, but anything cast *inside* them obeys the same restrictions.
- **Divine/patron spellcasting continues** — clerics, paladins, warlocks keep their magic (good for party casters, and for [[Father Donovich]]).
- **★ Strahd can intercept "contact" magic.** When someone casts a spell that contacts other planes (*Sending, Commune, Contact Other Plane, Augury* aimed beyond the Mists), **Strahd can choose to be the recipient** — *he* is who they reach.

> [!gmonly] ★ For this arc — a gift of a horror beat ★
> If a player tries to **Sending** for rescue, or **Commune** with their god about escaping, let **Strahd answer.** A warm, courteous, impossible voice replying to a desperate prayer is unforgettable — and it ties straight into the twist ([[Strahd von Zarovich (the unseen hand)]]). Also: any plan to *teleport home* dies on arrival — gently remind them, in-fiction, that the Mists eat such magic.

## Resurrection Madness *(death has teeth here)*
The souls of the dead are **trapped in the Mists** and cannot reach the afterlife.
- **Revivify** (cast within its time limit) still works — but the creature **screams on waking**, as from a nightmare. No madness (they were dead < 24 hrs).
- A humanoid **dead 24+ hours** that is returned to life (*Raise Dead*, etc.) gains a **random indefinite madness** (roll on the DMG *Indefinite Madness* table) — the soul's horror at realising it's trapped in Barovia, likely forever.

> [!gmonly] For this arc
> This is why the deadly fights *matter.* A quick Revivify saves a fallen PC (with a scream); raising someone left dead overnight brings them back *broken.* The grimmest implication — that a soul lost here may be trapped — is a powerful reason to **get out alive.** (This replaces any homebrew resurrection ruling; see [[Going Off-Script & Failure States]].)

## Cosmetic spell modifications *(free atmosphere)*
Reskin spells to deepen the dread (mechanics unchanged):
- **Find Familiar / Find Steed:** the creature is **undead** (immune to Turn Undead), not celestial/fey/fiend.
- **Mage Hand / Bigby's Hand:** **skeletal.**
- **Spirit Guardians:** ghostly **skeletal warriors.**
- **Phantom Steed:** a **skeletal horse.** **Find the Path:** a silent **child's spirit** guides you.
- **Alarm:** you hear a **scream**, not a ping. **Gust of Wind:** a **ghastly moan.** **Fog Cloud:** misty **claws** form. **Wall of Stone:** **tortured faces** in the rock.
- **Rary's Telepathic Bond:** the linked feel **something vile eavesdropping.** **Revivify:** the **scream** (above).
