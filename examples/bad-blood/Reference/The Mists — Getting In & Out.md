# The Mists — Getting In & Out

How the party reaches Barovia for this **flying visit** and how they leave again afterward — kept faithful to *Curse of Strahd* and *Ravenloft: The Horrors Within*, and wired into this arc's "Strahd used you" twist.

---

## The lore (what's true about the Mists)
- **The Mists belong to the Dark Powers, not to Strahd.** They wall Barovia off as a prison-demiplane. Even **Strahd himself cannot leave.**
- **Nobody enters or leaves Barovia without Strahd's permission.** The land is sealed. The **Vistani** are the famous exception — Strahd granted them free passage centuries ago, so they can guide others through.
- **Trying to walk out the border** doesn't work. *(Canon mechanics — [[Setting Rules — Barovia]]:* start your turn in the fog = **DC 20 Con save or a level of Exhaustion** that can't be removed while inside it; you're **turned around** and always end up back in Barovia, even flying; the fog is **heavily obscured**.*)* You don't leave by *walking* — you leave when **something lets you.**
- **No magic shortcut, either.** *Teleport, Plane Shift, Wish, Astral Projection* and the like **fail** if cast to leave (it's a sealed demiplane). And if the party tries **Sending/Commune** to call for rescue, **Strahd can make himself the one they reach.**
- **Mist talismans** are the clean tool: a *non-magical* object keyed to one specific domain. Carry it into the Mists, fix your mind on that domain, and the Mists carry you there. A Darklord can still **close their borders** against a talisman. The [[Mist Walker]] Dark Gift works the same way without an object.
- **The standard "win" exit** (kill Strahd, dissolve the curse) is **off the table for a 3-session visit** — see [[Strahd von Zarovich (the unseen hand)]] for why we keep him off-screen.

> [!abstract] The framing in one line
> The party is **pulled in by the Mists** at the start, and **released by the Mists** at the end — and both the snare and the door are, secretly, **Strahd's doing.** He needed capable outsiders to deal with his rival [[Lyssa von Zarovich]], and outsiders who can't leave make perfect, deniable pawns.

## Getting IN *(the cold open — played in [[Session 0.5]])*

### ★ Recommended — The Last Camp (Strahd's snare, overnight)
**Open in the party's own world, around a campfire on the road, the night before everything changes.** This doubles as the **character-introduction beat** — the party already know each other, so go round the fire and let each player remind the table who they are and draw out their bonds. Then, **overnight, the Mists rise and take them in their sleep.** They wake in Barovia and the fog *draws back*, stranding them — road gone, horses gone. Somewhere on one PC is a small object they don't remember acquiring — a **raven's feather**, or a **stoppered vial of dark red wine** (a literal *blood of the vine*): the spent **Mist talisman** used as bait.

This explains everything downstream: the Keepers ([[Urwin Martikov]]) **watch the Mist-borders for new arrivals**, which is how Urwin finds the party and offers them work almost immediately. The bait also quietly plants the truth — *someone wanted these specific outsiders here* — which pays off as the Strahd reveal. Full staging (intros, the watch, waking) is in **[[Session 0.5]]** — this is how it actually played on 29 June.

> [!readaloud] Read-aloud — waking in Barovia
> You wake to grey light and the smell of a woodsmoke that isn't yours. The fire is long dead. The fog is already thinning — drawing back between the trees like a tide going out — and as it clears, it takes your road with it. Black pines lean over a valley that was not here last night, under a sky the colour of a bruise. The horses are gone. Somewhere ahead, a raven calls once — and then, oddly, *waits.*

### Alternative entries (pick to taste)
- **The Vistani invitation.** A Vistani camp on the road welcomes the party to a feast, and by morning the wagons — and the party — have crossed into Barovia. (The Vistani can come and go; the PCs can't follow them back out.) Optionally include a brief **Madam Eva Tarokka card** pull as an ominous cold open. See the Vistani guide in [[Supporting & Minor NPCs]].
- **The werewolf trail.** The party tracks a fleeing beast into a fogbank and out the other side into Barovia — never noticing the border until it's behind them.
- **Already there.** Skip the in-fiction entry: they're travellers who got Mist-snatched weeks ago and have been surviving in the village. Start at Urwin's hook.

## Getting OUT (denouement of [[Session 3]])

The party did Strahd's dirty work — his rival is dead or routed. Now the Mists open. Choose the exit that hits hardest for your table. (The staging lives in the closing movement of [[Session 3]] — the coach, the letter, the castle window going dark.)

### ★ Recommended — A Debt Repaid (the cold gift)
After the old churchyard, a way home appears **unbidden**: a Vistani escort waiting at the treeline, or a sealed letter, or [[Ernst Larnak]] one last time — delivering a **Mist talisman keyed to the party's home** "with the Count's compliments." The horror is the message underneath: *Strahd is letting them go because they were useful.* They were never prisoners fighting to escape; they were **tools, and now they're being put away.** They'll never be quite sure the leash is gone.

> [!readaloud] Read-aloud — the door home
> It's waiting at the edge of the woods where the Mist still curls: a single black coach, no driver, the door already open. On the seat, two things — a folded square of cloth wrapped around something small and familiar, and **a sealed letter in black wax, pressed with the same crest as the one that started all this.** *(Hand the players [[Letter — Strahd's Farewell]].)* The Mist beyond the coach is thinning, and through it you can see — impossibly — your own road home.

### Alternative exits
- **The Keepers smuggle them out.** [[Urwin Martikov]]'s wereravens know Mist-paths of their own. As thanks for cutting the head off the digs, the Keepers guide the party to a border the Mist will honour. Warmer, less sinister — good if you want the heroes to *earn* it.
- **A recovered talisman.** Repurpose the **Bluetspur Mist talisman** found in Lyssa's crypt (Session 3) — or rule that the spent home-talisman from the cold open *reawakens* once Lyssa's interfering magic is broken. Player-agency flavour.
- **They stay (sequel hook).** If the table loved it, the Mists *don't* open — the door home is the campaign's next goal. (Drops the "flying visit" framing; only do this if everyone wants more Barovia.)

---

## Keeping it a *flying visit* (3 sessions, ~3 hrs each)
- **Strahd never becomes the quest.** He is weather and dread, not a boss — see [[Strahd von Zarovich (the unseen hand)]].
- **Don't open the Ireena/Tarokka/Fanes plot.** Those are the 200-page campaign. Name-drop at most.
- **The party's throughline is simple:** *get a foothold → follow the thread → finish the job → go home.* The Mists bookend it; everything between is the three published-plus-bridge sessions.
