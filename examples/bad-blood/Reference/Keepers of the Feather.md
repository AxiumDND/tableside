# Keepers of the Feather
*Faction · the wereravens who watch over Barovia*

A secret network of **wereravens** (and a few trusted humans) who quietly resist Count Strahd — not with swords, but with **information, shelter, and memory.** They are servants of the **Dawn (the Morninglord)**: the raven-and-sunburst is their sign, the same symbol carved in the buried Temple of Ravenkind and worked into the [[Ravenkind Shard]]. They watch the roads and the Mist-borders, keep the small lights burning, and pray for the day someone can finally end the Count. Led in the village of Barovia by [[Urwin Martikov]].

> [!gmonly] Faith as a hook
> Urwin's veneration of the Dawn is what makes the party's arrival land as an **answered prayer** (Session 1) — his earnest faith is the emotional engine of the opening hook, and the cruel counterpoint to the truth that **Strahd** sent them. See [[Urwin Martikov]].

> [!quote] Their creed
> *"We do not fight the devil in the open. We outlive him. Trust the feathers. Fear the smiles."*

## In this arc
- **S1:** [[Urwin Martikov]] (in human guise) hires the party to investigate the Blood of the Vine.
- **S2:** the reveal — wereravens all — and the night **Lyssa sieges their refuge** (the village church). The party fight beside them; [[Sef Martikov]] may fall.
- **S3:** the Keepers arm the party for the old churchyard (the [[Rewards & Leveling|Under-Level Boost Kit]]) and can smuggle them out of Barovia (an alternate ending — see [[The Mists — Getting In & Out]]).

## Running them
- **Allies, not a crutch.** In the siege, run 2–3 wereravens (stat: [[Bestiary|Wereraven]]) simply — each downs a ghoul or Helps a PC. They soak action economy so four players aren't overwhelmed, without stealing the spotlight.
- **Tone:** warm, weary, decent — a rare honest faction in a poisoned land. Their kindness is what makes Sef's danger hurt.
- **Members named in this arc:** [[Urwin Martikov]], [[Sef Martikov]].
