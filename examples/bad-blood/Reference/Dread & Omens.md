# Dread & Omens — running Bad Blood as horror
*Optional, light-touch tools to make the table **feel** the dread, not just roll for it. Use as much or as little as you like — even just the omens table earns its keep.*

---

## 1 — The Dread Track *(table-wide tension meter)*
A single shared counter, **Dread 0–6**, that you (the GM) raise as the horror mounts and *spend* to make Barovia bite. It is **not** a punishment meter — it's a pacing tool that lets the players feel the pressure climbing.

**Raise Dread by 1 when:**
- A creature dies in front of the party (ally, villager, or a foe that dies *wrong*).
- A PC fails a save against Frightened, or rolls a natural 1 on a fear-adjacent check.
- The party opens something they were warned not to (a coffin, a sealed door, a journal).
- A boss is revealed, or a horror first shows its true shape.
- The party rests in an unsafe place, or splits up in the dark.

**Lower Dread by 1 when:** the party scores a hard-won victory, reaches true safety (the Keepers' church, a completed long rest), or a player leans all the way into a brave/heartfelt moment. *Dread resets to 0 at the start of each session* (it should *build* within a night).

**Spend Dread (GM, any time):**
| Cost | Effect |
|---|---|
| **1** | Trigger an **omen** (roll/choose below) — free atmosphere, no mechanics. |
| **2** | A creature acts with sudden viciousness: re-roll its damage and take the higher, *or* it gets a free Hide/reposition. |
| **3** | The Mists stir: an exit closes, a light gutters out, or one PC must make a **DC 13 Wis save** or be **Frightened** until end of next turn. |
| **5** | **A Pall of Dread** — until end of the scene, the party has Disadvantage on the *first* save each makes against fear, and enemy crits land on a 19–20. (Use once a session, at the worst moment.) |

> [!tip] Keep it invisible
> Track Dread behind the screen. Players should *feel* the night getting worse, not watch a number. The point is mood, not bookkeeping.

## 2 — Character Fears *(from the Domains of Dread "fear reaction")*
At session zero (it's in [[Player Pitch — Bad Blood]]), each player named **one thing that scares their character.** Write them down. Then:
- When the game brushes a PC's fear, call for a **DC 12 Wisdom save**; on a fail, they're **Frightened (of that thing)** until the end of their next turn — *and lower Dread by 1*, because the horror "landed."
- A PC who **overcomes** their fear in a clutch moment (pushes through, saves a friend) earns **Heroic Inspiration.** Courage isn't the absence of fear — reward the overcoming.
- **Never** weaponise a *player's* real-world fear. Character fears only. (See the PG-13 tone note in the pitch.)

## 3 — Omens of Barovia *(d20 — drop between scenes, on Dread, or whenever the room goes quiet)*
*Pure atmosphere. No mechanics unless you want them. The recurring motifs — ravens, the wine, the watching, the castle — quietly reinforce the arc's threads.*

| d20 | Omen |
|---|---|
| 1 | Every raven in sight turns its head to follow you at once, then looks away together. |
| 2 | Your wine — your waterskin — has gone the colour of old blood. It tastes fine. That's worse. |
| 3 | For three steps, your shadow lags a half-second behind you. |
| 4 | A child laughs, close by. There is no child. |
| 5 | The fog at the treeline has a shape in it that is almost a person, patiently waiting. |
| 6 | A single black feather drifts down from a clear, dead sky and lands at a PC's feet. |
| 7 | Up on the mountain, a window in the distant castle is lit. You didn't notice it before. |
| 8 | The candles all lean toward one PC, as if breathed on. |
| 9 | A dog howls, cuts off mid-note, and does not start again. |
| 10 | Someone says your name — your *real* name, the one you didn't give anyone here. |
| 11 | The reflection in a puddle shows the sky a heartbeat after the real one changes. |
| 12 | Flowers by the path are blooming. In Barovia. In the cold. Pointing the way you're going. |
| 13 | A villager won't meet your eye, touches two fingers to their lips, and hurries on. |
| 14 | The temperature drops, just around one PC, just for a moment, like a door opened behind the world. |
| 15 | You find footprints in the mud that match your boots exactly, going the other way. |
| 16 | The church bell tolls once, far off. It is not the hour, and the church has no bell-ringer. |
| 17 | Something large moves through the mist alongside you, keeping pace, never closing, never leaving. |
| 18 | A raven lands near the party, drops a small bright object (a coin? a tooth? a ring?), and waits. |
| 19 | You all dream the same dream that night: a grey city under a green sky. *(Bluetspur — strongest near the old churchyard.)* |
| 20 | For one second, every NPC in view stops — mid-word, mid-step — and looks directly at the party. Then they carry on as if nothing happened. |

---

> [!tip] The March of the Dead
> Every midnight, the village cemetery looses a silent green procession of dead adventurers marching to Castle Ravenloft (full text: [[Village of Barovia — Reference]]). It's a **perfect wordless omen** — drop it between scenes in any session. *Others came. This is where they ended up.*

## 4 — Per-session dread beats *(updated 19 July 2026 to the three-session reality)*
- **Session 0.5 (into the Mist — played):** the Harbinger ([[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade]]) **attacks** here — its first fight, a scary one it walked away from. Afterward, omens **5** (*the waiting shape in the fog*) and **17** (*something keeping pace, never closing*) *are* it, stalking the party; it doesn't fully fixate until **the blood door opens in S2.**
- **Session 1 (the siege — played):** omens of *being watched* and *the castle* (1, 5, 7, 17) before the attack. The faction war did the dread work — the players clocked it themselves.
- **Session 2 (the battlefield & the crypt):** above ground, the dragging blade circling the old quarter (don't comment). At the blood door, each failed offering = **one bell toll** below — spend Dread on every toll. In the crawl, the Warden's bisected dig crew and the groove in the floor are the dread engine; keep the grinding always one corridor away. **The rematch lands at max Dread** — it steps over its own toppled statue.
- **Session 3 (the descent):** omens of *Bluetspur / dreams* and *the cold* (14, 19, 20). Save the **Pall of Dread** (Dread 5) for the Mind Burst moment in the Trap. The door home is the one place Dread should finally *fall* — relief, then the letter snatches it back.
