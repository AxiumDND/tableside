# GM Cheat Sheet — Bad Blood
*One page to run from. Everything else is a click away. Remapped to the four-session plan, 4 July 2026; **refreshed 26 July to the as-played state** (S1–S2 played; S3 = the 21–26 Jul rewrite). Master plan: [[Arc Plan — Four Sessions]] (historical). Live state: [[Tracker — Bad Blood]].*

## The arc in one breath
Outsiders are pulled into Barovia by the Mists (Strahd's snare). They survive the Harbinger and reach the church (**S0.5**), hold it through a night of siege — Lyssa's *field test* (**S1**), crawl the catacombs, open the blood door on Lucian, and end on a cliffhanger at the great door with the Warden descending (**S2 as played**), then fight the Warden, meet [[Lyssa von Zarovich]] at last — **who names herself AND reveals they were Strahd's invited pest control** — and break her engine at full lethality, win, lose, or TPK (**S3**). Then: the Mists open in payment (they leave *knowing*) — or **the Long Dark** (fifty years, shallow graves, old Sef). **The tavern is cut** (backup: [[Tavern — Blood o' the Vine (Backup)]]). A **Session 0.5 on-ramp** then **3 sessions (+ S4 as a retreat/overrun valve), ~3 hrs.** Levels **6 → 7 → 8 — but as played the Boost Kit never reached them and L8 waits on the Warden: the S3 gap is deliberate.** All in and under the **village of Barovia.**

## The twist timeline *(don't explain — let them feel it)*
0. **S0.5:** a spent **Mist talisman** appears on a PC (Strahd's bait). 1. **S1** *(played)*: the **"— L." note** on the prototype names the old churchyard; unseen wolves kill *her* dead in the graveyard (the faction war — the players clocked it unaided); prisoner tattoos; *Strahd controls wolves* (Urwin). 2. **S2** *(played 20 Jul)*: the **blood door** opens on Lucian's blood (*"welcome home, blood of the line"* — shared openly); the gravestone rubbing reads **an older branch of Zarovich**; Speak with Dead makes the Warden's victims *the other faction* ("sent by the master of these lands"); the **"L"** rides the Lens rim and scroll labels *(Milos + manifest cut — Lyssa still unnamed)*; **Lykta writes to Strahd** — no reply; the dais-elevator; cliffhanger at the great door. 3. **S3** *(rewritten 21–26 Jul)*: the Warden at the door → the Choir's green passage → **★ Lyssa finally seen: she names herself AND reveals the frame — Strahd invited them to remove her** (true; the Choir confirms it) → the Trap + the Binding + the Unchaining at **full lethality** → the Long Dark, or the Mists open in silent payment. **No coach, no letter — Strahd himself never appears.** *(Larnak cut 19 July 2026.)*

> **The spine in one line:** Lyssa *causes* the horrors; Strahd *frames* the party to cull her; the open door is a leash — ★ and at the Trap, **she tells them so.** Full map: [[The Throughline — Lyssa & the Hidden War]].

## Session 0.5 — Into the Mist (L6, no level-up) *(played 29 June)*
**Session 0** (bonds, the party's quest, fears) → cold open → camp → **Mists rise** → wake in Barovia → **the Harbinger attacks** (scary, unwinnable; **Urwin's Dawn drives it off** — the rescue) → **Urwin's hook** → the party chose the **church**, not the tavern. Ended in the bell tower, the dead massing at the cemetery edge, **David's PC spotted running through the Mist.** *Ran with 3; Jasper joins in the S1 cold open.* Only radiant touches the **[[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade|Harbinger]]**.

## Session 1 — The Night the Church Held (L6 → 7) *(✅ played 13 July — [[Session 1 — Actual Play]])*
**What happened:** Jasper tracked the party in solo (and hid from Sef at the gates). **Two waves ran** (Wave 2 cut): the doors fight (ghouls, wolves, wight archer, specter) and the **prototype** (killed — the "— L." note found in its coat). The players clocked the **faction war unaided**; prisoner tattoos + Strahd-controls-wolves landed. Sef arrived safe *after* the siege. **No Shard, no Larnak, no Lyssa.** Long rest; ended at morning; **Level 7 banked.**

## Session 2 — The Tomb-Warden's Due (L7) *(✅ played 20 July — cliffhanger cut — [[Session 2 — Actual Play]])*
**What happened:** Lykta's **letter to Strahd** (retconned to siege night — unanswered); the battlefield + Jasper's **gravestone rubbing** (an older branch of Zarovich); the **★ blood door opened on Lucian** — *"welcome home, blood of the line,"* shared with the table at once; the crawl (erasure/de-consecration proven, holy-water font, Chime, Lens, Signet, scrolls, brine jars); **Speak with Dead** made Room D's dead the *other* faction; **Combat 1** ended in ~2 rounds (the Fireball); the rotunda's **dais is an elevator** — and the session cut with the party in the **lower cavern at the great door, the Warden descending.** **No Level 8, no Boost Kit** — *"go kill one Harbinger first."*

## Session 3 — Treacherous Blood (L7, un-boosted — deliberate; FULL LETHALITY) *([[Session 3]] — rewritten 21–26 July)*
**Beats:** **cold open in initiative** — the Warden at the door (quarry: Lucian; radiant = the win; the button is live; it won't cross the threshold) → the green passage (spawn sentinels usher; the voices are **the Choir Below**, and they talk to Lykta) → **★ the Trap: Lyssa names herself + REVEALS THE FRAME** (Strahd invited them — true, Choir-confirmed) → her offer refused → **2 Vampire Mind Flayers + Lyssa, no safety nets**, with **the Binding** as the objective layer (2 anchor-stones + the sarcophagus-seal; Chime/holy water/radiant; Lucian's blood + Signet = master key) → **the Unchaining** (3 rounds; Lyssa killable; the works die on-screen) → **the Long Dark** (protected 25 min) or **the Mists open** — payment, no letter, no coach, and they *know*.
**Watch:** TPK is the likely, planned-for ending — **kill with a straight face**; retreat to the surface is always legitimate (→ [[Session 4]]). Protect the epilogue over everything. Spotlight ledger: Lucian's walk · Lykta's ear · **Dallas's volatile vats** · **Jasper's Warded Line + Hunter's Eye**.

## Session 4 — Overflow *([[Session 4]])*
Runs **only** if the party retreats to the surface mid-S3 (re-planned assault) or the night runs long. The Long Dark epilogue is never deferred — it plays the night they fall.

## Standing rulings
- ~~Near-TPK safety net~~ — **OFF for S3 (the lethality contract, 21 Jul):** by the Trap the party's usefulness is spent; the Mists do not swallow the fight and nobody is saved by the plan. The one mercy that stands: **retreat to the surface is always allowed.** See [[Session 3]] / [[Going Off-Script & Failure States]].
- **Radiant answer-key:** the party's own spells — Lykta (Guiding Bolt, Starry Wisp, Moonbeam) + Lucian (Starry Wisp) — plus holy water ×3 and silvered arrows. The **Gem of Brightness** is loot in Lyssa's sanctuary (win branch — its 5-charge **Daylight burst counts as magical sunlight**). *(The [[Ravenkind Shard]] is cut — never handed over.)*
- **Dread:** keep a hidden 0–6 Dread track; spend it for omens and the Pall of Dread. See [[Dread & Omens]].
- **Sunlight rule:** Barovian *daylight isn't sunlight* — only **magic** (a Daylight effect, the Gem's burst) triggers vampire Sunlight Hypersensitivity. Plain radiant still hurts undead. [[Setting Rules — Barovia]].
- **No escape magic:** Teleport/Plane Shift/Wish to leave **fail**; if they Sending/Commune for help, **Strahd can answer.** Death 24h+ then raised = **Resurrection Madness.**
- ~~Rescue clock (S1 Wave 2)~~ — never ran; **Sef is alive, unbitten, and fascinated by the party.** Don't spend her; S3 colour and sequel material.
- **Lyssa always flees** when meaningfully hurt — **with one exception (25 Jul): during the Unchaining the Choir holds her in the room and her mist-escape FAILS.** If the party earns her death, she dies on screen.
- **Strahd never fights and never appears** — climate, not boss. ★ **Lyssa does his talking now (26 Jul):** she reveals his frame at the Trap; the opening Mists are his only signature. *(The Farewell letter and coach are cut.)*
- **Radiant** vs every vampire (Sunlight Hypersensitivity); shuts off the Nosferatu's Regeneration.
- **Mind Burst** (mind flayers): 30-ft Emanation (it extends *vertically*), Int DC 15 or Incapacitated — punish clumping; teach spacing with the S1 prototype.
- **The Harbinger:** first fight S0.5 (unwinnable — Urwin's rescue); **the real fight opens S3 in the lower cavern at the great door** (S2 ended mid-descent) — quarry: **Lucian** (the blood that opened the door) — **only radiant ends it** (else Curse-Bound Rejuvenation re-forms it in 24 hrs); it will not cross the door's threshold. See [[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade]].
