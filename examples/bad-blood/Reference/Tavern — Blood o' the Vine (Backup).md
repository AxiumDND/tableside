## ⚡ GM Toolkit — quick links

*Open these before you start. Pull the RTHW stat blocks first — three of the four bosses are new.*

### Combat reference
- **Alenka — [Relentless Slasher]** · *RTHW Ch. 5 — **CR 8, AC 15, HP 91**, Legendary Resistance + 3 legendary actions.* Ground-floor stalker; hunts by sound; **2× knife = 2d4+4 +6d6 necrotic each (~60/round)**. Plays like a horror-movie killer — appears, strikes (Vanishing Strike teleports her 30 ft), vanishes. **Don't run her as a stand-up brawler.**
- **Mirabel — [Ghast Gravecaller]** · *RTHW Ch. 5 — **CR 6, AC 16, HP 97**.* Second floor; commands **2× [Ghoul]**. Claw **Paralyzes** non-undead; ranged Horrific Necrosis Frightens; Stench (DC 13 Con) Poisons adjacent.
- **[Ghoul]** x2 · *MM/SRD — CR 1, AC 12, HP 22, Speed 30.* Bite + Claws; claws **Paralyze** (DC 10 Con; **not elves**) for 1 min, save ends. *(Verify against your book.)*
- **Sorvina — [Unspeakable Horror]** · *RTHW Ch. 5 — **CR 8, AC 13, HP 126**.* **Attacks against her have Disadvantage** (Incomprehensible Form) and she **regens 10/turn**. **Terrifying Aura (15 ft): Wis DC 15 or Frightened *and* Paralyzed.** Raises the skeleton on her first turn, then fights alongside it.
- **[Minotaur Skeleton]** · *MM/SRD — CR 2, AC 12, HP 45, **Vulnerable to Bludgeoning**, Speed 40.* Gore + Greataxe; **Charge** (move 10ft straight at a target, gore for +2d8 piercing). Bones **remain intact** when destroyed. *(Verify.)*
- **[[Arik the Barkeep]]** · No stats. Animate corpse; **falls to pieces if attacked.** Atmosphere only.

> [!gmonly] Encounter pacing & difficulty
> **Heads-up: these are the real RTHW numbers and they're brutal for L6** — a CR 8 stalker, a CR 6 + two CR 1, and a CR 8 + CR 2 finale. Survivable because the bosses are *solo* and the fights are **spaced** — but **do NOT run them back-to-back with no rest.** Bank a **short rest** in the cleared Office (Movement 1) before the basement. The boss room is a **two-target spike** (Sorvina **and** the Minotaur Skeleton). Full difficulty breakdown and softening levers in [[Encounters & Stat Blocks]].

### Rules at the table
- **Hunting by sound (Alenka).** She tracks the noisiest PC. Stealth and *not* shouting matters — reward players who stay quiet.
- **Paralysis (Ghouls).** The scary part of Movement 2. Remind martials this is why you don't all clump up.
- **The Dark Gift (Movement 3).** Touching the skeleton offers [[Second Skin]] — see that note and [[Domains of Dread — Index]]. Only one PC; not if they already have a Dark Gift.

## Movement 1 — The Tavern *(ground floor)*

**Map:** *Map 2.2: Blood of the Vine Tavern* (see [[Locations]]). A vertical dungeon — taproom and office here, bedrooms above, a hollow below.

![[map-03-02.blood-of-the-vine-tavern-player.jpg]]

> [!readaloud]
> The Blood of the Vine sits dark at the end of the village lane, shutters drawn, a wooden sign creaking on one hinge — a bunch of grapes, the paint flaked to the colour of dried blood. The door is unlocked. It gives with a groan and a breath of stale, sweet rot.

### The Taproom — Arik
> [!readaloud]
> Dust lies thick on every table, undisturbed but for one set of footprints behind the bar. There, a grey-skinned man in a stained apron polishes a shard of broken glass — over, and over, and over — though the cup it came from is long gone. He does not look up. The rag squeaks against the glass.

![[Scene - The Taproom.png]]

This is **[[Arik the Barkeep]]**. He is **not a fight.**

> [!gmonly]
> Arik repeats one motion forever. He won't respond to speech — or only with a single hollow line: *"…we're closed."* **If attacked or grabbed, he immediately collapses into a heap of bones and apron** — no combat, no XP, just a horrible little moment. A DC 12 Medicine/Religion check confirms he's been dead for weeks and shouldn't be moving at all.

### Alenka stalks
> [!gmonly] How to run Alenka
> **Alenka ([Relentless Slasher]) hunts by sound.** Don't put her on the board yet. She stalks — a sound here, a shadow there, a knife embedded in a table that wasn't there a moment ago. She targets the **noisiest** PC (anyone who shouted, smashed a door, cast a loud spell). She strikes from concealment, then breaks line of sight and repeats. Run two or three "stalk beats" before the real exchange so the dread builds. **Trigger the fight** when the party makes a lot of noise, corners her, or reaches the kitchen (her lair). Then bring the lights up and let it be brutal and fast.

> [!readaloud]
> The sound stops. For one breath, the tavern is utterly silent — and then she's *there*, where a moment ago there was only a coat on a hook: one of the sisters, or what's left of her, grinning around a mouthful of too-many teeth, a butcher's cleaver in each ruined hand.

| Party behaviour | Alenka's response |
|---|---|
| Loud / smashing things | She fixates on the loudest PC; ambushes them first. |
| Quiet, methodical search | She holds longer; the party may find the **kitchen lair** (gnawed bones, a nest of knives) first — DC 13 Perception to spot her coiled in the rafters. |
| Light + fire | She recoils from sudden bright light one round (advantage on attacks that round) — primal, animal aversion. |

### The Office *(NE corner)*
> [!readaloud]
> A cramped office, every surface buried in clutter — tally-slates, unpaid accounts, a ledger gone to mould. But one letter sits apart from the rest, kept: fine cream paper, an elegant hand, no signature but a single pressed line at the foot — *"— the lady."* It is addressed to Sorvina. It speaks, warmly, of *"the old tomb that surely lies in the hollow beneath your floor — and the fortune the forgotten dead have no further use for."* It encourages her, gently, to dig.

> [!gmonly] The Courier's Letter (the first fingerprint — arc seed)
> This is the arc's **first real clue**, and the party won't understand it yet — that's the point. Someone *put the idea in Sorvina's head.* A courier brought this letter; an elegant, unsigned hand styling itself only **"the lady"** flattered a greedy woman into excavating something that should have stayed buried. **Don't explain it.** Let it land as: *this wasn't bad luck — somebody wanted this dug up.* Full prop text in [[Handouts & Props]].
>
> **The payoff is the "— L." orders from [[Session 1]]:** the same fine hand. If the party already has the orders (they will, if this runs after S1), the connection lands *immediately* — the unseen patron of the tavern gets a name: [[Lyssa von Zarovich]]. The whole chain is mapped in [[The Throughline — Lyssa & the Hidden War]].
>
> **A trapdoor** under a moth-eaten rug (DC 12 Investigation, or automatic on a floor search) descends to the hollow beneath. **Good short-rest spot** before they go down.

## Movement 2 — Upstairs

> [!readaloud]
> The stairs complain under your weight. The upper floor is close and hot and smells of rotten meat. Broken dishes crunch underfoot. From the far room comes a wet, rhythmic sound — something being *gnawed.*

### Mirabel & her ghouls
> [!gmonly] ★ Read this before play ★
> **Mirabel ([Ghast Gravecaller])** stalks this floor with **two [Ghoul] servants.** She's not subtle — a predator holding court. Open with the ghouls flanking from side rooms while Mirabel commands from the gnawing-room doorway. **Paralysis is the threat:** ghoul claws **Paralyze on a failed DC 10 Con save (not elves)** — a paralysed PC is a free crit magnet. Punish clumping.
>
> **Mirabel can be *talked to* — briefly.** A flicker of the woman she was surfaces if a PC says her name or mentions her sisters (DC 15 Persuasion/Insight). It won't stop the fight, but it can buy a round, or reveal *"Sorvina did this — Sorvina, downstairs, with the bones."* These are victims, not villains.

> [!readaloud]
> She's hunched over what used to be a person, and when she lifts her head the lower half of her face is dark and glistening. She was beautiful once; you can still see it, under everything. "Ohhh," she breathes, almost delighted. "Guests. We're *closed*, you know. But I'm sure we can find something for you." From the rooms on either side, two more shapes unfold from the dark.

### The Private Dining Room *(SE corner)* — the journal
> [!readaloud]
> Broken dishes, rotten food gone to grey fur, and pages — dozens of them — torn from a book and scattered like leaves. The handwriting is a woman's, growing wilder down the page. It's a journal. Mirabel's.

> [!gmonly] Read or hand these out as props
> **First entry —** *"A fine lady's courier came to the door, asking for Sorvina. Since then she won't stop talking about a tomb full of treasure in the hollow beneath us — she's sure of it, sure as scripture. She's started to dig. Alenka thinks she's mad. Maybe she is."*
> **Second entry —** *"Her digging has uncovered standing stones. I'm frightened for her. She's started whispering to herself, obsessing over them. She doesn't sleep."*
> **Third entry —** *"She found bones at the centre of the stones. The bones of some monster. We all dream now, and wake starving. Sorvina says the hunger is a blessing. Food has lost its taste — but the patrons… the patrons smell delicious."*
>
> This tells the party what waits below, that **Sorvina is the source** — and, quietly, that **someone outside steered her to it** (the courier, the "fine lady"). It corroborates the office letter without naming her. Don't underline it; let a sharp player notice.

## Movement 3 — Beneath the Tavern *(climax)*

**Map:** *Map 2.2: Beneath the Tavern* (see [[Locations]]).

![[map-03-03.beneath-the-tavern-player.jpg]]

> [!readaloud]
> The ladder ends in cold, raw earth. This is no cellar. Standing stones lean out of the dirt walls, half-unburied — tall menhirs carved with ravens in flight wheeling around a rayed sun. The carvings are old beyond reckoning, and they were made by someone who loved whatever this place once was.
>
> At the centre of the circle, hunched over a heap of black bones — antlered, tusked, *wrong* — kneels the last of the sisters. She is singing to it. A low, wet, gravel-voiced song, and the bones are twitching in time.

![[Scene - Temple of Ravenkind.png]]

### Sorvina & the Skeleton
> [!gmonly] ★ Read this before play ★
> This is **Sorvina ([Unspeakable Horror])**, the worst of the three. When she notices intruders she **howls and raises the black bones as a [Minotaur Skeleton]** — then **both fight to the death.** Your two-target spike: Sorvina (caster/horror) plus a CR 2 bruiser that **Charges** (move 10 ft straight, gore +2d8).
>
> **Tactics:** the Skeleton screens Sorvina and charges the squishiest target; Sorvina uses the menhirs as cover and works the party's fears. No negotiating — she's too far gone. For a gut-punch, let *one* human word slip mid-fight (*"…it's so much quieter now"*) before the monster reasserts. **When the Skeleton drops, its bones stay intact** — that matters next.

> [!readaloud]
> The song stops. Her head turns too far, and what's left of Sorvina's face splits into something that was never a smile. She throws back her head and *howls* — and the black bones at the heart of the circle lurch upright, antlers scraping the earthen ceiling, a thing of horns and tusks and hate dragging itself together to stand between you and her.

### The Cursed Remains — the Dark Gift moment
> [!gmonly] How this works
> The black skeleton is the imprisoned **spirit of the Svalich Woods**, sealed here when this was a true Temple of Ravenkind. Two ways to end it:
> - **Remove Curse** on the skeleton **destroys it cleanly.**
> - **A character touches it** (animate or inert) and the spirit *whispers*, pleading with them to **end its ancient curse.** Accepting **destroys the skeleton AND grants that PC the [[Second Skin]] Dark Gift.** A PC who already has a Dark Gift can't accept.
>
> Play the whisper as **seductive, not threatening** — relief, an old voice grateful to rest. If a PC takes [[Second Skin]], **roll on its Change Catalyst table together now** — that catalyst becomes a recurring horror beat in Sessions 2–3.

> [!readaloud]
> Where your skin meets the black bone, the cold goes straight to the back of your skull — and a voice that isn't sound speaks inside it. *Old. So tired.* "…you can end it," it breathes. "What they did to me. What they made me. Take it from me — carry it out of this cursed ground — and let me, at last, be **nothing.**"

> [!gmonly] A splinter of the Dawn (treasure)
> Among the menhirs — a DC 13 Investigation, or simply hand it to a PC who searches the stones — lies the **[[Ravenkind Shard]]**: a fragment of a true Holy Symbol of Ravenkind that survived the corruption. It's the arc's **radiant answer-key** against the vampires of Sessions 2–3 (its *Dawnlight* counts as sunlight), and the diegetic core of the [[Rewards & Leveling|Under-Level Boost Kit]]. Urwin will name it for them on their return. Full write-up: [[Treasure & Magic Items]].

> [!gmonly] ★ Taking it wakes the Harbinger *(recurring nemesis trigger)* ★
> The instant a PC lifts the Shard from the stones (or destroys the antlered spirit), they have *disturbed the silence of the tomb* — and far off in the Mist, the runes on a great blade **scream once.** From now, **[[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade]] hunts the Shard-bearer.** It was glimpsed as a silent shape in [[Session 0.5]]; now it has its quarry. Give a glimpse on the road back (omen **#17**, [[Dread & Omens]]); the real fight lands in the [[Session 2]] catacombs rotunda. *(In the current arc the Shard comes from Donovich instead, and the Harbinger already hunts the bearer — see [[Ravenkind Shard]]. Skip this trigger if it's already in play or already dead.)* *(If the party somehow leaves the Shard behind, the Harbinger instead fixes on whoever struck the spirit down — someone disturbed the tomb either way.)*

## Movement 4 — Return to Urwin

> [!gmonly]
> Urwin **pays the 500 GP each** as promised. If the party **destroyed the skeleton**, he also hands over **two random Quaal's Feather Tokens.** Then he tells them what this really was — the hook for Session 2. (He still doesn't show the feathers yet; that's the Roost reveal next session.)
>
> **Throughline note:** this is where the arc's spine first surfaces *for the players* — not a haunting, but sabotage by an unseen hand "working down a list." If they found the **courier's letter** / the journal's "fine lady," let Urwin's words click with it: *someone is deliberately waking the valley's buried evils.* He doesn't know who yet. *(If this runs after [[Session 1]], the hand already has a name — Lyssa named herself from the rooftop — so play this beat as grim confirmation instead.)* See [[The Throughline — Lyssa & the Hidden War]]. **Do not** mention Strahd here.

> [!readaloud]
> Urwin counts out the gold without a word, then stands looking at the dark tavern. "I'd hoped I was wrong," he says quietly. "That place — what happened to those women — it wasn't a haunting. Somebody *dug that temple up on purpose.* Somebody who knew what slept inside, and didn't care who it ate getting to it." He looks at you. "My people have been seeing this. All over the valley. Old, sleeping evils, woken one after another, like someone's working down a list." A pause. "I think it's time you met us properly. My people have taken the village church — we're sheltering the folk who've nowhere else to go. Come at dusk. There's more gold in it — and a great deal more truth."

> [!example] Wrap-up
> - **Pay:** 500 GP each (+ 2 Quaal's Feather Tokens if the skeleton was destroyed).
> - **Dark Gift:** [[Second Skin]] to one PC, if taken.
> - **Level up to 7** (milestone — see [[Rewards & Leveling]]).
> - **Hook out:** *Join the Keepers at the village church at dusk.*
