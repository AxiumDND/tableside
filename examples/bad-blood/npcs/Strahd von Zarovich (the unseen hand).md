*Climate, not combatant. He baited them in, pointed them at his rival, and pays in an open road when the work is done.*

## Look & voice

- **Look** — Tall, regal, immaculate. Pale hawk features; unbothered stillness.
- **Manner** — Courteous, amused, utterly assured. Politeness that lands like a threat.
- **Quotes** — *"Do not mistake an open door for a friend."* · *"You kept your half of a bargain you never knew you'd made."*

![[Curse of Strahd - Castle Ravenloft.webp]]

> [!gmonly]
> **Golden rule.** A Level 6–8 party cannot beat him. Keep him off-screen and felt. If they force a fight, he toys, lands the lesson, and lets them live.
>
> **His hand is revealed by Lyssa**, at the Trap — she tells them he invited them to remove her. His only signatures: the Invitation's borrowed crest-seal, and the Mists opening when the work is done.
>
> The Farewell letter, black coach, and on-screen cameo were cut (21 Jul). Keep that material for a sequel — fifty years of restraint will be worth it. Ernst Larnak, his spy, is also cut.
>
> He notices a pattern: old horrors waking, all one hand — [[Lyssa von Zarovich]]. Too proud to be *seen* striking at family, so he opens the right door and lets the right strangers wander through.

> [!readaloud]
> A figure at the edge of the Mist where a moment ago there was none — tall, pale, unbothered by the cold. He does not approach. "The rest, I have written down. Read it on the road." When you blink, the road home is there, and he is not.

## Notes

Optional foreshadowing: wolves that pace and never strike; the castle always in view; locals falling silent at his name.
