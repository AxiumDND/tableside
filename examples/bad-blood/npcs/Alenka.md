*The strong, blunt eldest sister who ran the kitchen — now a sound-hunting killer with butcher's cleavers. A tragedy wearing a grin.*

## Look & voice

- **Look** — Gaunt, jerky, too-fast; too many teeth; blood-stiff apron; a cleaver in each hand.
- **Manner** — Silent stalking, then sudden savagery. Hunts the noisiest PC. Wet, delighted giggling — she doesn't speak.

> [!gmonly]
> **Don't run her as a stand-up brawler.** Stalk, strike, Vanishing Strike 30 ft., repeat. Two or three dread beats before the real exchange. Recoils from sudden bright light. ~60 damage/round burst — respect it. Full tactics in [[Tavern — Blood o' the Vine (Backup)]].
>
> She is a **victim**, not a villain — corrupted by what Sorvina woke. No curing her; the mercy is a clean death.

## Notes

Cut content. In play only if the party goes into the tavern.
