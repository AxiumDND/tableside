# The Sisters — Alenka, Mirabel & Sorvina

*The corrupted owners of the Blood of the Vine. Cut from the arc — in play only if the party goes in. Backup: [[Tavern — Blood o' the Vine (Backup)]].*

Three sisters, transformed after **Sorvina** excavated the Temple of Ravenkind beneath their tavern. [[Lyssa von Zarovich]]'s agent steered her there. **Victims, not villains** — play the tragedy.

> [!warning]
> Source names them only **Alenka, Mirabel, and Sorvina** — no surname, and they are **not** Martikovs. Keep them distinct from [[Urwin Martikov]]'s family.
