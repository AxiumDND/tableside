*The frightened, stubborn old priest who keeps the church doors open to the desperate. Already met in Session 0.5 — a human face for the place they are now defending.*

## Look & voice

- **Look** — Sixties; threadbare cassock; worn holy symbol of the dawn; a candle in hands that shake.
- **Manner** — Tired, devout, quietly brave. Lights the candles even when his hands shake.
- **Quote** — *"This is a house of the dawn, devils. You are not welcome at its door."*

> [!gmonly]
> Shepherds the Brauns and the wounded into the undercroft. Not a fighter. Once during the siege, his prayer can grant a PC a Holy Water flask or a one-use +1d6 radiant strike — small, but it sells the sanctuary. Let the candles flare when the prototype crosses the threshold.

## Notes

Doesn't fully trust the wereravens. The Mists have taught him to take what shelter he can.
