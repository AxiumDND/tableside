*A flexible face for Mist-travel if you don't want an unmanned coach. She speaks in half-prophecies and knows far too much.*

## Look & voice

- **Look** — Ancient; layered scarves; a Tarokka deck in weathered hands.
- **Manner** — Knowing, amused. Never explains the rules; only hints.
- **Quote** — *"The Mists brought you. The Mists will decide when you leave. I only drive the horses."*

> [!gmonly]
> **Entry alt:** a Vistani feast on the road; by morning they have crossed. **Exit alt:** an escort at the treeline. Optional Tarokka pull — [[Handouts & Props]]. Don't let her solve the mystery. See [[The Mists — Getting In & Out]].

## Notes

Optional. Skip her if the Invitation and the Mists are doing the work.
