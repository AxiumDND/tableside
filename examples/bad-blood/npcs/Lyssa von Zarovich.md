*A distant von Zarovich who means to depose Strahd — waking the valley's buried evils and building Mist-touched servants. The party spend the arc with only a handwriting. Session 3 is the first time they see her.*

## Look & voice

- **Look** — Tall, pale, aristocratic; faded finery a century out of date; eyes that weigh you like a butcher weighs meat.
- **Manner** — Contemptuous, gracious, certain. Talks before she fights. Treats them as tools.
- **Quotes** — *"You're not his, are you? His dogs have a certain smell."* · *"You were invited. By my dear cousin on his mountain."* · *"The rest of you have nothing I want. But you. Little cousin."* *(the Offer — Lucian alone)*

> [!readaloud]
> A pale woman in old, fine clothes turns from the worktable as though you were expected for dinner. "There you are," she says. "Allow me the honour. I am Lyssa. Lyssa von Zarovich."

> [!gmonly]
> **Spine.** She is the cause of everything they meet. Find a buried site, wake what's sealed — chaos for Strahd, fuel for her Bluetspur servants. S1: her handiwork, never her. S2: never seen, never named. S3: she names herself, then names Strahd.
>
> **She knows.** She worked out that Strahd aimed the party at her, and she tells them so at the Trap — truthfully, out of spite. The Offer is to **Lucian alone** (his name back, a place at her side, priced at the party's lives). The other three are told they have nothing she wants.
>
> **Kinship.** Erased jailer-branch; distant elder cousin of Lucian's mother's line. **Not his mother.** *"Your mother's line ran. I stayed."*
>
> **How she dies.** She always flees when bloodied (~71 HP) — except during the Unchaining, when the Choir holds her and mist-escape fails. Three rounds; if they earn it, she dies on screen. Sunlight/Radiant is the weakness.
>
> Run **1** mind flayer for an un-boosted Level-8 table, **2** with the boost — this night runs 2 on purpose.

## Notes

Leaves the Bluetspur Mist talisman if she lives. If she dies, the sequel threat is the freed Choir and the Count who now owns the board.
