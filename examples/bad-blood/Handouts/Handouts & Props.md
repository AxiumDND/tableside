# Handouts & Props
*In-world texts to read aloud, print, or hand to players. Spoiler-free for players unless noted.*

---

## 1 — The Invitation *(before play — the hook onto the road)*
*A flattering commission from an anonymous patron that lures the party onto the east road, where the Mists take them. Secretly from **Strahd**; the enclosed token is the bait Mist talisman. Full printable prop: [[Letter — The Invitation]].*

> To the bearers of this letter — with my compliments. Your reputation travels further than you know… Take the east road, and keep to it after dark — the fog has a way of leading wanderers astray. Enclosed is a small token of my regard, a measure of a particular vintage, dark as old blood. Carry it. It will see you safe to my door. I have looked forward to our meeting for longer than you would credit. — *Your patron*

---

## 1½ — The Courier's Letter *(✂️ tavern cut — backup only)*
*Fine cream paper, an elegant hand, no signature — only a single pressed line at the foot. The arc's **first fingerprint** of [[Lyssa von Zarovich]], left unattributed. The same hand returns as the **"— L." orders** (prop 3) in Session 2; that's when "the lady" gets a name. See [[The Throughline — Lyssa & the Hidden War]]. **Don't explain it when it's found.***

> To Mistress Sorvina, with my regard — A little bird tells me you keep a curious mind behind that bar of yours. Then you will have wondered, as I have, what the old ground beneath your floor is keeping from you. There is a tomb in that hollow, I am quite certain of it — and the forgotten dead have no further use for what they were buried with. Dig, my dear. What you find there will change your fortunes entirely.
> — *the lady*

## 2 — Mirabel's Journal *(✂️ tavern cut — backup only)*
*Three torn, water-stained pages in a woman's hand, growing wilder down the page.*

> **First entry.** A fine lady's courier came to the door, asking for Sorvina by name. Since then she won't stop talking about a tomb full of treasure in the hollow beneath us — old stones down there, carved with ravens and a burning sun. She's started to dig. Alenka thinks she's mad. Maybe she is.
>
> **Second entry.** Her digging has uncovered standing stones, a whole ring of them. I'm frightened for her. She's started whispering to herself, sitting with them for hours, and she doesn't sleep any more. None of us sleep well now. The dreams.
>
> **Third entry.** She found bones at the centre of the stones. The bones of some *monster* — antlers, tusks, all wrong. We all dream of it now, and we wake *starving.* Sorvina says the hunger is a blessing. Food has lost its taste. But the patrons… the patrons smell *delicious.* God forgive me, I wrote that and I did not stop myself.

## 3 — The Intercepted Orders *(Session 1, morning after — on the dead wight)*
*A single folded page wrapped in oilcloth inside the wight's rotted coat. The hand is elegant — educated, feminine, utterly composed. **The arc's key physical clue** — it must reach the party's hands before they leave the church (if nobody searches the bodies, Urwin's kin find it at dawn). Lyssa named herself on the rooftop minutes earlier; let a player make the "— L." connection out loud. Don't make it for them.*

> The work at the old churchyard is nearly ready. The new servants take to the change well — better than the last. Keep the feather-folk busy — and the strangers too — until I send word. Leave no clever corpses.
> — L.

---

## 3½ — The Shipping Manifest *(Session 2, the chapel-crypt)*
*Tucked among the crates of stolen grave-goods — the **same elegant hand** as the orders (prop 3). This is the second fingerprint; by now the party knows exactly whose hand it is.*

> The oldest bones below. Reliquaries and anything with the sun-mark to me, intact. Burn the rest. When the seal is open you will be paid as agreed — and you will forget the stair.
> — L.

## 4 — Larnak's Contract *(Session 1, morning after — left on a broken pew)*
*A folded paper, absurdly formal, bone-dry despite the rain. He sets it down and is gone before anyone decides to stop him. **Already signed** — he signed without reading, which should unsettle more than haggling would. Hand it over and let Jasper the Scribe pick it apart. The unsettling details are load-bearing: he counted the party without counting them, and the witness line is blank on a document that claims witnesses.*

> **ARTICLES OF ENGAGEMENT**
>
> *Being a contract at law between* **ERNST LARNAK**, *factor, of no fixed residence (the first party), and* **the four visitors lately sheltered at the church of the village of Barovia** *(the company).*
>
> **The company undertakes:** *item*, to proceed to the old churchyard beyond the east wall; *item*, to cause all excavation there to cease, by whatever means the company judges proportionate; *item*, to be discreet.
>
> **The first party undertakes:** payment of **two thousand five hundred pieces of gold** upon completion, in coin of whatever age the company prefers. No advance is offered. None will be needed.
>
> *Signed before witnesses,*
> **E. Larnak**
> *witnessed by* ______________
>
> *(a small annotation in the margin, in the same hand, almost warm:)* — *first light, if you please. Schedules.*

> [!gmonly]
> "The four visitors" — he never asked how many they were. If a player notices the blank witness line or the counting, give it to them; no roll needed. The margin note is why the party marches at next dawn.

## 5 — Strahd's Farewell *(the end — the twist lands)*
*Handed to the party at the door home (Session 3, Movement 6) with the talisman that takes them back. The "patron" who sent the invitation was **Strahd**; they were his instrument all along. Full printable prop: [[Letter — Strahd's Farewell]].*

> Did you never wonder who sent you down that road? … You have done me a service. My ambitious cousin and her borrowed horrors will trouble my valley no longer — and I did not so much as lift my hand. … Three things, that we may part as friends. **Do not return.** **Do not speak my name where the fog can hear it.** **Do not mistake an open door for a friend.** Until we meet again — for I am patient, and you have proven *interesting* — *Strahd von Zarovich, Lord of Barovia.*

---

## 6 — Optional: Madam Eva's Tarokka Pull *(cold open or send-off)*
*Draw three cards (or improvise). Themed to this arc:*

- **The Past — the Broken One (Swords).** *"You came here broken open, all of you — pulled through a door you never chose. Something wanted you here."* (The bait talisman.)
- **The Present — the Tempter (Glyphs).** *"A smiling man walks beside your road. He offers gold and points the way. His master is not the one you think."* (Ernst / Strahd.)
- **The Future — the Donjon (the trapped card).** *"You will open a door beneath the earth, and what waits there has been waiting a long, long time. Whether you walk back out… the cards are quiet on that."* (The old churchyard.)

> [!gmonly]
> Keep the Tarokka ominous and vague — atmosphere, not a walkthrough. It's a great way to seed the twist without explaining it.
