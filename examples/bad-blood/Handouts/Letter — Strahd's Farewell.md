# Letter — Strahd's Farewell *(player prop)*

> [!gmonly]
> **Hand this to the players at the very end** (Session 3, Movement 6 — the door home), with the Mist talisman that takes them back. This is the twist landing: the "patron" who sent the [[Letter — The Invitation|invitation]] was **Strahd**, and they were his instrument all along. Let them read it themselves; don't explain it. Works whatever the outcome — adjust the one bracket if Lyssa was killed outright vs. driven off. 
>
> **The deepest cut:** [[Urwin Martikov]] believed the party were sent by *the Dawn* — an answered prayer. They believed a *patron* hired them. This letter reveals it was **Strahd** all along. If you've played Urwin's faith honestly, that false comfort makes the reveal ache. You don't have to spell it out — they'll feel it.

---

*(The same aged paper, the same blood-red wax seal pressed with a single ornate letter — and now, beneath the signature, you can read it: a **Z**. The hand is the same elegant old-fashioned script as the letter that started all this. You know it now, and it makes the back of your neck cold.)*

![[Letter — Strahd's Farewell.webp]]
*The print-ready prop. Hand it over and stay quiet while they read.*

> To the company who came through my Mists — and lived. My compliments.
>
> Did you never wonder who sent you down that road? Who set the little token in your hand, and let the fog do the rest? You did, I think. Toward the end. I saw it in you, and I was *delighted.*
>
> You have done me a service. My ambitious cousin and her borrowed horrors will trouble my valley no longer — and I did not so much as lift my hand. I had only to open the right door and let the right strangers wander through it. You were magnificent. You were exactly as useful as I had hoped.
>
> So I keep my word. The door home stands open; the Mists will carry you back the way they brought you. Go. Take your gold, your trinkets, and whatever this valley has carved into you that you will never quite leave behind.
>
> But understand three things, that we may part as friends.
>
> **Do not return.** The Mists know your scent now, and they are not always mine to call off.
>
> **Do not speak my name where the fog can hear it.** It carries.
>
> **Do not mistake an open door for a friend.**
>
> Until we meet again — and we may, for I am patient, and you have proven *interesting* —
>
> — *Strahd von Zarovich, Lord of Barovia*

---

> [!tip]
> "Whatever this valley has carved into you" = the **[[Second Skin]] Dark Gift** (if taken), scars, or simply the memory. Pair the read with the **Castle Ravenloft** image and the window going dark. See the closing movement of [[Session 3]] and [[Strahd von Zarovich (the unseen hand)]].
