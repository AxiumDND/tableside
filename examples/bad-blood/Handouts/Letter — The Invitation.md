# Letter — The Invitation *(player prop)*

> [!gmonly]
> **Hand this to the players at the very start** — it's why they're on the road the night the Mists take them. It reads as an ordinary, flattering commission from a wealthy patron. **It is secretly from Strahd**, and the *enclosed token* is the spent **Mist talisman** they'll find on themselves after they wake in Barovia (Session 1, Movement 0). Don't reveal the sender; the [[Letter — Strahd's Farewell|farewell letter]] pays it off. **It's deliberately generic — you don't need to know the party's name to hand it out.** If you happen to know one of their past deeds, you can name it in the second line for an uncanny "he knows us" touch — but it works fine cold.

---

*(Heavy cream paper, a blood-red wax seal pressed with a single ornate letter you can't quite place — a flourish, a serpent, a maker's mark? The hand is elegant and old-fashioned, the ink so dark it looks wet.)*

> [!gmonly] The seal is the tell
> It carries the **same red 'Z' seal** as [[Letter — Strahd's Farewell]] at the very end (von Zarovich) — the players shouldn't place it now, but they'll feel it later.

> To the bearers of this letter — with my compliments.
>
> Your reputation travels further than you know. I have followed the deeds of your little company with no small interest, and I find I am in want of people of exactly your quality — people who do not flinch.
>
> I will be plain, as plain folk prefer. I have work, and I pay as few men can. Three nights east, where the old road forks at the broken shrine, a matter of mine wants tending. **Five hundred gold to hear me out. A great deal more to see it done.**
>
> Take the east road, and keep to it after dark. The country thereabouts grows a fog of an evening, and the fog has a way of leading wanderers astray. Enclosed is a small token of my regard — a measure of a particular vintage, dark as old blood. Carry it. It will see you safe to my door.
>
> I look forward to our meeting.
>
> I have looked forward to it for longer than you would credit.
>
> — *Your possible new patron*
