# Session 1 — Actual Play Record
*What actually happened at the table — 13 July 2026. Three players: Glen (Dallas), Nat (Lykta), David (Jasper — first session). Joe absent — Lucian held the bell tower all night, as planned.*

> [!warning] Deviations from the run guide — the big ones
> - **Only two waves ran: Wave 1 and Wave 3 (the prototype). Wave 2 was cut entirely** — no vampire spawn assault on the walls, **no Sef clock. Sef was never in danger.**

> - **The Ravenkind Shard was never handed over.** Donovich wasn't formally introduced until after the fighting. **It's still at the altar — must happen next session before the crypt.**
> - **No wereraven reveal** — Urwin helped as a human throughout. Still in pocket.
> - **No Lyssa cameo, no Ernst Larnak, no contract.** Neither appeared.
> - **The "— L." note was found on the mind flayer prototype**, not the wight or a spawn.
> - **Session ended at morning after the long rest** — no graveyard investigation, no march to the shattered grave. All Movement 4–5 content is now Session 2's open.
> - **Jasper did not drink the wine.** The vial is still in his pack.

## Part 1 — Jasper's road in (~25 min)

Session opened with David finalising Jasper on D&D Beyond — AC 19, HP 52, silvered rapier, Longbow of Warning (2024 wording confirmed at the table: he and allies within 30 ft can't be surprised), holy water, owl familiar. Table described him as "Van Helsing with a notebook" — monster hunter by trade, survivor by choice.

**What Jasper actually did in the Mists:**
- Got lost when the Mists came in, eventually stumbled back to the party's camp. Found the vial of black wine and Lykta's note (with an arrow pointing the direction they went).
- **Chose not to drink it.** Arcana 12 — no major vibes, but it was red when delivered and is now black tar, sickly sweet. He pocketed the vial and followed the thinning mist instead.
- Survival **dirty 20** — tracked the party cleanly to the gates (standing open).
- **Spotted a young girl (~15, dark hair, simple garb) darting between the trees, clearly searching for someone while trying not to be seen.** Nature check 11 — couldn't read more. He hid (Stealth 19); **she passed within feet of him and never knew.** *This was Sef, out looking for him. Neither knew.*
- Arrived at the village at dusk as the undead massed, guided in by Lucian yelling from the bell tower.

## Part 2 — Reunion & the last quiet minutes (~20 min)

- The wine question landed exactly as hoped. Lykta: *"Did you drink it?"* Jasper: *"No."* Table: *"Oh no, he's gonna die."*
- Party caught Jasper up on the patron, the letter, the token. Jasper: *"Did you see the monster?"* — *"I saw a little girl."*
- Urwin (read at the table as an older barkeeper-ish helper — **still unrevealed**) barred doors and windows: attacks for the last two weeks, *"but there seem to be so many of them tonight."*
- Jasper's Investigation 16 (after a nat 1 re-roll): ghouls, wolfish amalgams, *and other things that don't belong together* — undead that shouldn't be working with one another. His monster-hunter instincts flagged it as wrong from the start.
- Positioning: Jasper barred the front door and guarded the floor; Lykta took a window ledge on the side Lucian couldn't cover; Dallas helped shove pews against the doors; Lykta's owl went up by the bell tower as eyes.
- Jasper's parting wisdom as the doors shook: **"Don't try poison. Poison will not work on the ghouls."**

## Part 3 — Wave 1: The Doors (~60 min)

Doors exploded inward — **ghouls and dire wolves** at the front, **wight archer** sniping from the graveyard, **specter** through the wall.

| Beat | What happened |
|---|---|
| **Jasper opens** | *"You shall go no further."* Cast **Protection from Evil and Good** on himself, held the line at the door. |
| **The specter** | Phased straight through the wall (*"There's a ghost. There's a GHOST."*), hit Jasper for 13 — he made the Con save (11 vs DC 10), no max-HP drain. |
| **Dallas vs the specter** | **Misty Stepped onto the altar** and burned it down with Fire Bolts (8, then 16 — *"you vaporise the shadow away"*). Specter dead by round 3. |
| **★ The beam jump ★** | Dallas tried to leap from the altar to a roof beam. **Rolled a 4.** Crashed to the floor, 7 fall damage, prone — *"just looking around to make sure no one saw him do that."* Then got on the broom like a sensible gnome and cast **Haste** on himself. |
| **Jasper in melee** | Rapier + Vex advantage — eviscerated the first ghoul, then killed a dire wolf. Learned mid-fight that you can't holy-water-coat a blade (nice try). Action Surged to clean up. |
| **Lykta's rough night** | Wolf and ghoul attention; dropped from 40 to 17 HP. **Went Starry Form (Chalice)** — glimmering constellation — and Cure Wounds'd herself back to full (24 + chalice bonus). Big heals kept everyone up. |
| **The wight** | Never entered. Sniped at Dallas on the broom and traded fire with Lucian's tower — then **took a hit from something else out in the dark and moved off.** Never killed; never pursued. |

**★ Faction war — seen and understood.** Lucian yelled it down from the tower first: *"There are two groups — one from the Mist, one from the tree line — we're caught in the crossfire!"* Lykta risked her owl overhead to confirm: undead and wolves from one direction, **strange creatures and floating figures from the other, fighting each other**, with the church merely in the middle. The wight taking fire from an unseen third party sealed it. The players clocked the faction war in Wave 1, on their own, hard.

## Part 4 — The breather (~15 min)

DM gave them 1–2 minutes of in-fiction time. Smart play throughout:
- Barricaded every window **except one, deliberately left open as a funnel** — *"if they're going to come through, make them come through where we choose."*
- Dragged the heavy pews over the **crypt trapdoor** to shield the families below.
- Lykta lit every sconce (Light cantrip) plus her own Chalice glow.
- **Readied actions before initiative:** Jasper held a holy-water throw on the door; Lykta held Guiding Bolt on the first thing through.
- Dallas burned a sorcery point to convert back a spell slot.

## Part 5 — Wave 3 (run as the second wave): The Prototype (~60 min)

Through the funnel: a ghoul, a **vampire spawn** (scraggly grey-haired woman, fangs), and behind them — floating, tentacled, in a dead man's long coat — **the prototype.**

| Beat | What happened |
|---|---|
| **★ Jasper's trick shot ★** | Held action triggered: **threw a holy water bottle in the air and threw his dagger through it** — glass shatters, dagger flies on into the creatures, holy water splashes the lot. 11 radiant, spawn and ghoul both failed. Table highlight of the night. |
| **Lykta's held Guiding Bolt** | Slammed the prototype the moment it floated into view — 12 radiant + advantage rider. Followed with Starry Wisp (11 radiant). Radiant clearly hurting it. |
| **Mind Burst** | The big moment fizzled gloriously — DC 15 Int save, and the table rolled **21, 17, 19. All three saved.** *"You have broken your nuts."* |
| **The prototype fights dirty** | Climbed to Lykta, claw + tentacles: 10 slashing + **15 necrotic (max HP drained)**, then grappled her. She healed through it (Cure Wounds 24 + Chalice spill-over onto Jasper) and **punched it in the tentacles** — 12 magical bludgeoning, twice. |
| **Dallas from the broom** | Sorcerous Burst (acid), quickened **Magic Missile** (14), Fire Bolts. Steady artillery all wave. |
| **The kill** | **Jasper carved the tentacles off Lykta and put the prototype down** — body hit the ground, grapple broken. The spawn clawed Lykta and immediately ate an 11-damage counterblow with 4 HP left. Wave over. |

**Lucian, from the tower, as it died:** *"The forces are retreating toward the crypts. One side is winning."*

## Part 6 — Aftermath in the nave (~35 min)

**The "— L." note** — Jasper searched the prototype (Investigation 19, Lykta assisting): inside the coat, in a very elegant hand —

> *"The work at the old churchyard is nearly ready. The new servants take the change well — better than the last. Keep the feather folk busy — and the strangers too — until I send word. — L."*

The crest on the paper matches the seal from the party's original letter; the Harbinger's sword-runes were *close but not identical* (same family, different branch — they remembered from the gate). The table is now actively theorising **a conflict between two related bloodlines.**

**The prisoner tattoos** — investigating the spawn's body: crude, common tattoos — **a number sequence on one arm, a gang mark on the other.** Urwin, asked about prisons: *"There are containment places in the cities of Barovia... not everybody here is from here."* **New clue landed: Lyssa's servants are being made from prisoners.**

**The dominated wolves** — Lykta, Arcana **25** on the wolf corpses: *definitely* magically controlled, 100%. She then asked Urwin — carefully, apologetically — *"You have an affinity with ravens. Does anyone else have something similar?"* Urwin: **"We believe Strahd can. Wolves. Bats."** The table connected it themselves: two powers fighting for control, and the church is just in the way.

**Father Donovich** — formally met at last. Attacks for two weeks, a few hours a night, never this scale, and they went for the church *fast*. The old crypts are at the **far north end of the churchyard** — which is exactly where the surviving forces retreated. He gave Jasper **2 holy water**, agreed to walk Lykta round the **graveyard family crests in the morning** (his crest-recognition roll: nat 1 — *"it looks... a little familiar. Apologies."*). **The Shard never came up — it's still at the altar.**

**★ Sef's actual entrance ★** — a hammering on the barricaded door: *"Hello? HELLO? Is everyone all right? My uncle's still in there!"* **Dallas, from inside, attempting menace: "I'm a fully armed barbarian warrior!"** — fooling absolutely no one. (Insight 16: no harm in her at all.) Urwin: *"Sef — is that you?"* She'd been out for days as the contact on the ravens' end, **searching for Jasper** — and Jasper realised, out loud, that she's the girl he hid from at the gates. Lovely, entirely accidental payoff. She's safe, she's inside, and she's now met the whole party.

**The wounded** — one of the three village defenders outside went down during the night; the party stabilised him (they'd also handed out healing potions before the siege — it mattered).

**Small colour:** Jasper started a formal notebook entry — *new species: undead mind flayer* — very much his whole character in one image. The children below are still pale and vacant, a few slightly brighter than before. Someone mentioned the tavern; the table agreed it was not tonight's problem.

## Part 7 — Close

- Bodies dragged outside, doors re-barricaded, watch rotation from the bell tower with familiars as extra eyes. **Long rest completed.**
- **Level 7 banked** (fixed HP). At-table choices: Lykta hit **Monk 3 → Warrior of Mercy** (traded the duplicate Insight/herbalism proficiencies by DM ruling); Jasper weighed **War Magic**; Dallas is staying pure sorcerer (*"AC 24 with Shield, baby"*).
- Morning came — what passes for it, a brighter grey. **Session ends here.** The crypts, the graveyard investigation, and everything in Movements 4–5 are untouched.
