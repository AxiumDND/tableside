# Session 3 — Actual Play Record
*What actually happened at the table — 27 July 2026 (2h 48m). All four players: Glen (Dallas), Joe (Lucian), Nat (Lykta), David (Jasper). Full Otter transcript: [[session 3 actual transcript]]. Arc wrap-up and sequel notes: [[Bad Blood — Arc Conclusion & Continuity]].*

> [!warning] Deviations from the run guide — the big ones
> - **★ THE PARTY LIVED. All four of them.** The Long Dark never fired. Nobody went down permanently; Lykta was the only PC to drop, and she was up again inside a minute. The planned TPK ending — shallow graves, fifty years, old Sef — is **unplayed and unspent.**
> - **★ THE FAREWELL LETTER AND THE MESSENGER CAME BACK.** Both were cut on 21 July. At the table the DM reinstated them: **Ernst Larnak walked down the shaft during the party's short rest**, introduced himself by name, and handed over **Strahd's letter with the matching seal**. The Count's frame was revealed in full, in his own words, from a page. *"I had only to open the right door and let the right strangers wander through."*
> - **★★ AND THE PARTY DID NOT TAKE THE DOOR.** Asked straight out whether they walked into the Mists, they **didn't go.** Lykta wanted her hit point maximum back before she'd leave (*"I feel like I've lost all of my income"*), and the answer was *"give it time."* **The arc ends with all four PCs still inside Barovia, standing in a wrecked crypt, having declined the Devil of Barovia's one and only courtesy to his face.** This is the biggest single consequence of the night and it is entirely the players' doing. **Strahd's account is now open, and it is not open in their favour.**
> - **★ The Choir Below never spoke — because it was in the amber sarcophagus, and Lykta let it out with her own blood.** The whole Binding apparatus (two anchor-stones, the Chime, the seal, the three-round Unchaining, the round-by-round coaching) **did not run.** Instead: Lykta, bloodied and grappled, staggered to the amber sarcophagus, put a bleeding hand on it, heard ***"I'm so glad I saved you"*** — and it opened. Silver light, a 40-ft column, a room-wide detonation (DC 14 Dex, 21 force), the last mind flayers destroyed by shadows, and **its power poured into Lykta**, who dropped unconscious and glowing green. Wildly better than the plan. Wildly harder to bill for.
> - **★ Lyssa was named, but the Reveal and the Offer never landed.** She gave her name freely and warmly, half-admitted the letter (*"a cousin of mine may have been involved"*) — and then **Jasper attacked before she got to the Frame or to Lucian's Offer.** The Strahd reveal arrived forty minutes later, in Ernst's letter, instead.
> - **★ Lyssa died — twice.** Lucian burned her to ash with Misty Step + Eldritch Blast; **the ash crawled as mist through a gap behind the sarcophagus to her hidden coffin.** The party worked it out cold (nat 20 on the vampire lore check), blasted the casket lid off through the gap and put **Starry Wisp** into the ash. **She is permanently destroyed.**
> - **The Warden fight was a rout.** Faerie Fire + ball bearings + Mirror Image + Phantasmal Force, and he was down in ~5 rounds having landed one real hit. *"Well, that's as easy as I thought."* **Executioner's Arc never recharged. Level 8 was never awarded** — despite the radiant trigger being met.
> - **Four mind flayers, not two.** All four tubes were live and all four fought. This is what made the Trap genuinely dangerous.
> - **The Offer, the anchor-stones, the Chime's real purpose, the sentinels in the green passage, and the green passage itself** — all unplayed.

## Part 1 — The button (~10 min)

Cold open exactly where S2 cut: the Warden halfway down the dais on chains, the party at the great door, Jasper's ward lit.

The table spent its thirty seconds well. **Ball bearings across the cavern floor** in front of the dais (Glen), a quick huddle — *"Do we go through the door, guys?"* — and then the call: **press the button.** The doors began their slow grind.

📖 **First look at Area B, through ten seconds of opening door:** a crypt with a **throne**; a woman seated at the far end; **four large tubes flanking the room with figures suspended in liquid**; and behind her, **an absolutely enormous sarcophagus, half stone, half amber.**

The party's response was, correctly, *"I'm tempted to close the fucking door again"* — and they did. From the far end of the room came **a laugh.**

## Part 2 — Combat 1: The Warden at the Door (~35 min, five rounds, one bad day)

**Longbow of Warning: whole party advantage on initiative.** The fight never got out of second gear.

**No short rest.** Lucian burned **Magical Cunning** for slots (one minute), Lykta chugged a healing potion, and the party walked to the doors. *"I think we should go and talk to the person that just laughed at us."*

## Part 3 — The naming (~15 min — she got about half her material out)

The doors opened. Lykta ran straight up the wall. Lucian walked in and opened with *"My name is Lucian. You come here often?"*

> **"My name is Lyssa."**
> *"And it is good to finally meet you all. Assuming you're the ones that have been holding out at the church these last nights."*

- **The letter.** Asked straight out whether she sent it: ***"Maybe one of them. A cousin of mine may have been involved in one of the others."*** — the Frame, offered as a shrug, and the table did not pull the thread. Lucian read the workshop manifest back to her (*"the work of the old churchyard is nearly ready… keep the feather folk busy, and strangers too, until I send word"*). She didn't blink.
- **The bloodline.** *"You're of my line, aren't you?"* — *"As am I."* Recognition, both ways, in about four words.
- **Insight, escalating: 13 → 21 → 28.** Ruling at the table: *partial truth every time.* *"It's always for her own game."* At 28: **the party could tell she isn't certain her plan will work — but she is giving it a damn good chance.**
- **Her pitch.** Free the valley from his control and take it back. *"My brother has ruled this land — sorry, my **cousin** has ruled this land for a long time. It can't be any worse than that."*
- **The interrogation was the best roleplay of the night.** The party pressed her on the things that actually mattered to them: *Will the villagers be safer?* — *"I don't know if I can free people from this land, but if it was not under his control, I think things would be better."* *Do you not have to feed?* — *"There is gadgetry."* *Can you send us home?* — ***"Honestly, I don't know. But when I have control of this place, maybe then I also have that power. Maybe."***
- **The tubes.** Investigation through the haze: **four vampire mind flayers**, ready to go. *"They are experiments. They have taken the change very well… they are useful weapons."* She confirmed one of them hit the church — and confirmed the leash: ***"It's not perfect control. Apologies. I do send them through."***
- **★ The sarcophagus spoke.** Only Lykta caught it, from the amber at the far end, glowing faintly: ***"We are saved."*** Asked what it was, Lyssa was honest in the worst way: *"It's a dark power. I do not understand everything about it yet, but I will."*
- **★ The trigger was Jasper, not Lyssa.** No Offer, no Frame speech, no *"and these are my children."* David walked up to her pedestal, said the quiet part — ***"One vampiric dictator over another is not really a winning situation one way or the other"*** — and opened with a blade.

## Part 4 — Combat 2: The Trap (~60 min, five rounds, genuinely dangerous)

**Jasper's opener hit for double damage** (23) before she could react. Her own first turn was two whiffs and a bite that rolled double sixes on the dice that don't help.

Then **all four tubes opened.**

| Beat | What happened |
|---|---|
| **The flayers** | Four, not two. Ceiling-crawling, floating, 30-ft moves, **Mind Burst DC 15 Int** (the party made every one of them) — and then the real threat: **tentacle grapple → Drink Sapience.** 25, then 22, then 19 psychic, each with **a level of exhaustion and a permanent max-HP reduction**, healing the flayer for the same. |
| **★ Lykta's nat 1** | Natural 1 on the Wisdom save; her own feature made her roll a second save to avoid something worse (she passed). **25 psychic, exhaustion, max HP shredded.** She spent most of the fight grappled by something that was eating her mind, and never stopped acting. |
| **Lucian's necrosis** | Form of Dread + **Arcane Necrosis** — converting damage to necrotic *and ignoring resistance* — plus the extra die on one blast. Knockback on every hit. He was the party's reliable damage all fight. |
| **★ 128 DAMAGE** | Jasper, hasted, with advantage and a **natural 20**: *"Just carving into her like Zorro."* **128 points on Lyssa across the fight** and she was *officially* bloodied. |
| **Lyssa runs** | She tried to slip into the shadows and vanish. Nobody's passive perception cleared 16. Then **Dallas dropped a Fireball on the area — 29 damage** — and she reappeared, robes burning, cursing him. |
| **★ ASH** | Lucian **Misty Stepped** to her and hit for 28. *"You will turn her into ash."* Grey mist, lying on the ground where she'd been. |
| **★ …and the ash crawled** | The mist moved. Behind the great amber sarcophagus, into a gap, and **down.** The table clocked it immediately: *"If somehow we can get in there, we can kill her at the moment."* |
| **The grind** | Four mind flayers with the boss gone is still four mind flayers. Fireball on the ceiling (29, three failed saves). Jasper cut himself free by **severing the tendrils carved around one's head**. Lykta was grappled, drained, grappled again, and dropping fast. |

### ★ The amber sarcophagus — the beat of the arc

**Lykta, on the edge of unconsciousness, walked to the amber sarcophagus and put her bloody hand on it.**

> ***"I'm so glad I saved you."***

- A silvery column of pale light bloomed out from under her hand — **5-ft radius, 40 ft high**, moonlight in a crypt.
- The DM asked the question out loud: *"Would she accept their gifts?"* — and answered it: *"At this point she's so far gone she wouldn't know how to say no."*
- **The sarcophagus detonated.** Everyone in the room: **DC 14 Dex, 21 force damage**, half on a save.
- **Shadows rose around the enemies** — *"as they have been asked to do, in some form"* — and **the last of the mind flayers were destroyed.**
- **Power poured into Lykta.** She went down unconscious, **glowing green**, and was brought round with a potion.

**Aftermath (Investigation 22, History 27):** the amber shell is empty. Whatever was inside was **far older than anything else in the crypt — ancient, immensely powerful, present before any of this.** *"Lord knows what she was trying to do with it, but whatever that was would not have been good."* Jasper sketched it into the monster notebook. *(Nobody at the table knows what it was. Neither, yet, does Lykta.)*

### ★ Finishing Lyssa properly

- The bat familiar squeezed through the gap behind the sarcophagus: **a small chamber, and a coffin.** No door.
- **Natural 20 on the vampire lore check:** they know exactly what's needed — **holy water or radiant damage, while she's in mist form in her resting place.**
- They **blasted the lid off through the gap** (Magic Missile and Eldritch Blast between them), the bat looked down at **the ash inside**, and Lykta put **Starry Wisp** — radiant — straight into it.

**Lyssa von Zarovich is dead. Permanently. No mist, no coffin, no return.**

**Loot from the sanctuary:** a **large gem on a chain** from the body, the rune blade, the second signet ring, and the wreck of a laboratory nobody had time to properly inventory.

## Part 5 — The offer, refused (~15 min)

The party settled into a **one-hour short rest** in the ruin, trying to work out what they'd just let out.

📖 **A man came down the shaft.**

> *"Looks like you are all done down here. Well done. My name is **Ernst.** I have been sent to offer you passage from this place."*

He carried a letter. **The seal matched the seal on the Invitation** — the same crest, the same wax. Read aloud, in the Count's voice:

> *"For the company that came through the Mists and lived — my compliments.*
> *Do you ever wonder who sent you down that road? He sent that little token into your hand and let the fog do the rest. I saw it in you, and I was delighted.*
> ***You have done me a service. My ambitious cousin and her borrowed horrors will trouble my valley no longer — and I did not have to so much as lift my hand. I had only to open the right door and let the right strangers wander through.***
> *You were magnificent. You were **exactly as useful as I had hoped.***
> *So I keep to my word: the door home stands open. The Mists will carry you back the way you were brought. Take your gold, your trinkets, and whatever this valley has carved out of you — you will never quite leave it behind.*
> *But understand three things, that we may part as friends:*
> ***Do not return. The Mists know your scent now, and they are not always mine.***
> ***Do not speak my name where the fog can hear it. It carries.***
> ***Do not mistake an open door for a friend.***
> *Should we meet again — and we may, for I am patient — you have proven interesting."*

### ★★ And then they didn't take it

The DM put the question directly: *do you take the offer, and go out through the Mists?*

**They didn't.**

**Lykta** had the strongest reason and said it plainly: the mind flayers had eaten a permanent hole in her — *"I feel like I've lost all of my income"* — and she wanted to know whether anything in this valley could give it back before she left it. The answer was **"not in the moment, but there'll be time"** and ***"give it time."*** The others noted the gold was still owed. Nobody walked into the fog.

**The arc ends with all four PCs still inside Barovia**, standing in a wrecked laboratory in a desecrated family tomb, holding a dead vampire's gem and a dead champion's sword, with something ancient riding in one of them — having been handed the only exit the Count has ever offered anyone, and **left it standing open behind them.**

> [!gmonly] ★ What this actually costs them
> The run guide's answer to a refusal was gentle — *"the road simply waits, patient as its owner."* **That is not how it should play now.** Strahd's letter was a **settlement**: a service rendered, an account closed, three conditions, and a door. Refusing the door doesn't pause the transaction — it **rejects the terms**, in front of his messenger, in his valley, from four people who have just proved they can kill a von Zarovich.
>
> He said it himself: *"Do not mistake an open door for a friend."* They didn't. They mistook it for something they could keep in their pocket.
>
> **He was patient because they were leaving.** They are not leaving. Everything about his tone in that letter — *magnificent · exactly as useful as I had hoped · you have proven interesting* — is the tone a man uses about **a tool he is putting back in the drawer.** A tool that declines to go back in the drawer is not a tool any more. It's a **loose end**, and loose ends are the one thing [[Ernst Larnak|his people]] are explicitly on record as hating.
>
> Suggested read for whatever comes next: **the door closes, and the courtesy with it.** No second letter. No coach. The Mists that "know their scent now" stop being an exit and start being a fence. And somewhere between them and the eastern road, the wolves stop conspicuously sparing anyone.

Closing table quote, from Nat, still holding a character sheet with a savaged maximum HP: ***"It was still a good fight, so I'm very happy."***

## What this means for the campaign

Short version: **the players won a fight the prep expected them to lose, were handed the only way out of Barovia anyone gets, and turned it down** — leaving four living PCs inside a closed valley, one of them carrying something ancient, and a Count who has now introduced himself in writing and been snubbed in the same evening.

Everything the arc leaves live — Lykta's inheritance from the amber, the unanswered letter, Strahd's three conditions, Ernst's face, the village left mid-crisis, and the loot state — is written up in **[[Bad Blood — Arc Conclusion & Continuity]]**. Read that before these characters are ever used again.

Unfinished business worth a line here:

1. **★★ They are still in Barovia, and they refused Strahd's door.** Everything else on this list is downstream of that. The valley is now a closed room with four people in it who embarrassed its owner.
2. ✅ **Level 8 — awarded.** Never called at the table, resolved afterwards: **they play at Level 8 next time.**
3. **★ Lykta is carrying something.** Nobody at the table knows what — including Nat. That is a gift, a debt, and now a reason she cannot simply be allowed to leave.
4. **Lykta's hit point maximum is still shredded.** It is the stated reason they stayed. Whatever restores it is the party's next quest, and the valley's only sources of that kind of power belong to people who charge.
5. **The unattuned second signet ring** and the **rune blade** left the crypt with them. So did **a large gem** (the Gem of Brightness, unidentified).
6. **The village is still up there, mid-crisis** — no goodbyes, no Sef beat, no Donovich, and the party is still underground with the war's cause dead and nobody told.
