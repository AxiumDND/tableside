# Session 2 — Actual Play Record
*What actually happened at the table — 20 July 2026 (2h 52m). All four players: Glen (Dallas), Joe (Lucian — back after missing S1), Nat (Lykta), David (Jasper). Full Otter transcript: [[session 2 actual transcript]].*

## Part 1 — The morning, and the letter (~30 min)

Session opened with the night resolved from Lucian's tower view: one force pushed the other back past the church, then **something came out of the crypts and finished the last of the undead** — and all went quiet. Long rest completed, **Level 7 applied** (fixed HP sorted at the table for Joe).

**★ The letter.** Nat cashed in a note she'd made during S1: with an hour of the siege night spare and two familiars available, **Lykta** composed a message to **"Lord Zarovich"** — roughly: *she and her companions found themselves embroiled in a faction battle at the village church; the Count should perhaps investigate, for the villagers' safety; she intends no offence and no interference with any plans; and if the conflict is in the Count's interest, she will keep an ear out for his reply.* Sent by familiar into the dark. **No reply came.** ("Someone that wanted to be your new patron — they haven't actually come across with that.") *The spirit medium who hears the dead, writing courteous correspondence to the valley's apex predator — a dhampir introducing herself to the Count, politely, on spec.*

Morning business, light: the going-home question (the wine-as-key theory resurfaced — Jasper still has the corked vial and the wax-sealed bottle exists); **Insight 17 on Donovich** — earnest, exhausted, no bad vibes; nobody died in the night. Donovich offered to come; the party told him to **save his strength** — so no crest tour. **Urwin's ravens shadowed them** to the crypt instead. Re-reading the "— L." note: the DM ruled the hand does **not** match the Invitation letter; the Harbinger's runes ≈ the letter's seal stands from 0.5. Table joke of the morning: *"The L was actually for you, Lucian. You were the mastermind."* (Give it a minute, lads.)

## Part 2 — The battlefield & the name (~20 min)

Crossing the graveyard (Investigation 17/19): **two or three more graves match the crest from the letter** — same family, clustered near the crypt. Jasper broke out **parchment and chalk for a rubbing** (advantage), History 19: the worn name is **an older form of "Zarovich"** — *"not fully Zarovich, but of that line — an older branch."* First time the family-name connection truly landed at the table, from a gravestone rubbing.

## Part 3 — The Blood Door (~45 min — the puzzle got its full time and earned it)

The mausoleum as written: thorn-vine **half carved into the stone, half real and growing**, the chalice basin, and the inscription read aloud twice by increasingly worried players: *"THE TREE DRINKS ONLY OF ITS OWN. KNEEL, KIN, AND BE KNOWN. ALL ELSE FEEDS THE ROOTS."*

| Beat | What happened |
|---|---|
| **Reading it** | Investigation 19 (Jasper, magnifying glass + guidance): **old spattered blood in layers, and one clean recent pour that worked.** Arcana 20/19: warded — keyed to *undeath, faith, or force*. Detect Magic: the door blazing. Round the back: **defaced sun symbols** (Religion 15 — partial). |
| **The spawn-blood gambit** | Jasper ran back to the church and drew a vial from yesterday's vampire spawn corpse. **Dallas's Mage Hand came out SKELETAL** — Barovia has been quietly corrupting his magic — *"Honey, I don't think we're in Kansas anymore."* The bony hand poured spawn blood into the chalice from a safe distance: **nothing.** Wrong blood, no reaction. |
| **The thorns choose** | The vines **turned toward the dhampirs** like flowers to sun. Lucian's Wis save (17): *something pulling him toward the door.* Voices in his head — wanting him forward. A ten-minute tavern-vs-crypt debate later ("the Blood of the VINE… vines that drink blood… is the tavern connected?" — good instincts, wrong door), he committed. |
| **★ WELCOME HOME ★** | **Lucian knelt and gave the door his hand.** Thorns wrapped his arm, bit in, drank deep; the chalice filled and glowed; the door **sank into the earth** — and in his mind: ***"Welcome home, blood of the line."*** He told the party immediately: *"Maybe this is my mother's side. I don't know who my dad is."* The table went quiet in the right way. |

## Part 4 — The crawl (~90 min — methodical, familiar-led, excellent play)

🗺️ **The crypt as actually played** — drawn at the table, rooms lettered to the run-guide key:

![[Crypt Level 1 — as played (Session 2).webp]]
*The as-played layout (this supersedes the Neutral Party plan as canon): the niche hall with the sun rosette (A, bottom right), the urn room (B), the wells (C), the workshop with the strap tables (F, top centre), the broken span (H, the diagonal planks), the rotunda with the dais and the toppled statue-and-sword (K, right), and the dug hole down to the lower cavern (centre). The party's position at the cut: the cavern below the hole, at the great door.*

- **A — Hall of Chiselled Names:** every nameplate scrubbed (Inv 19 — methodical, purposeful), the sun rosette gouged *rougher, angrier* than the plates — and the swept boot-paths **deliberately detour around the sun symbol.** Dallas **Arcana 27**: this is *de-consecration* — someone stripping the wards that kept these dead from rising. The party put the whole erasure thesis together unaided.
- **B — Urn Store** *(scouted by Lucian's bat, Vampi)*: wax-sealed urns, residual ash — *the ashes of people* — names removed from the urns too.
- **C — Four Wells:** black fonts, defaced blessings; one still faintly magical. Arcana 23: *it might not take much to reignite it.* **Jasper poured holy water in — the font woke, ran crystal clear** — and they bottled **three vials of holy water** before it silted back. Then **Lucian drank from it.** Dhampir. It tasted *horrible*. (A nat 1 also happened in this general area and was legendary.) Possibly the strangest holy-water tasting in Barovian history — the blood of the line, sampling the family font.
- **D — The Warden's Work:** **three** bodies bisected along a corridor with a sunken drain-channel; Investigation 25 (with the monster notebook out): one enormous blade, one stroke each, gouges dragged in the stone. Comparisons to the thing from the gate were immediate. **★ Then Lucian cast Speak with Dead.★** The corpse answered: killed by *"a large, shadowy figure"* — and they were **"sent… by the master of these lands… to kill his enemies."** No crests, no tattoos, ordinary Barovian clothes. The table built the theory on the spot: **two branches of one family at war** — Strahd's dominated forces on one side, the crest-family on the other. *"Family drama. Christmas edition."* (They are exactly right, one session early, and earned it.)
- **E — The Chapel:** the drained-water chapel; Investigation 22 found the **thumb-slot under the altar lip** — the **silver funeral chime**, worn smooth, magical. Wisely wrapped in cloth rather than rung.
- **F — The Workshop:** grave-goods on trestle tables, crates, **two tables fitted with restraints**; the **Embalmer's Lens** (silver rim, maker's mark in the elegant hand, **an "L" engraved on the side** — they clocked it); two **scrolls: Gentle Repose + Protection from Evil and Good**, labelled in a hand matching the note; and the **brine jars** — Nature 25: *something was stored alive in salt solution*; Investigation 19: *"a tadpole… something very, very small."* **Two jars stand empty.** The table connected it themselves: *"the new servants take the change well" — the change needs people restrained.* Every experiment seed landed, no exposition. **A trapped door** (pressure plate — Jasper nat 20, Investigation 25) was found and disarmed.
- **The niche room:** four niches, one broken open — the bat spotted a body inside with a **glowing ring**: the **Signet** — blackened silver, the tree crest, *cold to the touch* (History 21: a crest of that line, an older styling).
- **★ Short rest in the dungeon ★** — the d12 gamble came up a **2**: an hour of quiet. Identified the lot: **the Signet** (advantage on Charisma vs the line's undead; a word of old Barovian; *Command*), **the Chime of Last Rites**, **the Embalmer's Lens.** *(Who carries the Signet — confirm at the top of next session; the ring's "blood of the line" attunement means it's warm in exactly one person's hand.)*
- **H — The Broken Span:** planks over blackness; a light-cast vial dropped — a long, long fall to a tiny point of light; wind roaring up the shaft — **and whispers.** Lykta wall-walked out over the dark and the voices found her: ***"Come down to us."*** She answered them out loud — *"Why are you down there? Come up."* They only repeat. She kept going. (Nat is collecting the arc's best horror beats one whisper at a time.)

## Part 5 — Combat 1: The Remnant (~25 min — two rounds, one crater)

**Longbow of Warning: whole party advantage on initiative.** Everyone but Dallas beat the room.

| Beat | What happened |
|---|---|
| **Jasper opens** | Through the doors into mid-room — rapier out, holy water ready, **Protection from Evil and Good** up, holding the line so the others could enter. |
| **Lykta from the ceiling** | **Guiding Bolt 19 radiant into the wight** (advantage rider hung on it) — then spider-dropped off the ceiling into a ghoul with a **nat 20** kick-and-punch. |
| **★ THE FIREBALL ★** | Dallas descended slowly on the broom until he was framed in the doorway, fireball swelling in his hand — and put a **4th-level Fireball** into the room. **36 damage. Every ghoul in range died instantly.** The wight made its save behind cover, half-singed. *"There is no such thing as overkill when it's a life-or-death situation."* |
| **Lucian cleans up** | Two Eldritch Blast bolts (22 and 19 to hit) — **21 damage, wight down.** The scorched S1 archer, cornered at last, never got its parley. |
| **The last ghoul** | Jasper: rapier 12, then **Green-Flame Blade** — left it *barely* standing — and **Lykta punched it dead** ("Can you do one hit point of damage?" — she could, twice over). |

**Loot:** the wight's quiver — **silver-tipped arrows** — claimed on a (nine) Investigation that found exactly the obvious thing. Nothing else pinged.

## Part 6 — The rotunda, the shaft, and the elevator (~40 min — the geography rewrote itself)

- **The cells:** four small chambers off the corridor, empty — leather restraints fixed to walls, human-sized. Same fittings as the workshop tables. Nobody home. Worse that way.
- **★ The rotunda, through a crack in the door:** a raised circular **dais**, and a **collapsed stone figure knocked off its plinth — a statue of a man with a large ruined sword.** They *recognised the sword instantly*: the thing from the gate. The statue's likeness stood on that dais once. They did NOT go in swinging — they investigated like professionals.
- **The dais mechanism (Inv 18):** pressure-plates on the dais that "press in" — a mechanism, purpose unknown. A second **trapped stone door** off the rotunda (Inv 23 — same pressure-plate trap family as the one Jasper disarmed; handled). Beyond it: a bare, quiet chamber flanked by **two stone guard statues** (checked: statues, for now), and another plain door.
- **★ Dallas up the light-shaft:** flew up through the crack in the rotunda's dome — a squeeze-sized opening to the surface, **behind the tree line near the mausoleum.** And the table put it together at once: *"this is where the things rose up that scared away the undead at the end of the siege."* **The dais is how the crypt's forces come and go.** Canon now: the siege ended from THIS room.
- **The dug hole:** through the inner door, a **freshly dug shaft** — mud, not stone, *"something dug down… something dug up"* — dropping about **70 feet** into a cavern. A copper coin with Light thrown down: far below, **the remnants of a stairway and a very, very large door.** The voices that wanted Lykta downstairs? **They're calling through that door.**
- **The descent:** broom-ferrying and careful climbing got everyone down (the DM ruled the broom is not a party bus). At the bottom: the great doors, the voices coming through them — and **two pressure-plate mechanisms matching the dais plates** (Inv 18/24). *"It's obviously a button. I like pressing buttons."* Plan formed: press it, and run.

## Part 7 — The cliffhanger

📖 As they stood weighing the button: **a thump from above. Chains rattling. And the dais began to LOWER into the shaft — with him standing on it.** The DM confirmed it at the table: *it's the Harbinger.* Halfway down as the session ended. Jasper got **Protection from Evil and Good** up before the cut ("I will, out of combat, cast another Protection from Evil" — veteran move). **"Shall we find out next time? We'll leave it as a cliffhanger."**

Position at cut: the party in the **lower cavern** at the great door (voices behind it, an openable mechanism at hand), the **Tomb-Warden descending the shaft toward them**, Jasper warded, everyone unbloodied but an hour of resources down. No level-up — *"go kill one Harbinger first."*
