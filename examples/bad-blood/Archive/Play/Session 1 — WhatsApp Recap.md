🧛 **BAD BLOOD — Session 1: The Night the Church Held** ⛪

Last night in Barovia...

Jasper walked out of the Mists! He found the camp, the note, and the ominous black wine — and NOPED right past it (vial's still in his pack 👀). Instead: a dirty-20 of tracking straight to the gates. En route he spotted a mysterious girl darting through the trees, clearly searching for someone... and hid from her. Stealth 19. She never knew. More on her later.

Reunion at the church! "Did you drink it?" — "No." — "Oh no, he's gonna die." 😅 Barely time for introductions before every raven in the rafters screamed and the doors came off their hinges. Ghouls, dire wolves the size of ponies, an archer sniping from the graveyard, and a GHOST straight through the wall. Highlights:

⚔️ Jasper's "you shall go no further" doorline, ghoul-eviscerating rapier work — and later throwing a holy water bottle in the air and putting a dagger through it mid-flight to splash-damage the whole doorway 🤌
🧹 Dallas misty-stepping onto the altar like a tiny wrathful god, fire-bolting the ghost into vapour... then attempting a heroic leap to the rafters (nat 4, floor, 7 fall damage, dignity check failed) before remembering he owns a flying broom
✨ Lykta going full glowing constellation, healing everyone back from the brink, tanking a mind flayer's tentacles and PUNCHING her way out of its grapple
🗼 Lucian holding the bell tower solo all night, eldritch fire strobing across the graveyard and a running commentary yelled down through the floorboards

The party even got a breather and used it like veterans — barricading every window but ONE, funnelling the horde into a kill-box of their own choosing. Big brain plays. 🧠

Then the second assault brought something *wrong* — a floating, tentacled thing wearing a dead man's coat. Its big psychic scream hit the whole party at once... and everyone saved. 21, 17, 19. We broke its brain before it broke ours. Jasper carved the tentacles off Lykta and put the thing down for good, and the church HELD. 💪

But the mysteries are stacking up: a note on the creature's body in an elegant hand, signed "— L.". Prison tattoos on the vampire's arms. Wolves that were definitely — Arcana 25 definitely — being *controlled* by someone. And out in the dark, TWO armies fighting each other, with the church just caught in the middle. Whose side is anyone actually on? 🤔

And at dawn, a hammering at the barricaded door: "Hello? Is everyone all right? My uncle's still in there!" To which Dallas, our 3-foot gnome, bellowed back "I'm a fully armed barbarian warrior!" in his most intimidating voice. It fooled no one. 😂 The visitor: Sef, age 15. Also, Jasper: that girl you hid from in the woods? Yeah. Her. She'd been out searching for YOU the whole time.

📈 LEVEL 7 and a long rest banked. Next time: the morning after, the old churchyard where the crests might hold answers, the crypts where the enemy retreated... and whatever "— L." has *nearly finished* down there. 🕯️
