# Session 3 — WhatsApp Recap (player-facing)
*Copy-paste below the line straight into the group chat.*

---

🧛 **BAD BLOOD — Session 3: Bad Blood, Finally Spilled** 🩸

Last night in Barovia... you killed her. **All four of you still standing.** And then you decided to stay. Let's talk about how.

We opened exactly where we left off: the Warden coming down the chains, you at the great door. Thirty seconds on the clock. You spent them on **a floor covered in ball bearings** and **pressing the button anyway.** 🔘

The doors ground open for ten glorious seconds, long enough to see a throne, a woman on it, four tubes with things floating in them, and an ENORMOUS half-amber sarcophagus — at which point the collective party response was *"I'm tempted to close the door again."* You did. And from the far end of the room, somebody **laughed.** 😅

Then the Warden arrived, and honestly? It was rude. Faerie Fire blinded him, the ball bearings put him flat on his back (DC 10 Dex, he rolled an 8, *"he's not the most Dexy"*), Jasper made three copies of himself going *"come out, come out, come out, come on"* 🗡️🗡️🗡️, and Lucian's Phantasmal Force conjured a monster that wasn't there so he spent the fight swinging at something imaginary. Starry Wisps rained radiant, Lucian's blasts knocked him back into the ball bearings TWICE, and Jasper closed it out with 43 points of green-flame in a single turn. The scariest thing in the valley landed **one hit** all fight. 🪦

Loot off the corpse: a rune blade bigger than Jasper, and a **second signet ring** identical to the one you've got.

Then you went to meet the lady who laughed.

**Her name is Lyssa.** 🕯️ Warm. Delighted to meet you. *"Assuming you're the ones who've been holding out at the church."* Lucian led with "you come here often" and she took it in her stride, because she also clocked him instantly — *"You're of my line, aren't you?"* — *"As am I."* 🩸

And then, the good bit: **you interrogated her properly.** Not "roll to persuade" — actual questions. Will the villagers be safer? Do you feed on people? Can you send us home? And she answered honestly enough to be terrifying: *"I don't know if I can free people from this land, but if it were not under his control, things would be better."* / *"There is gadgetry."* / **"Honestly? I don't know. But when I have control of this place, maybe then I have that power. Maybe."** Insight 28: she isn't sure her own plan works. She's just giving it a damn good go. 🎲

The four tubes? **Vampire mind flayers.** Ready to go. One of them is the thing that hit the church. Her exact words on the leash: *"It's not perfect control. Apologies. I do send them through."* 🫙🦑

And then the big amber sarcophagus behind her whispered **"we are saved,"** and she cheerfully admitted she has no idea what's in it. *"It's a dark power. I do not understand everything about it yet, but I will."* Cool. Normal. Fine. 🟡

At which point **Jasper** looked at the offer of swapping one vampire dictator for another, said *"that's not really a winning situation one way or the other,"* and stabbed her. **Absolutely correct. No notes.** ⚔️

WHAT FOLLOWED WAS AN HOUR OF CARNAGE.

All four tubes cracked. Mind flayers on the ceiling, floating, grabbing people with tentacles and DRINKING THEIR MINDS — 25, then 22, then 19 psychic damage, each one costing a level of exhaustion and a permanent chunk of maximum HP. 🧠 Lykta rolled a **natural 1** on a save and spent most of the fight grappled by something eating her brain, and never once stopped acting. Legend.

Meanwhile Jasper, hasted, rolled a **NAT 20** and put **128 damage** into Lyssa. *"Just carving into her like Zorro."* 🤺 She tried to slip into the shadows and vanish — until Dallas dropped a Fireball on the spot she'd disappeared into (29 damage 🔥) and she reappeared, robes burning, absolutely furious. Lucian misty-stepped to her and **turned her into ash.** 💨

...and then the ash MOVED. Crawled behind the sarcophagus. Into a gap. Down. Because of course it did.

And then the moment of the whole arc. **Lykta — bloodied, exhausted, half-drained — staggered to the amber sarcophagus and put her bleeding hand on it.** And a voice said:

**"I'm so glad I saved you."** ✨

Silver light forty feet high. The room exploded (21 force damage, everybody duck). Shadows rose up and tore the last of the mind flayers apart. And whatever was inside that amber — something **older than everything else down there, ancient, from before any of this** — poured itself **into Lykta**, who dropped unconscious, glowing green. 🟢

Nobody knows what it was. Including her. **Enjoy that.** 😊

Then, in cold blood and with beautiful competence, you finished the job: nat 20 on the vampire lore check (radiant or holy water, in the coffin, in mist form), bat familiar through the gap, blasted the casket lid off from range, and **Lykta put a Starry Wisp straight into the ash.** 🌟

**Lyssa von Zarovich is gone. Properly. For good.** 🧛‍♀️❌

And then, during your short rest, a man came down the shaft. Smiling. Dry. *"My name is **Ernst.** I have been sent to offer you passage from this place."* And he had a letter, and the seal on it matched the seal on the invitation that dragged you into this valley in the first place. 📜

> *"You have done me a service. My ambitious cousin and her borrowed horrors will trouble my valley no longer — and I did not have to so much as lift my hand. I had only to **open the right door and let the right strangers wander through.** You were magnificent. You were exactly as useful as I had hoped."*

Yeah. **You were hired.** From the very first night. The letter, the road, the Mists, the whole thing — a Count too proud to raise a hand against family and too careful to be seen trying, who fetched four strangers to do his pest control and never told them. 🏰

His terms for letting you go:

🚫 **Do not return** — the Mists know your scent now, and they are not always mine.
🚫 **Do not speak my name where the fog can hear it** — it carries.
🚫 **Do not mistake an open door for a friend.**

*"Should we meet again — and we may, for I am patient — you have proven interesting."*

**AND THEN YOU DIDN'T TAKE IT.** 😳🚪

Offered the only exit from Barovia that anyone has ever been offered, by the man who owns the place, you looked at it and said: not yet. Lykta wants the hole the mind flayers ate in her filled back in first — *"I feel like I've lost all of my income"* — and the rest of you would quite like to be paid. So the door is standing open, the messenger has gone, and **all four of you are still down a hole in Barovia.** 🕳️

Which means the story is not over. Which means, somewhere up a mountain, a very patient man has just been told *no* by four people he'd already finished thinking about. 🏰🕯️

For the record: you were supposed to die down there. You didn't. All four of you walked out of that fight, killed a von Zarovich properly and permanently, let something ancient out of a box, and then declined the Devil of Barovia's one and only courtesy to his face.

**You're Level 8. See you in the valley.** 📈🖤
