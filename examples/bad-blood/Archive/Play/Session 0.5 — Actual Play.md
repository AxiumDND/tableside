# Session 0.5 — Actual Play Record
*What actually happened at the table — 29 June 2026. Three players: Glen (Dallas), Joe (Lucian), Nat (Lykta). David absent.*

> [!warning] Deviations from the run guide
> - The Harbinger was **not** driven off by Urwin's Dawn-light — the party climbed the gate to high ground and it simply **withdrew into the mist on its own**, barely damaged. Urwin arrived *after* the combat ended.
> - After meeting Urwin, the party went **straight to the church** rather than the Blood of the Vine Tavern. They never approached the tavern. Urwin mentioned it but did not push; the party followed the more immediate hook (people at the church, Harbinger harassing the village).
> - Session ends mid-siege setup at the church, not on the tavern doorstep.

---

## Part 1 — Session Zero (~70 min)

### Character setup & equipment
The session opened with the players finalising equipment on D&D Beyond. Key decisions:
- **Lykta** bought basic poison and extra potions — fits her herbalism/foraging identity. Spent time explaining her basket of ingredients to the group.
- **Lucian** confirmed disguise kit + forgery kit from Charlatan background.
- **Dallas** noted his broom can carry 400 lb (two gnomes' worth of cargo became a brief running joke).
- Group agreed spells and abilities are flexible on this first session; DM hand-waved components where sensible.

### Motivations established
| Character              | Motivation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Notes                                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lucian Radu**        | Find the vampire who fathered him — to understand it, and possibly kill it. The letter interests him because it *links to where he thinks his father came from.*                                                                             | Immediately framed everything in terms of money and contracts.                            |
| **Dallas Hinterfield** | 350 years teaching magic to villagers — he's never actually adventured. Decided it was time. Five wizard/sorcerer siblings; youngest sister blends tinkering with spellcraft.                                                                | "Had some sort of psychotic break," in his player's words. Money's also handy.            |
| **Lykta Endrino**      | Turned by a vampire she thought was a psychic (both involve reading patterns — stars/palms). During the turning, *whispers entered her head.* Trying to reconcile the voices and who she is. Money is a means to buy specialist ingredients. | Offered DM a hook: happy for her vampire-turner or a related figure to appear in the arc. |

### Name etymology (Nat shared at the table)
- **Lykta** = "lantern" in Swedish
- **Endrino** = "black spots" in Spanish
- She chose Small size for her Dhampir; described as pale, pointed ears, unusual bright violet eyes, dark hair, fitted deep blues with silver trim.

### Party bonds
The three PCs have been travelling together for a while; silences are comfortable. No friction, shared history implied. Dallas has been "babbling about arcane theory for twenty minutes" as the campfire scene begins — presented as normal. Lucian will cook (prefers raw meat, as a dhampir).

The **fourth chair** (David's PC) was left deliberately empty — "David scouted ahead and hasn't come back." No explanation pushed yet.

### The letter & the dark wine
- The party had received a letter before the campaign opened — an anonymous commission that put them on this road.
- It enclosed **a small, very dark bottle of blood-red wine.** Very old vintage, label almost worn off.
- Lucian suspicious from the start; Lykta used Augury (divination cards) to check. The wine *had* changed somehow since the location shift (confirmed by card reading).
- DM noted Lucian's patron hook is "possibly new" and "may become clear by the end of the session."

## Part 2 — The Last Camp & The Mists (~25 min)

- Lucian took **first watch.**
- DC 13 Wisdom save — rolled **17**, succeeded. He stayed lucid as the mist rolled in.
- Within an hour: fog built until visibility was ~2 feet. Trees had changed — *not the same trees.* Different climate entirely; drab pines, not what surrounded them before.
- Woke the others.

**Investigating the mist:**
- Lucian: circled the camp, checked tracks — old path visible, nothing else.
- Dallas: cast **Light** into the fog — rolled 18 perception. The fog almost *recoiled* from the light; he could swear he saw faces moving away. No stars at all above.
- Lykta: used **divination cards (Augury)** — asked whether following the path opening in the mist was wise. Result: *yes.* Also noted the wine had changed (was deep red; now utterly black, like treacle).

**Drinking the wine:**
- Poured into camp cups. Flavour: ash and treacle, very sweet and sickly.
- Lykta left **a vial by David's pack** with a note: *"If we are not here, drink it and follow."*
- After drinking: the fog began to thin in one direction. An old path opened ahead — signs of old travel. The party followed it northwest.

---

## Part 3 — The Gate (~15 min)

After walking through the mist, the path widened and the fog thinned to reveal:
- A **massive iron trellis-work gate** with two **enormous ancient statues** either side — heavily worn, humanoid/elven-looking, symbols of bygone kings barely legible.
- History check: too worn and obscure to identify with certainty.

Lucian began climbing the wall (~30 ft up). Lykta cast **Guidance** on him.

---

## Part 4 — The Harbinger Fight (~30 min)

The Harbinger emerged from the mist as Lucian climbed — 2-3× PC height, dragging its runed sword with the characteristic grinding noise.

**Initiative:** Lucian 25 (Alert), others ~17–22.

| Turn | What happened |
|---|---|
| **Lucian** | **Form of Dread** (bonus action). **Eldritch Blast** — hit AC with 20, force damage, **Repelling Blast** pushed the creature back. Frightful Avatar triggered: DC 15 Wis save. Did not frighten it. |
| **Dallas** | Backed up toward the gate. **Magic Missile** — three darts, ~12 total damage. |
| **Lykta** | Studied the rune-symbols on the creature's sword — close to but *not identical* to the wax seal on the letter. Same runic family tree. Held up the letter: *"Are you here to show us the way?"* The creature ignored this and attacked. Took two hits — 14 slashing + necrotic damage (necrotic resisted). |
| **Round 2+** | Lucian dealt **radiant damage** (Starry Wisp) — the creature flinched visibly. Dallas cast **Haste** on himself, hopped on the broom, flew up and over the top of the gate. Lykta punched with **Eldritch Claw Tattoo** (magic unarmed, rolled 30) — then **Patient Defense** bonus action to disengage, then **Spider Climbed** the gate/statue to reach high ground. |

**Outcome:** With all three PCs on or above the gate out of its reach, the Harbinger **withdrew back into the mist on its own — barely damaged, still very much alive.** It did not speak. It just stopped, looked up at them, and stepped back into the fog.

> [!gmonly] Note for future
> The Harbinger is at near full HP. It has clocked the party and will return. The players don't know this yet — they may think they "scared it off." Use that confidence against them.

**Radiant worked.** When Lucian hit it with Starry Wisp (radiant) the runes guttered visibly. This was noticed. The lesson landed.

**Lykta's Whispers:** reaching out during the fight, she heard *nothing* — a soul-shaped hole where a presence should be. Rattled her more than the sword did.

## Part 5 — Urwin Arrives & The Hook (~20 min)

From the top of the gate, Lucian spotted (good perception):
- The **castle** visible in the far distance (Ravenloft).
- A **village** about a mile up the path.
- A **human-sized figure** walking from the village toward the gate.

The party waited. Lucian climbed down when the figure was ~30 feet away.

**Urwin Martikov** — introduced plainly. Large moustache, dark eyes, travel-worn clothes. Had been praying for days for someone to arrive. He confirms:

- They are in **Barovia**, within the Mists, domain of "the Lord."
- **Warning:** distance or planar-crossing spells — "he" will be listening. (Lykta had already tried Message to David; got nothing.)
- The **Blood of the Vine Tavern**: three sisters ran it. Two weeks ago went silent. Anyone who goes in disappears.
- The **Harbinger** (he calls it the Tomb-Warden) has been harassing the outskirts.
- People have taken refuge in the **church.**

**Lucian's immediate move:** proposed a contract — "per person per hour, plus damages." Urwin accepted in principle. Lucian drew up paperwork.

**Walking to the village:** Urwin explained conditions. No sun — always gray and overcast. A valley. Three villages. Some trade still exists. Hard life, scratching out food without sunlight.

---

## Part 6 — The Church (~25 min)

The party arrived at the **village of Barovia** — 4-5 streets deep, some lights, smoke from chimneys. People inside, not showing their faces.

Urwin mentioned people sheltering at the church. The party went there **rather than the Blood of the Vine Tavern.**

**Lykta tried to read the church** (Guidance + perception of its spiritual nature) — was overwhelmed by voices, failed the check. Did identify: the symbol on the church is the **symbol of dawn/sun/light**, but a very old and unusual version of it.

### Inside the church

- ~10 adults upstairs, sitting on broken/shattered pews. A small fire/candle blaze in the middle.
- **Father Donovich** — very pale, white-haired, in priestly robes — giving a quiet sermon at the front. Tired, stubborn, genuinely faithful.
- Echoes from **below** — a crypt or undercroft. Cries of children.
- Below: **4 families with young children**, sheltering in the basement.
- The children were disturbing: pale, gothic clothing, "not enough life in them" — not undead, but drained of something. Two children were lost three days ago and buried.
- Father Donovich confirmed: wolves got into the church a few nights back. He's holding on.

**Lykta's response:** went to one of the children, conjured a small light, handed over what was left of the letter's seal as something to hold. Some children responded; others couldn't.

**Goodberry:** Lykta made Goodberries and handed them to Father Donovich to distribute. He was genuinely, tearfully grateful.

**Urwin** quietly helped downstairs — bandaging the injured, keeping people calm.

**By the altar:** something at the altar caught attention (faint, hard to pin down — possibly a relic or item of interest for next session).

---

## Part 7 — The Siege Begins (end of session)

Lykta sent out her familiar/used Wildshape to scout the perimeter and reported back:

> The mist is in rings around the outside. There are figures — many of them — at the edges of the cemetery. Wolves. And something else. They are coming from all directions.

Urwin spoke quietly to a raven. Ravens flew off into the dark.

Father Donovich: *"Prepare yourselves. I do not know what will happen."*

**Session ends** with the party climbing to the **bell tower** to look out. From there, they can see:
- Undead and wolves massing at the cemetery edges, beginning to move toward the church.
- A lone figure running through the mist toward the church gate — **David's PC**, stumbling out of the Mist to rejoin them.

*Cut to black.*
