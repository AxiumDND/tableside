🧛 **BAD BLOOD — Session 2: Welcome Home** 🚪

Last night in Barovia...

First revelation of the evening: on the night of the siege, Lykta had quietly sent a POLITE LETTER to Lord Zarovich by familiar-mail. 📜 "Should conflict be in your interest, I shall keep an ear out for your reply." Our spirit medium, writing directly to the Devil of Barovia to offer her services as a freelance informant. No reply has come. *Yet.* 👀

Morning after the siege: the battlefield told its story, and Jasper broke out parchment and chalk for a genuine gravestone rubbing 🪦 — History 19 — and cracked it: the crest from your mysterious letter matches the graves by the old crypt, and the worn name on the stone is an OLD form of "Zarovich." Not the Count's line. An older branch. The plot, like the graves, deepens.

Then: the crypt door. A mausoleum wrapped in thorns — half carved in the stone, half alive — with a chalice basin and an inscription nobody liked: *"THE TREE DRINKS ONLY OF ITS OWN. KNEEL, KIN, AND BE KNOWN. ALL ELSE FEEDS THE ROOTS."* 🌹

The science phase was glorious. Vampire-spawn blood fetched in a vial and poured in by Mage Hand — which came out SKELETAL, by the way, because apparently Barovia has opinions about Dallas's magic now ("Honey, I don't think we're in Kansas anymore" 💀✋). Result: nothing. Wrong blood. But the thorns kept turning toward certain party members like flowers following the sun...

So Lucian knelt, and gave the door his hand. The thorns drank DEEP. The chalice glowed. The door sank into the earth. And a voice in his head said: **"WELCOME HOME, BLOOD OF THE LINE."** 🩸 Lucian, out loud, to the group: "...maybe that's my mother's side. I never knew my dad." SIR. WE NEED TO TALK ABOUT YOUR FAMILY.

Below: a crypt being ERASED. Every nameplate chiselled blank, the sun-ward gouged out, swept paths that carefully avoid stepping on the holy symbol. Dallas (Arcana 27!) worked out someone is deliberately de-consecrating the place so the dead can rise. Also found: urns full of people-ash, black defaced fonts — one of which Jasper resurrected with holy water and bottled THREE fresh vials from 💦 ... and then Lucian — half-vampire, recently welcomed home by the family door — took a taste. Reviews: horrible. Would not recommend. One star. 🌟

Then it got dark. A corridor of bodies cut clean in half — ONE stroke each, cauterised black, with a groove in the stone the width of a dragged greatsword. You know the one. 😱 Lucian cast Speak with Dead and the corpse gave up the goods: *"We were sent... by the master of these lands... to kill his enemies."* So the two armies at the church? Increasingly looking like TWO BRANCHES OF ONE FAMILY having the worst Christmas ever. 🎄

Loot haul of dreams: a silver funeral chime that makes lesser undead flinch 🔔, a creepy embalmer's lens (maker's mark: an elegant hand... and an "L" 👁️), two scrolls, a trapped door disarmed off a NAT 20, and — found glowing on a dead man's finger — a blackened SIGNET RING carved with the family tree. Cold to the touch. For most people, anyway. 💍

Also: tables with RESTRAINTS, and jars of brine that once held something small and alive. Two jars empty. "The new servants take the change well..." Yeah. Sleep tight. 🫙

Crossing a collapsed span over a bottomless drop, the whispers found Lykta: *"Come down to us."* She told them to come UP instead. Negotiations ongoing. 😤

And THEN the doors opened on a wight and a room full of ghouls — and Dallas, lowering slowly through the doorway on his broomstick with a fireball swelling in his hand like the world's smallest, angriest thunderhead, deleted the ENTIRE ROOM in one cast. 36 damage. 🔥 "There's no such thing as overkill when it's life or death." Lykta nat-20 dropkicked a ghoul off the ceiling, Lucian's eldritch blasts finished the archer that's been dogging you since the church, and Jasper set the last ghoul on fire with his sword. Two rounds. One crater.

Then the geography got WORSE. Through a crack in a door: a great rotunda with a raised dais — and a toppled statue of an armoured man with an enormous stone sword. You know that sword. 🗡️ The dais? Covered in pressure plates. A MECHANISM. Dallas flew up the light-shaft in the dome and popped out at the surface behind the treeline — and the penny dropped: this is exactly where those forces rose up that ended the siege. The dais is an ELEVATOR. This is how they come and go. 🛗💀

Beyond a second disarmed trap (thank you, nat 20) and two very dormant-looking statue guards: a freshly dug hole dropping 70 feet into a black cavern. A coin with Light on it went down... and lit up the remains of a stairway and a VERY large door. The whispers that wanted Lykta to "come down to us"? They're on the other side of that door. Everyone climbed down. Found the door's opening mechanism. "It's a button. I like pressing buttons." 🔘

And then, from above: a THUMP. Chains rattling. And the dais began to descend into the shaft — with something standing on it. Something big. Something you last saw at the gates of Barovia, dragging a sword that screams. It was halfway down when we cut. ⚔️😱

Next time: he's coming down. The button is right there. The voices are waiting. Roll initiative. 🎲
