# Session 2 — "The Tomb-Warden's Due"
> _The morning after, the shattered grave, the upper catacombs, and the rematch. Read **from** this at the table. Stat blocks embedded._
> *Rewritten 15 July 2026 to match [[Session 1 — Actual Play]] — the previous version (which assumed S1 ended at the grave's lip) is in `_archive/`.*

> [!tip] The shape of tonight
> **Pick up on the morning after the siege — in the church, not at the grave.** S1 ended at the long rest; none of the morning business has happened yet. Tonight carries it all: **Larnak's contract, the Shard changing hands, Donovich's crest tour, Sef and Jasper's reunion** — then out past the east wall to the old quarter, where they find the **shattered grave** and, standing at its lip in the first grey light, **Lyssa von Zarovich herself.** She invites them down, and goes first. The catacombs below are her dig from the inside — silent, already visited by something worse — and the **Harbinger rematch** in the rotunda is the night's spine. It ends at the **broken seal**: green candlelight far below, and a voice. The descent is Session 3.
>
> **Arc note (15 July 2026):** three-session plan, middle session. **All five PCs — Joe is back.** Wave 2 of the siege never ran, so the party is fresh: long-rested, Level 7, unbloodied. Don't be gentle.

> [!warning] Must-land beats — everything else is cuttable
> 1. **The Shard changes hands** (Donovich → Jasper) before they go underground. Non-negotiable; the whole rematch is keyed to it.
> 2. **Larnak's contract** — 2,500 gp, *"discreet."* Plant his face; they should distrust him.
> 3. **Lyssa is finally seen** — the shattered-grave cameo. She has never appeared in person; fix that tonight.
> 4. **The Harbinger rematch** — the fight designed to be won.
> 5. **Level 8 + Boost Kit** at the close.

> [!gmonly] Why the Harbinger is here — hold this in your head
> It is a **Tomb-Warden**. Lyssa's dig is the greatest desecration in the valley, and the party (Shard in hand, standing among robbed graves) are exactly what it exists to punish. It ignores Lyssa's war entirely — it has *already been through* the catacombs tonight, and what it did to her dig crew is the party's first warning. The party may believe they scared it off at the gate (0.5 — it withdrew barely scratched). **Use that confidence.** Tonight they finally have the tools to end it: **radiant, the [[Ravenkind Shard]], Level 7, and five of them.** See [[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade]].

# MOVEMENT 1 — The Morning After *(15 minutes)*

▸ **Goal:** re-plant them in the church, give Joe the first word, and count the cost of the night.

📖
> Morning comes to Barovia the way everything else does — grudgingly. A brighter grey through the shutters, bells with no sun to ring for. The nave smells of tallow, blood, and pew-varnish burned by holy water. Below the floor, a child is laughing at something — actually laughing — and half the room flinches at the sound before remembering it's allowed.

🎲 **Lucian's debrief — give Joe the first word.** He held the tower alone all night; the table only knows what he shouted down. Ask him: *"You watched the whole war from up there. What kept you awake?"* Then confirm what he saw, in his voice or yours:
- The two forces **fought each other** all night; the church was just in the way.
- The survivors **retreated north** at the end — toward the old crypts at the far end of the churchyard. *"One side is winning,"* he said. He still thinks so.
- The **wight archer** never came back. It took a hit from something out in the dark and limped off. *(Loose thread — see Movement 3.)*

🎲 **The cost, in small beats** *(pick what the table reaches for)*:
- The stabilised defender is awake, arm in a sling, embarrassingly grateful.
- The children below are *slightly* brighter — a few of them. Not all.
- Donovich has not slept. He is re-consecrating the doorway with a thumb of oil, one word at a time.

⏭ **Breakfast** — such as it is. Movement 2 comes to them.

# MOVEMENT 2 — The Morning's Business *(the church — 30 minutes)*

▸ **Goal:** three scenes over breakfast: Sef & Jasper, Larnak's contract, the Shard. Run them in whatever order the table walks into; end with the Shard.

## 2a — Sef & Jasper: "You hid from ME?"

🎲 Sef has appointed herself the party's shadow this morning — fetching, carrying, listening at exactly the wrong moments. She is **fascinated** by them, Jasper especially. Play the double-act the table already set up:
- *"Three days. THREE DAYS I was out there looking for you, and you were in a bush?"*
- She presses a **raven-feather charm** into Jasper's hand: *"Uncle says it's luck. He lies. It's so I can find you next time you hide."*
- She knows the churchyard's old quarter — played there as a child, dared each other to touch the leaning stones. She can describe the roofless chapel and the black yews *before* they see them. **Urwin forbids her past the east wall,** flatly, in front of everyone. She goes quiet in a way that means nothing of the sort.

> [!gmonly] Sef tonight
> She does **not** come down the stairs. If the players try to bring her, Urwin ends it with a look. But she's available as a runner/lookout at the east wall — and if you need a scare later, a raven with *her* charm's twin lands at the grave's lip mid-crawl. Don't spend her; she's S3 colour and sequel material.

## 2b — Ernst Larnak: the contract

🖼 ![[Ernst Larnak.png|200]]

📖
> He comes in with the morning bread, which is the first wrong thing, because nobody saw him on the road. Trim, well-kept, dry — *dry*, in this valley — with a wide-brimmed hat and a smile that arrives a half-second before the rest of his face. He looks at the scorch-marks, the stacked bodies outside, the barricade scars, and says, delighted: "Oh, *good*. Capable people."

🎲 **The offer** ([[Ernst Larnak]]): he represents *"certain families of standing"* troubled by desecrations in the old churchyard. Someone has been **digging**. He'd like it stopped. **2,500 gold pieces** on completion. *"I do hope you'll be discreet."*
- **He names the old churchyard unprompted** — the same place the "— L." note names. Let the table clock it. If asked how he knows: *"One hears things. It's a small valley. Sound carries."* He never answers a direct question; he flatters instead.
- **Lucian is the contract man** — let Joe drive the negotiation. Larnak agrees to reasonable riders with suspicious ease. He is *dry in the rain* levels of wrong. **Insight DC 13:** he's not lying about the job. He's lying about something.
- If pressed hard he simply leaves: *"Someone really ought to look into it. Someone capable."* The job stands.

> [!gmonly]
> He's **Strahd's spy** — the tip comes from the Count's servants; the job is to aim the party at Lyssa. Never show it. Plant the face, take the distrust, move on. He does not fight (Spy, CR 1) — he flees into fog.

## 2c — The Shard changes hands ★

📖
> Father Donovich waits until the fixer is gone. Then he beckons you to the altar — to the thing that's been catching your eye since the first night — and works loose a stone that was never mortared. Inside, wrapped in twenty years of altar-cloth: a finger-length splinter of pale stone, warm as a sunlit sill, a raven and a rayed sun worked into its face. It is the only warm thing in Barovia.
> "I have kept it since I was young enough to believe keeping was enough," he says. "Last night I watched you *spend* yourselves for people you'd never met." He presses it into your hands. "The dawn goes with you. In whatever form it can."

🎲 **[[Ravenkind Shard]]** — he gives it to **Jasper** (the man who held the door) unless the table redirects it. **Attunes on touch** (twenty years of a priest's prayer). Read the abilities aloud — Dawnlight 1/day (sunlight, 20-ft), +1d6 Radiant vs undead (3/rest), advantage vs undead fright.
- **Urwin, from across the nave, knows it on sight:** *"A splinter of the Dawn. The Count hates these. Keep it close."*
- Donovich also hands over **2 flasks of holy water** — Jasper's down to his last after the trick shot.

🔀 **Small colour, if Jasper produces the wine vial** *(still in his pack)*: held near the Shard, the black wine **stirs** — a slow turn, like something rolling over in its sleep — and the Shard's hum flattens. Same family of workings, opposite ends of it. Say no more. *(Clue-chain whisper; detonates in S3.)*

⏭ **Donovich promised Lykta a crest tour.** The graveyard is waiting, and so is the job.

# MOVEMENT 3 — The Crest Tour & the East Wall *(the graveyard — 25 minutes)*

▸ **Goal:** the promised morning investigation — battle-signs, the retreat trail, the crest payoff — ending at the east wall with Urwin's counsel. Dread up, weapons away: nothing attacks up here.

📖
> The graveyard by daylight is an argument that nobody won. Headstones toppled, turf ripped in long claw-furrows, black feathers and blacker ichor freezing into the mud. The bodies you dragged out are where you left them. The bodies you *didn't* kill — the other faction's dead — are simply gone.

🎲 **Investigation** (let them work; feed by check):
- **DC 12 Investigation — battle-signs:** two engagement lines, neither centred on the church. The church was an *obstacle* in someone else's battlefield. The heavier force gave ground all night, north.
- **DC 15 Survival — wolf tracks:** the wolf pack came, fought, and left *in formation* — no scatter, no scavenging. Dominated, coming and going. (Confirms the Arcana 25 read; reward the Strahd-controls-wolves theory.)
- **DC 15 Perception — the wight's trail:** black-fletched arrows, and a scorch-line where something hit it hard. A dragging footprint trail toward the tree line, then nothing. **It's still out there, wounded.** *(Loose thread — leave it hanging; do not spend it tonight.)*
- **The retreat trail** *(free, once they look north)*: everything that survived went the same way — through the north gate of the churchyard, into the **old quarter.**

🎲 **★ Donovich's crest payoff ★** — walking the family plots (his promised tour), he stops at a leaning row of stones. Last night's nat-1 becomes this morning's grace:
> "Forgive me — last night, by candlelight, I could not place it. In daylight there is no mistaking." *(his thumb traces the worn crest — then holds it beside the seal from the party's letter)* "**These are von Zarovich graves.** Cadet lines. Cousins. The family's quiet dead." *(a pause)* "The crest on your letter is the same tree, my friends. A different branch."
- This **confirms the two-bloodlines theory at last** — they built it themselves across two sessions; hand them the win. What he *cannot* explain: why half these stones look recently disturbed.

> [!gmonly] Seed the Harbinger
> Somewhere in this movement, far off toward the old quarter: **stone-on-stone. A blade, dragging. Circling.** One beat, then gone. Don't comment. They know that sound — and they think they scared it off once.

🎲 **The east wall — Urwin's counsel.** He walks them to the north gate and no further. Quiet, plain, certain:
> "The old quarter's been closed longer than I've been alive. Whatever she's dug up down there, it was buried by people who knew more than us." *(a look at the Shard)* "Take the Dawn. And come back."

⏭ **Through the gate.** The old quarter is right there.

# MOVEMENT 4 — The Shattered Grave & the Lady *(20 minutes)*

▸ **Goal:** the dig site investigation — and Lyssa's first appearance in the flesh. She has been a hand, a letter, an elegant script. Tonight she gets a face, and then she gets to leave.

📖
> Past the north gate the graveyard gets older. Headstones lean at drunken angles under black dripping yews; a roofless chapel stands hip-deep in brambles, its carvings weathered to whispers. And in the middle of it: **industry.** Churned mud. A hand-cart. Tools stacked neat as surgery. One grave stands open — its stone slab not lifted but **shattered from beneath**, and stone steps descend into a cold that has nothing to do with the morning.

🎲 **Investigation of the site** (feed by check):
- **DC 10 Investigation:** the diggers worked *down to meet something*, then the slab burst *up*. Two directions of work meeting in the middle.
- **DC 12 Medicine / Investigation** on three corpses half-buried in the spoil heap: worked to death and then *dry* — bloodless. And on their arms: **crude number sequences and gang marks.** The same prisoner tattoos as the spawn from the siege. (Reward the theory: *her servants are made from prisoners* — and so was her labour force.)
- **DC 12 Religion** at the roofless chapel: the lintel carving is the **raven-and-sun** — the same mark as the Shard — with every raven's head **chiselled off.** Recent, methodical work.
- **DC 15 Perception:** among the tool-marks, one set of prints that is *wrong* — barefoot, too long, walking weight with no stride variation. It has walked this yard **many times.** The most recent set goes **down the stairs.**

## ★ The Lady at the Grave ★

🖼 ![[Lyssa von Zarovich.png|300]]

📖 *(when the investigation winds down — or the moment someone starts down the steps)*
> She is standing among the yews as though she has been there the whole time, and perhaps she has: a pale woman in old, fine clothes, dressed for a better century, watching you work with the particular patience of someone who owns the clock. When you notice her, she smiles like a hostess.
> "The church still standing. The old warden shrugged off. And now here you are, reading my little mess like a ledger." A small, genuine incline of the head. "You held up *far* better than the last strangers. I confess I'm rather pleased with you."

🎲 **Run the cameo, not a fight** ([[Lyssa von Zarovich]]):
- She confirms, airily, what they've earned: the siege was **hers** — *"a field test, and you were a marvellous control group"* — and the digging is hers too. She doesn't apologise; it doesn't occur to her.
- **The Lucian beat:** her eyes find him and *linger* — a slow, curious tilt of the head, the way one looks at a familiar face in a stranger's portrait. She says **nothing about it.** Give Joe the moment; let him decide what it feels like. *(Do NOT play the "his dogs have a certain smell" line or the name-offer — those are Session 3's. Tonight is only the look.)*
- If attacked: she becomes **mist before the first blow lands** — no initiative, no fight. *"Manners."*
- **Her exit, and the invitation:** she steps to the grave's lip and descends into the dark without a torch, her voice drifting back up:
> "Do come down when you're done up here. I'd say mind the stairs — but it isn't the stairs that want you punished."

> [!gmonly]
> She is *baiting them into the Trap* (S3) — that's the whole cameo. She knows the Tomb-Warden is between them and her, and she is entirely happy for it to soften them up. If the players smell a trap: **good.** They're right, and they'll go anyway — Larnak's gold, the "— L." note, and the retreat trail all point the same way.

⏭ **Down the stairs.**

# MOVEMENT 5 — The Upper Catacombs *(the silent crawl — 35 minutes)*

▸ **Goal:** Lyssa's dig from the inside — already visited by something worse. **No fight down here tonight** (cut 15 July — the rematch gets the space). The dread does the work: the dig crew's keepers are dead, and the party is sharing a catacomb with what killed them. Theatre-of-mind, three spaces.

📖 *(the stairs)*
> The steps are older than the chapel above — and the walls change as you go down: fieldstone, then dressed stone, then **bone.** Femurs stacked like cordwood, skulls in niches, all of it furred with dust — except where hands have been. A lot of hands. The dust is wiped in long corridors of work, and lantern-stubs sit in the eye-sockets of skulls like the diggers thought it was funny.

🔀 **The Shard flares** the deeper they go — brighter, warmer, insistent. It *wants* to go down. (Foreshadow, and a free torch.)

## 5a — The Ossuary Gallery *(the warning)*

📖
> The gallery runs long and low, niches floor to ceiling. Halfway down, the bones have been **sorted** — pelvises here, skulls there, long bones bundled with twine like firewood. Something has been *harvesting.* And then you find the harvesters. Four of them — ghouls, by the teeth and the grave-stink — laid out along the corridor where they fell. Each one is in two pieces. **One stroke each.** The cuts are cauterised black, and the bone-dust around them is scuffed by a single set of bare, too-long footprints — and one long, continuous groove in the stone floor, the width of a dragged blade.

🎲 Let them read the room — no checks needed, but reward care:
- The ghouls died **facing it.** They tried to fight. It didn't slow down.
- **DC 12 Arcana / Investigation:** the black cauterised cuts hum faintly of necrotic *and* something older — the same warding-script energy as the runes on the blade they remember from the gate.
- The groove in the floor runs **onward, deeper** — the same direction they're going.
- This is where the table realises what the fight tonight is. Let the silence sit. If someone says *"we should go back,"* that's the room working.

## 5b — The Chapel-Crypt *(the clue room)*

📖
> A vaulted funerary chapel, and it stops you: every name on every tomb has been **chiselled out.** Not weathered — *removed*, methodically, the stone still pale in the cuts. Someone is erasing a family from its own graves. Against the far wall: crates. Packed straw, grave-goods, reliquaries — and pinned to the top crate, a **shipping manifest** in a fine, elegant hand.

🎲 **The manifest** *(hand it over — same hand as the "— L." note in Jasper's notebook; they'll compare instantly)*:
> *"The oldest bones below. Reliquaries and anything with the sun-mark to me, intact. Burn the rest. When the seal is open you will be paid as agreed — and you will forget the stair. — L."*

🎲 **DC 12 History / Arcana** *(easier if Donovich's tour landed — give advantage)*: the defaced tombs are **von Zarovich** — the same cadet lines from the morning's crest tour. She isn't just robbing the yard. She's *editing the family down to two names: hers and his.*

🔀 **Loot:** the crates hold the valuables listed in [[Treasure & Magic Items]] (S2 entry). Take grave-goods here and the Harbinger's claim gets *personal* (see Movement 6 — it's a feature, let them).

## 5c — The Broken Seal *(the way down — don't descend tonight)*

📖
> The catacomb ends at a door that isn't a door: a single slab, twice head-height, carved edge to edge with the raven-and-sun — and it has been **forced.** Not smashed: *unmade* — the wards chiselled out in the same methodical hand as the names, then the slab levered aside with engines. Beyond it, stairs spiral down into air that is colder, older, and faintly green. Far below: candlelight. And something breathing — wet, patient, too many lungs.

🎲 This is the threshold of the **von Zarovich sanctuary** (Session 3). If they start down tonight: the Harbinger arrives *now* — the fight happens on the threshold instead of the rotunda. Otherwise —

⏭ **The grinding starts.** Loud. Above and behind them. It's inside — and it's between them and the way out.

# MOVEMENT 6 — The Tomb-Warden *(the rematch — give it an hour)*

▸ **Goal:** the fight they can finally win. Give it space, give it dread, give the Shard its night.

🖼 ![[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade.png|300]]

📖
> The grinding comes down the ossuary gallery like weather. Bone-dust sifts from the niches with every step. And then it fills the archway: shrouds over dead plate, no face under the cowl, the colossal blade dragging sparks off the floor — and the runes along it are not humming now. They are **screaming.** It has found the desecrated tomb. It has found the thieves standing in it. It does not hurry, because it has never needed to.

🎲 **Arena — the rotunda:** a round ossuary chamber where the dig-shaft breaks through the ceiling: one column of **grey daylight** in the middle of the floor (the diggers' entrance from above). Collapsed niches make difficult terrain; bone-pillars give half cover.
- **The daylight shaft matters:** the Harbinger **will not step into it** (it routes around — telegraph this hard). A PC in the shaft holding the **Shard** can spend an action to **flare the Dawnlight**: DC 15 CON save or the Harbinger is **Blinded until the end of its next turn** (once per fight per PC). The arena teaches the lesson: *light is the answer.*
- **Radiant is the win condition:** Vulnerability to Radiant, and **Guttered Runes** (radiant since its last turn → Disadvantage). Lykta and Lucian's radiant + the Shard = the kill. Say it in fiction: *"your steel rings off it — but where the light lands, the runes go out like drowned coals."*
- **Five PCs at Level 7, fresh off a long rest:** run the base block **+ the 2 Legendary Actions** (Stride / Mist-Step / Reaping — in the statblock note). Drop the legendaries the moment two PCs are down.
- **If they took grave-goods in 5b:** it goes for *that* PC first after the Shard-bearer. Its priorities are legible — that's the horror.
- **Lucian's night to shine:** Joe missed the siege. Form of Dread vs the thing that wouldn't be frightened at the gate, Repelling Blast to keep it off the casters, radiant to gut it — feed him targets.

🔀 **They kill it with radiant:** the runes gutter out one by one — and for one heartbeat, as the last one dies, the script **resolves**: older, cleaner, the same tradition of old-Barovian warding as… *(let Lykta or Dallas make the check — DC 13 Arcana)* …**the wax seal on the letter that brought you here.** The same school. The same age. Say no more. *(This detonates in Session 3 with the Farewell letter.)*
🔀 **★ Lykta's whispers at its death ★:** if she reaches out as it dies — and after the gate, she will — the **soul-shaped hole** is there again… and then, for one breath, it *isn't*. Something passes through where the silence was: not a voice, a **release.** Like a sentry finally relieved of a post. Then nothing. Give Nat that beat unprompted if she doesn't ask.
🔀 **They drop it without radiant:** it collapses into rune-lit slag and Mist — and **re-forms within 24 hours** (Curse-Bound Rejuvenation). It will be waiting outside the sanctuary in Session 3. A fine outcome; they didn't *end* it.
🔀 **It's going badly** *(a PC dying, dice cruel)*: **★ the wereraven reveal fires here ★** — ravens pour down the dig-shaft, and Urwin *unfolds* out of the flock mid-air with two Keepers behind him; dawn-light floods the shaft, the Harbinger recoils and withdraws. The rescue he didn't get to make at the gate. The floor, not the plan.

🎲 They end at the **broken seal**. Green light below. The wet breathing. And then — clear, unhurried, amused, drifting up the spiral stair — **the voice from the graveyard:**

> "You *ended* the old warden. Even *he* never managed that." *(a pause; the breathing below stills, listening)* "I told you to come down when you were done. You're done. And I so rarely get guests worth the candles."
