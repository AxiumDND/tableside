> _The old churchyard, the upper catacombs, and the rematch. Read **from** this at the table. Stat blocks embedded._

> [!tip] The shape of tonight
> **Pick up exactly where S1 cut: standing at the shattered grave, steps going down.** (Larnak's contract and Urwin's counsel already happened in S1's close.) > **Arc note (11 July 2026):** three-session plan — this is the middle session. With the job scene moved to S1, tonight has extra room: spend it on the catacombs crawl and give the Harbinger fight all the space it wants.

> [!gmonly] Why the Harbinger is here — hold this in your head
> It is a **Tomb-Warden**. Lyssa's dig is the greatest desecration in the valley, and the party (Shard in hand, standing among robbed graves) are exactly what it exists to punish. It is not hunting *for* anyone — it ignores Lyssa's war entirely. Tonight the party finally has the tools to end it: **radiant, the [[Ravenkind Shard]], Level 7, and five of them.** This is the fight designed to be won — see [[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade]].

# MOVEMENT 2 — The Shattered Grave

▸ **Goal:** the investigation. Dread up, weapons away — nothing attacks up here. *(They saw this site at S1's cliffhanger — the readaloud below is a reprise to re-set the scene; trim it if the recap already did the work.)*

📖
> Past the east wall the graveyard gets older. Headstones lean at drunken angles under black dripping yews; a roofless chapel stands hip-deep in brambles, its carvings weathered to whispers. And in the middle of it: **industry.** Churned mud. A hand-cart. Tools stacked neat as surgery. One grave stands open — its stone slab not lifted but **shattered from beneath**, and stone steps descend into a cold that has nothing to do with the morning.

🎲 Investigation of the site (let them work; feed by check):
- **DC 10 Investigation:** the diggers worked *down to meet something*, then the slab burst *up*. Two directions of work meeting in the middle.
- **DC 12 Medicine / Investigation** on three corpses half-buried in the spoil heap: villagers — missing weeks. Bloodless. Worked to death *and then dry*. Lyssa's labour force, used up and discarded.
- **DC 12 Religion** at the chapel: the lintel carving is the **raven-and-sun** — the same mark as the Shard — with every raven's head **chiselled off.** Recent work. *(Clue: same hand as the temple corruption — her.)*
- **DC 15 Perception:** among the tool-marks, one set of prints that is *wrong* — barefoot, too long, walking weight with no stride variation. Something dead walks this yard in the dark.

> [!gmonly] Seeds for the Harbinger
> Somewhere in this movement, far off in the Mist at the tree line: **stone-on-stone. A blade, dragging.** One beat, then gone. Don't comment. They know that sound.

⏭ **Down the stairs.**

# MOVEMENT 3 — The Upper Catacombs

▸ **Goal:** a ~45-minute crawl — Lyssa's dig from the inside, one fight, the clue room, the broken seal. Theatre-of-mind, three spaces.

📖 *(the stairs)*
> The steps are older than the chapel above — and the walls change as you go down: fieldstone, then dressed stone, then **bone.** Femurs stacked like cordwood, skulls in niches, all of it furred with dust — except where hands have been. A lot of hands. The dust is wiped in long corridors of work, and lantern-stubs sit in the eye-sockets of skulls like the diggers thought it was funny.

## 3a — The Ossuary Gallery *(the fight)*

📖
> The gallery runs long and low, niches floor to ceiling. Halfway down, the bones have been **sorted** — pelvises here, skulls there, long bones bundled with twine like firewood. Something has been *harvesting.* And then the nearest bundle shifts, and the smell hits you: grave-rot, fresh.

🎲 **4 Ghouls** (the dig crew's keepers — they burst from cleared niches, flanking) **+ 1 Specter** (rises through the floor at the end of round 1, drifts at the casters). Tight quarters: the gallery is 10 ft. wide — Jasper's Sentinel owns this hallway; the ghouls' paralysis is the danger in a corridor where nobody can retreat past each other.

🔀 **The Shard** flares the whole fight — brighter the deeper they go. It *wants* to go down. (Foreshadow, and a free torch.)

## 3b — The Chapel-Crypt *(the clue room)*

📖
> A vaulted funerary chapel, and it stops you: every name on every tomb has been **chiselled out.** Not weathered — *removed*, methodically, the stone still pale in the cuts. Someone is erasing a family from its own graves. Against the far wall: crates. Packed straw, grave-goods, reliquaries — a **shipping manifest** in a fine, elegant hand.

🎲 **The manifest** *(hand it over — same hand as the "— L." orders, they'll clock it instantly)*:
> *"The oldest bones below. Reliquaries and anything with the sun-mark to me, intact. Burn the rest. When the seal is open you will be paid as agreed — and you will forget the stair. — L."*

🎲 **DC 12 History / Arcana:** the defaced tombs are **von Zarovich** — cadet lines, cousins, the family's quiet dead. She isn't just robbing the yard. She's *editing the family down to two names: hers and his.*

🔀 **Loot:** the crates hold the valuables listed in [[Treasure & Magic Items]] (S2 entry) — take gravegoods here and the Harbinger's claim gets *personal* (see below; it's a feature, let them).

## 3c — The Broken Seal *(the way down — don't descend tonight)*

📖
> The catacomb ends at a door that isn't a door: a single slab, twice head-height, carved edge to edge with the raven-and-sun — and it has been **forced.** Not smashed: *unmade* — the wards chiselled out in the same methodical hand as the names, then the slab levered aside with engines. Beyond it, stairs spiral down into air that is colder, older, and faintly green. Far below: candlelight. And something breathing — wet, patient, too many lungs.

🎲 This is the threshold of the **von Zarovich sanctuary** (Session 3). If they start down tonight: the Harbinger arrives *now* — the fight happens on the threshold instead of the rotunda. Otherwise —

⏭ **The grinding starts.** Loud. Above and behind them. It's inside.

# MOVEMENT 4 — The Tomb-Warden *(the rematch)*

▸ **Goal:** the fight they can finally win. Give it space, give it dread, give the Shard its night.

🖼 ![[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade.webp|300]]

📖
> The grinding comes down the ossuary gallery like weather. Bone-dust sifts from the niches with every step. And then it fills the archway: shrouds over dead plate, no face under the cowl, the colossal blade dragging sparks off the floor — and the runes along it are not humming now. They are **screaming.** It has found the desecrated tomb. It has found the thieves standing in it. It does not hurry, because it has never needed to.

🎲 **Arena — the rotunda:** a round ossuary chamber where the dig-shaft breaks through the ceiling: one column of **grey daylight** in the middle of the floor (the diggers' entrance from above). Collapsed niches make difficult terrain; bone-pillars give half cover.
- **The daylight shaft matters:** the Harbinger **will not step into it** (it routes around — telegraph this hard). A PC in the shaft holding the **Shard** can spend an action to **flare the Dawnlight**: DC 15 CON save or the Harbinger is **Blinded until the end of its next turn** (once per fight per PC). The arena teaches the lesson: *light is the answer.*
- **Radiant is the win condition:** Vulnerability to Radiant, and **Guttered Runes** (radiant since its last turn → Disadvantage). Lykta and Lucian's radiant + the Shard = the kill. Say it in fiction: *"your steel rings off it — but where the light lands, the runes go out like drowned coals."*
- **Five PCs at Level 7, fresh-ish:** run the base block **+ the 2 Legendary Actions** (Stride / Mist-Step / Reaping — in the statblock note). Drop the legendaries the moment two PCs are down.
- **If they took grave-goods in 3b:** it goes for *that* PC first after the Shard-bearer. Its priorities are legible — that's the horror.

🔀 **They kill it with radiant:** the runes gutter out one by one — and for one heartbeat, as the last one dies, the script **resolves**: older, cleaner, the same tradition of old-Barovian warding as… *(let Lykta or Dallas make the check — DC 13 Arcana)* …**the wax seal on the letter that brought you here.** The same school. The same age. Say no more. *(This detonates in Session 3 with the Farewell letter.)*
🔀 **They drop it without radiant:** it collapses into rune-lit slag and Mist — and **re-forms within 24 hours** (Curse-Bound Rejuvenation). It will be waiting outside the sanctuary in Session 3. A fine outcome; they didn't *end* it.
🔀 **It's going badly** *(a PC dying, dice cruel)*: **Urwin and the Dawn** — he followed with two Keepers; dawn-light floods the dig-shaft, the Harbinger recoils and withdraws. Same move as 0.5. The floor, not the plan.

# MOVEMENT 5 — The Threshold *(close)*

▸ **Goal:** loot, the level, and a cliffhanger with a voice in it.

🎲 **Aftermath:**
- Where the Harbinger fell: the **Rune-Blade**, inert, too heavy to wield — but a fist-sized rune-etched fragment breaks off easily. *(Keepsake / prop. If someone insists on the blade itself, see [[Treasure & Magic Items]].)*
- The chapel-crypt crates: portable valuables per [[Treasure & Magic Items]].
- **Larnak's gold** waits on completion — the diggers are stopped, but the *digger* isn't. He'd say so, smiling.

🎲 They end at the **broken seal**. Green light below. The wet breathing. And then — clear, unhurried, amused, drifting up the spiral stair:

> "You *ended* the old warden. Even *he* never managed that." *(a pause; the breathing below stills, listening)* "Do come down. I so rarely get guests worth the candles."
