# Arc Plan — Four Sessions
*The current master plan. Supersedes [[Revised Session Flow — Post Session 0.5]]. Decisions locked 4 July 2026: **the Blood of the Vine tavern is cut**, the **Harbinger rematch lands in the upper catacombs (S2)**, everything happens **in and under the village churchyard** (no travel — canon Berez is ignored), the ending is **protected at the end of S3**.*

> [!warning] UPDATED 11 July 2026 — compressed to THREE sessions
> A missed week forced the squeeze. The structure holds, redistributed:
>
> - **S1** runs as written **but ends at the crypt's mouth**: Larnak's contract lands in the morning-after (not deferred), then level 7 → rest montage → Urwin's counsel → the march past the east wall → **cut on the shattered grave**, the Harbinger's blade dragging behind them.
> - **S2** = **the upper catacombs**: picks up at the grave's lip, straight down. Dig site → ossuary fight → chapel-crypt clues → **Harbinger rematch** → broken seal, the voice below. Level 8 + Boost Kit at the close.
> - **S3** = **the main crypt and the Lyssa showdown**: unchanged — the descent, the Trap, the Elder Evil choice, the Door Home and the letter.
> - **S4 is cut** as a planned session. The file survives purely as an emergency overflow valve if S3's ending won't land in time — but the goal is to land the letter in S3, full stop.
>
> Session files updated to match ([[Session 1]], [[Session 2]]). The escalation ladder, clue chain, and threads below are unchanged — only the *when* moved, not the *what*.

> [!warning] UPDATED 19 July 2026 — post-Session-1 reality + the Session 2 rewrite
> [[Session 1 — Actual Play]] changed the plan, and [[Session 2]] was **rewritten 19 July** to match. The standing cuts and changes:
>
> - **Ernst Larnak is CUT from the arc.** No fixer, no 2,500 gp contract. The party's hooks are already theirs: the "— L." note, the retreat trail north, and Donovich's plea. Clue #6 dies; Strahd's aiming now rests entirely on the **Invitation-letter bait** and the **wolves seeding** (Urwin: *"the Count doesn't want her here either"*). *Suggested S3 sting: a purse of 2,500 gp waits in the coach with the Farewell letter — payment, punctual and infuriating, for a contract they never signed.*
> - **The Ravenkind Shard is never handed over.** It stays Donovich's altar relic, unused this arc. The radiant answer is Lykta + Lucian's spells — optionally the **Gem of Brightness** moved early into the S2 workshop crates (a "sun-marked reliquary" Lyssa was about to ship out).
> - **Lyssa never appears in S2.** She is **named** — by the surviving thrall **Milos** and her own manifest — but stays unseen until S3, the big bad held in reserve. The S2 close may use her *voice* up the stair, nothing more.
> - **S2 is now a full mapped crypt level** (Neutral Party map, rooms keyed A–N in [[Session 2]]) entered via the **blood-of-the-line door — it opens on Lucian's blood** (the arc's pressure point, one session before her name-offer). Two combats: the **remnant** (the wounded S1 wight + ghouls) and the **Harbinger rematch** (its Vengeful Tracker re-keyed to *the blood that opened the door* — Lucian).

> [!abstract] The arc in one paragraph
> The party holds the church through the night (**S1**) — and clocks, on their own, that they were caught in the crossfire of a war: undead and dominated wolves *fighting each other*, the church merely in the way, and an elegant "— L." giving the orders. In the grey morning (**S2**) the battlefield, the note and Donovich's crest tour lead them north into the **old quarter of the same churchyard**, to a mausoleum door that drinks only the blood of its own line — and opens for **Lucian**. Below is Lyssa's dig from the inside — a von Zarovich family being chiselled out of its own tombs, her name finally spoken aloud by a broken survivor — where the **Tomb-Warden** they fled at the gate corners them in the rotunda of its own toppled likeness: the fight they can at last win, and proof the horrors of this valley can die. Below the broken seal (**S3**) waits the sanctuary proper: Lyssa's trap, the monsters she built with power from *beyond the Mists*, a bricked-up thing even Strahd chose to bury — and when her lab is dust, the Mists open onto their own road home: a black coach, a folded letter, a blood-red seal they've seen exactly once before. They came in as strangers, fought like heroes, and leave as a tool being put back in its drawer.

## What changed and why

> [!warning] The tavern is cut
> The party showed no interest; we honour that. **Do not push it.** Urwin: it's on his list, his people will handle it when it's safe. The tavern's jobs move as follows:
> - **Ravenkind Shard** → ~~Donovich's altar relic, handed over in S1~~ **CUT 19 July 2026 — never handed over.** It stays at the altar (optional omen: it hums as the party passes). Radiant duty falls to Lykta + Lucian; the **Gem of Brightness** can move early into the S2 crates.
> - **"Woken on purpose" lore** → Urwin, S1 morning-after: old evils stirred one after another, *"like someone's working down a list."*
> - **The elegant hand / "the lady"** → the **"— L." orders** found on a dead thrall after the siege (S1 morning-after graveyard search): *"the work at the old churchyard is nearly ready… keep the feather-folk busy. — L."* One prop now does the work of the courier letter + orders.
> - **Raven-and-sun mark** → carried by the Shard itself (Donovich's relic) and echoed, corrupted, in the sanctuary carvings (S3).
> - Backup content stays in [[Tavern — Blood o' the Vine (Backup)]] if the players ever surprise us.

> [!gmonly] The Harbinger rematch (S2, the crypt rotunda)
> It withdrew at the gate barely scratched; the players may believe they scared it off. **Use that confidence.** It comes because it is a *Tomb-Warden* — Lyssa's dig is the greatest desecration in the valley, and the party (standing among robbed graves, one of them having **opened the family door with his blood**) are exactly what it exists to punish. Its Vengeful Tracker has re-keyed to **Lucian**. Arena: the **rotunda of its own toppled statue**, with a shaft of grey daylight through the broken vaulting (full staging in [[Session 2]] Movement 7 and [[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade]]).
> - **Win condition:** radiant. They learned this at the gate (Starry Wisp made the runes gutter); now they have Lykta and Lucian's radiant, Level 7, and four fresh bodies — run the **legendary actions from round 1** (this party is long-range with Dallas at AC 24). This is still the fight designed to be won.
> - **The rune thread pays here:** Lykta clocked in 0.5 that the sword-runes are the *same runic family* as the letter's wax seal. When it dies to radiant, the runes gutter out one by one — and for a heartbeat resolve into the same tradition of old-Barovian warding the seal descends from. A whisper, not an answer. (Detonates in S3 with the Farewell letter.)
> - **Dropped without radiant:** it re-forms in 24 hours (Curse-Bound Rejuvenation) and becomes an S3 surface complication — watching, not fighting. Sequel bait, never a second boss.
