# Session 3 — "Treacherous Blood" *(the door, the fall & the long dark)*
> _The Warden steps off the dais. The door opens. And what waits below has been waiting for guests. Read **from** this at the table. Stat blocks embedded._
> *Rewritten 21 July 2026 post-[[Session 2 — Actual Play]] — the previous version (coach-and-letter ending) is in `_archive/`. Changes: **opens in initiative** at the S2 cliffhanger; **full lethality, no safety nets** — the party is L7, un-boosted, and a TPK is the likely (and planned-for) outcome; **Lyssa names herself at the Trap** (Milos and the manifest are cut); **the Farewell letter and coach are cut entirely** — Strahd's hand stays hidden, forever if need be; and if the party falls, the arc ends in **the Long Dark**: shallow graves, fifty years, and a very old Sef.*

> [!tip] How to use this
> Each beat has: **▸ Goal**, **📖 + [!readaloud]** (say it or riff it), **🎲 run it**, **🔀 if the players…**, and **⏭ move on when…**. Read-aloud callouts are the table-facing text; everything else is yours.

> [!tip] The shape of tonight
> **Cold open in initiative** — the dais completes its descent and the **Tomb-Warden** steps off into the lower cavern, the party at the great door with the opening mechanism under their hands. The fight is winnable (radiant, the geometry, the button) — and however it ends, the road leads **through the door**: a green-candled passage where the whispering voices turn out to have faces, down into the sanctuary — where **Lyssa von Zarovich finally appears, names herself, and springs the Trap.** Run it honestly and it will probably kill them. **Let it.** If they fall: **the Long Dark** — they wake in shallow graves fifty years later, dug out by an old woman with a spade and a raven-feather charm. If they stand: the Mists open, and they go home never knowing whose game they won.
>
> **Arc note:** all four PCs, **Level 7** — the Boost Kit never reached them (Urwin is topside; they never resurfaced). That gap is not a bug. It is the whole tragedy, honestly rolled.

> [!warning] Must-land beats — everything else is cuttable
> 1. **The Warden fight, resolved** — ended by radiant (Level 8 on the spot), driven off, or *escaped through the door* (it will not cross the threshold).
> 2. **★ LYSSA NAMES HERSELF** — her first appearance and her full name, from her own mouth, at the Trap. If the party is about to die, the name is the last thing they learn. Non-negotiable.
> 3. **The Trap at full lethality — NO safety nets.** The Mists do not swallow this fight. Nobody is saved by the plan.
> 4. **If they fall: the Long Dark epilogue, protected.** Give it its full time (~25 min) no matter what. It is the ending — and the door to everything after.
> 5. **If they stand: the Mists open** — compressed, warm, and with no letter. They leave never knowing.

> [!gmonly] The lethality contract — read this twice
> Every prior session had a floor: the wereraven rescue, the Mists-swallow net, "Strahd won't let his tools die." **Tonight the floors are OFF.** Strahd's protection was always about *usefulness* — and by tonight, win or lose, the party has already done their job: Lyssa's dig is exposed, her crew is dead, her warden-problem is loose in her stairwell. If they die in her Trap, that outcome suits the Count *fine* — she spends her strength killing them and he crushes what's left at leisure. Run both fights with real teeth: legendaries on, Mind Burst on clumps, focus-fire the healer if that's what the creatures would do. Kill with a straight face and full dignity — no gloating, no apology. The epilogue is not a consolation prize; it's the best scene in the arc.
>
> **The one mercy that stands:** if the party *retreats* — back up the dug hole, out of the crypt, to Urwin — let them go. Level 8 (if the Warden fell), the Boost Kit, a long rest, and a re-planned assault next session are all legitimate. The trap only kills people who walk into it. If they do this, tonight ends early and the finale moves; that's the players playing well, not the plan failing.

# MOVEMENT 2 — ★ COMBAT 1: The Warden at the Door *(45 minutes)* ★

▸ **Goal:** the rematch, at last — in the arena the players chose by digging deep. Winnable with radiant and brains; escapable through the door; survivable by retreat. Its quarry is Lucian.

🖼 ![[Scene - The Warden Descends.png|500]] *(then the Harbinger portrait as it steps off)* ![[The Harbinger of the Rune-Blade.png|300]]

📖 *(as initiative resolves)*

> [!readaloud]
> The dais grinds to rest on the cavern floor and the sound stops all at once — chain, stone, everything — so that the only noise left is the runes. They are not humming. They are **screaming.** It steps off the platform into the torchlight, unhurried as ever, and the cowl comes around — past Jasper, past the light, past everything — until it fixes on **Lucian.** The blood that opened the family's door. It has been waiting fifty generations to punish exactly this, and it does not hurry, because it has never needed to.

🎲 **Arena — the lower cavern:** roughly 60 × 50 ft of rough stone; the **dais** (a raised 15-ft platform, half cover behind its lip); the **stair remnants** (rubble, difficult terrain, climbable to a ledge ~15 ft up — the ranged perch); the **dug shaft** overhead (70 ft up to the crypt — a broom or a climb; the way OUT); and the **great door** with its two pressure-plate mechanisms.
- **★ Its quarry is Lucian** (Vengeful Tracker). It walks at him through everyone else; only Sentinel, a big radiant hit, or a grave-goods/Signet carrier pulls it aside. Say it plainly: *"the cowl never turns. It's here for* him*."* If Lucian wears the **Signet**, the runes flare when it faces him — recognition, and rage.
- **Radiant is the win condition:** Vulnerability + Guttered Runes. Lykta's Guiding Bolt/Starry Wisp/Moonbeam, Lucian's radiant, the **Chime** (it's CR-capped — useless here; let them learn that with one ring), holy water. *"Your steel rings off it — where the light lands, the runes go out like drowned coals."*
- **Legendaries ON from round 1** (2/round: Stride · Mist-Step ×2 · Reaping ×2) — this party is long-range with Dallas at AC 24; **Executioner's Arc** (Dex save) ignores AC and Mirror Image.
- **★ The button is live.** Any PC at the door can press the mechanism as an **action**: the great doors grind open over one round — green candlelight floods the cavern, the voices STOP mid-word — and the Warden **will not cross the threshold.** *(GM truth: what lies past that door was unmade from the family's ground — chiselled out of consecration in S2's own erasure. Its oath ends at the seal. It stops at the line like a dog at a fence, blade dragging, runes screaming after them.)* Fleeing through the door is escape — into the Trap. Frying pan, fire.
- **Retreat up the shaft** is also real: it does not fly. It will be waiting when they return — re-formed, patient.

🔀 **Ended by radiant:** the runes gutter out one by one — and for a heartbeat the script *resolves*, older and cleaner *(DC 13 Arcana — Dallas)*: the same tradition of old-Barovian warding as **the wax seal on the letter that brought them here.** Say no more — tonight it may never pay off, and that's allowed. **★ Lykta's whispers:** the soul-shaped hole is there again… and then it *isn't* — a release, a sentry relieved of a post it was sworn to past death. **Level 8, on the spot** — milestone, mid-session, earned.
🔀 **Dropped without radiant:** slag and Mist; it re-forms in 24 hours. No level yet.
🔀 **They flee through the door without resolving it:** it waits. It will be between them and the surface afterwards — an S4/sequel problem, or the thing that watches them climb out if they somehow win below.

# MOVEMENT 3 — The Green Passage *(15 minutes — dread, no combat)*

▸ **Goal:** the walk to the Trap. The voices get faces. Nobody attacks — that's what makes it terrible.

🖼 ![[Scene - The Sanctuary of Green Candles.png|500]]

📖 *(through the great doors)*

> [!readaloud]
> Beyond the door the dark is full of small green flames — candles, hundreds of them, in niches and on ledges and set into the floor along the edges of a descending passage, burning the colour of pond-ice and giving no heat at all. The air is cold, old, and *interested.* And standing along the walls, spaced like an honour guard, are the voices.
>
> They were people. They are beautiful now, in the way a knife is — pale, still, eyes catching the candlelight. None of them moves toward you. One inclines its head, courteous as a footman, and speaks with a mouth that has forgotten how: *"She is expecting you."*

🎲 **The sentinels** are her vampire spawn — **they do not fight here.** They walk ahead, they hold doors, they *usher*. If attacked, they melt backwards into the dark without answering (and rejoin the Trap — add their HP to her side later; let the players realise the courtesy was a mercy they spent). The whispers that called Lykta all this way came from these mouths — *"come to us"* was an **invitation list.**
🎲 **The Lens hums** near the candles (same wrong-elsewhere energy as the brine jars). **Lykta's whispers:** the dead down here don't whisper — they *wait*, the way a held breath waits.
🎲 Show the sanctuary map as the passage opens out. Iron doors ahead, and behind them — the breathing. Wet. Patient. Too many lungs.

⏭ **The last doors.**

# MOVEMENT 4 — ★ THE TRAP: Lyssa, Named *(the spine — 55 minutes)* ★

▸ **Goal:** her first appearance, her name from her own mouth, and a fight run at full lethality to whatever end the dice decree.

🖼 ![[Lyssa von Zarovich.png|300]]

📖 *(the sanctuary chamber)*

> [!readaloud]
> The chamber is a vault of green light — candles in their hundreds, an altar-become-worktable, and crates you recognise: the same stencils as the workshop far above. A pale woman in old, fine clothes turns from the worktable as though you were expected for dinner, and looks at you the way a hostess looks at guests who have tracked mud through the hall and are *still, somehow, welcome.*
>
> "There you are," she says. "You opened my family's door with the family's own blood. You woke the fonts. You ended the old warden — *ended* him — on my very doorstep." She smiles, and it is genuinely delighted, and that is the worst thing about it. "Fifty years of careful work in this valley and nobody has ever *impressed* me. Allow me the honour. I am **Lyssa. Lyssa von Zarovich** — the name your letter wore, the hand on every order you've read, the lady of every ruin you've walked through since the Mists were kind enough to bring you to me."
>
> *(a pale hand comes to rest, flat, on the great iron doors behind her)*
>
> "And these are my children. Let's see how they feed."

🎲 **★ The naming, landed.** This is the beat the whole arc has owed them — "— L." finally has a face, a voice, and a full name, delivered as a courtesy before the kill. Give it room. Let them talk back; she enjoys it. She confirms, airily, whatever they've earned: the siege was hers (*"a field test — you were a marvellous control group"*), the dig was hers, the prisoners were hers. She never apologises; it does not occur to her.

> [!gmonly] The Lucian beat
> Her eyes find him and *linger* — then the offer, once, genuinely meant: *"Blood owed a throne and given a gutter. We are the* same*, little cousin-of-nothing. Stand aside, and I will give you back your name."* **If he wears the Signet:** *"Oh — you found grandfather's ring. Keep it. It suits the conversation we're about to have."* His refusal (or hesitation) is the arc's emotional pressure point. Don't linger; the doors are already opening.

🎲 **★ THE FIGHT — full lethality:** she opens the iron doors and looses **2 Vampire Mind Flayers** — and fights beside them. **The safety nets are OFF** (see the lethality contract). Run it as the creatures would:
- **Mind Burst on clumps** (30-ft Emanation, extends *vertically* — the broom is not safe). Int DC 15, Incapacitated. **Two of them can chain-stun the party.** Telegraph every time — *"tentacles flare, pressure behind your eyes"* — and then roll it straight.
- **Grapple → Drink Sapience** (6d6 psychic) on stunned, isolated PCs. Jasper's Int save is +2. That is the kill condition and everyone at the table will feel it coming.
- **Lyssa targets the healer.** She has watched Lykta's radiant undo everything she builds. Shadow Stealth, dark, Bite. She is not cruel about it; she is *efficient*.
- **If the sentinels were attacked** in the passage, they join from the dark at the worst moment.
- **She still values her own existence:** if the party somehow turns it — both mind flayers down and her below half — she **flees as mist**, swearing revenge, never a corpse. She wins by surviving either way.
- **Radiant + the party's tools** are the only levers: the Chime works on nothing here; the holy water and silvered arrows and Starry Wisp are worth actions; the **scroll of Protection from Evil and Good** is worth its weight in lives. If they play perfectly they can live. That's the honest gap between *can* and *probably won't*.

# MOVEMENT 5 — ★ THE FALL *(if the party dies — 10 minutes, unhurried)* ★

▸ **Goal:** four deaths with dignity, and the lights out. No dice. No rescue. The table should believe the campaign just ended — because, for fifty years, it did.

🎲 **Stage it, don't grind it.** When the fight is decided (last PC drops, or the survivors are stunned and grappled), stop rolling. Narrate each PC's ending in one or two sentences — quiet, specific, no gore for its own sake. **Hand each player an index card:** *write your character's last thought.* Collect them face-down. (They come back in fifty years.)
- **Lykta's bridge** *(do this one last)*: as the dark comes down, the whispers she has heard all arc are suddenly very close and very calm — and for the first time, they are not saying *come to us.* They are saying ***"we have you."*** Then nothing.
- **Lyssa's last line**, over them, to no one: *"Better than the last strangers. Truly."* *(a beat)* *"Somebody put them somewhere the wolves won't dig."*

📖 *(then, to the table — flat, final)*

> [!readaloud]
> The green candles burn on with nobody to see them. Somewhere far above, rain moves across a valley that has already forgotten you — the way it forgot everyone who came before, and everyone who came after.
>
> Darkness. It lasts longer than you can imagine. And then —

⏭ **Straight into Movement 7. Do not pause for table talk. The graves are part of the same breath.**

# MOVEMENT 6 — If They Stand *(the other ending — compressed)*

▸ **Goal:** the miracle, honoured — warm, brief, and with **no letter.** They leave never knowing.

🎲 If the party genuinely breaks the Trap — mind flayers destroyed, Lyssa fled as mist swearing revenge — give them the sanctuary: her worktable (research notes, Bluetspur scrawl — sequel fuel), the crated grave-goods, the **Gem of Brightness** in a sun-marked reliquary case, gold per [[Treasure & Magic Items]]. The bricked east wall and what sleeps behind it **stay shut tonight** (sequel; don't advertise it). **Level 8** if not already banked.

📖 *(the surface, whenever they climb out)*

> [!readaloud]
> Dawn in Barovia is a brighter grey, but this one is trying. Urwin is at the grave's lip with half the Keepers and an expression he will deny for the rest of his life. Sef counts you out loud as you climb — *"…three, four. Good"* — and does not let go of Jasper's sleeve for some time.
>
> And in the days that follow, something in the valley… loosens. The Mists that have hemmed you like a wall since the night they took you draw back from the eastern road — and through them, impossibly, you can see the way home. Nobody can tell you why. Urwin says the Mists give, sometimes. He does not finish the sentence about what they usually want back.

🎲 **No coach. No letter. No signature.** They walk out with gold, scars, a raven-feather charm each (Sef insists), and a mystery they never solve. **Strahd's hand stays hidden — forever, unless a sequel earns it.** *(If Lykta asks about her raven-mail: no reply ever came. Let that itch.)*

> [!example] CLOSE (this branch): the road, the charm, the castle window lit behind them — and as they cross the treeline, it goes dark. Cut. Arc over — the sequel hooks (Lyssa lives; the wall below; the unanswered letter) keep.

# MOVEMENT 7 — ★ THE LONG DARK *(the true epilogue — 25 minutes, protected)* ★

▸ **Goal:** fifty years in one breath; four shallow graves; one very old woman. The campaign's ending and the next campaign's cold open, in the same scene. **Reveal almost nothing about what they now are.**

🖼 ![[Scene - The Long Dark.png|500]]

📖 *(from the darkness of Movement 5 — no pause)*

> [!readaloud]
> — rain. You hear rain before anything else. Then the weight — earth on your chest, not much of it, a hand-span of wet soil — and the oldest instinct there is takes over and you are **digging upward**, and the ground gives, and you are out: on hands and knees in cold mud, dragging air into lungs that had forgotten what it was for, rain running down a face that — check with your fingers — is still yours.
>
> It is night, in a graveyard you half-know, though the yew trees are taller and one of them has died. Beside you, three more low mounds of turned earth — moving. And over you stands an old woman, leaning on a spade, rain dripping off the hood of a travelling cloak, watching you be born with an expression you cannot read at all. A raven sits on her shoulder. Another. The trees, you realise slowly, are *full* of them, silent, every eye on you.
>
> Around her neck, on a leather cord worn thin with fifty years of touching: a raven-feather charm. You knew a girl who handed those out once. She said they were so she could find you.
>
> "Well," says Sef Martikov, sixty-five years old, in a voice that has been waiting half a century to say it. "Took me long enough."

🎲 **Play the reunion small and human.** She helps them out of the mud one at a time. She counts them — *"…three, four. Good"* — and her voice breaks on *good*, the only crack she allows. Then practical: a shuttered lantern, dry cloaks from a handcart, bread. She has done this scene in her head ten thousand times; she is *ready*.

🎲 **What she tells them** *(fragments — she answers what's asked, plainly, and no more)*:
- **Fifty years.** *"You died the night you went below. All four of you. I was fifteen. I'm not now."*
- **The war:** *"She lost. Not to you — after you. The Count doesn't share the valley."* The lady's works burned or sealed; her name is not spoken. *(GM truth: Lyssa was broken, not destroyed — diminished, sealed, sequel.)* **The valley is exactly the same.** That's the horror: their deaths changed nothing Barovia would admit to.
- **The others:** Urwin — dead these twenty years, *"flying ahead, he'd call it."* Donovich — buried by his own altar. The Keepers — scattered, a handful left, and every raven in these trees is one of hers or loyal to her. **She leads what remains.**
- **How:** she goes very still. *"There's a temple, high up, where the mountains keep their amber. The ravens won't fly over it. I walked in twenty years ago and I asked, and something answered, and we agreed on a price."* *(that's ALL she says. If pressed: "You'll know what I paid when you need to. Not tonight.")*
- **Why:** *"Because you held a door for people you'd never met."* *(beat)* *"And because nobody else was ever going to come for you."*

> [!gmonly] ★ THE HIDDEN GIFTS — reveal NOTHING at the table ★
> Each PC carries a Dark Gift from the Amber Temple's vestiges — the price of four souls — and **the players learn none of it tonight.** No mechanics, no names, no descriptions. At most, ONE sensory wrongness each, delivered flat and never explained; discovery belongs to the next campaign. A menu (pick/replace freely — decide the actual gifts between campaigns, not now):
> - **Lucian:** his reflection in a puddle is a half-second behind him. *(Say nothing else.)*
> - **Lykta:** silence. For the first time since childhood, the dead aren't whispering — or rather, they've stopped talking *about* her and started *waiting for orders.* Tonight it just feels like quiet.
> - **Dallas:** he cups a hand out of habit and the light he conjures comes out **amber**, and warm, and doesn't flicker in the rain. He didn't cast anything.
> - **Jasper:** every scar he ever had is gone — including the ones he was fond of. Under his ribs, something keeps time that isn't quite a heartbeat. His old notebook, buried with him, is dry.
> **Sef's price (GM-only, hold it):** she cannot change any more. The vestige took her wings — a wereraven who will never fly again, standing under trees full of ravens. If a player ever asks why she doesn't just *shift*… that's a next-campaign scene.

🎲 **Return the index cards.** Each player reads their character's last thought aloud — fifty years old now — and says one line about opening their eyes. That's the character's first breath of the new campaign.

📖 *(the close)*

> [!readaloud]
> The rain eases. Down the slope, where the village was, a handful of lights still burn — fewer than you remember, but burning. And high on its mountain, patient as ever, the castle sits exactly where you left it, exactly as it has sat for four hundred years, and one window — the same window — is lit.
>
> Fifty years, and the valley never even noticed you were gone. Sef shoulders the spade like a woman half her age and looks back at the four of you, mud-born and breathing.
>
> "Come on, then," she says. "You've been dead half a century. There's work."

> [!example] CLOSE THE ARC — the Long Dark
> - **Cut there.** No mechanics, no levelling, no gift reveals, no questions answered. The next campaign opens on this exact spot.
> - **What carries forward (GM ledger):** four hidden Dark Gifts · Sef's unpaid-for price · Lyssa diminished-not-dead · the Harbinger (if never ended by radiant, it re-formed — fifty years of it is a sequel gift) · the sealed east wall below · Lykta's letter, never answered — *Strahd knows they wrote, and Strahd knows they're back* · the Amber Temple, which now holds four IOUs.
> - **Strahd's hand: still never shown.** Fifty years of restraint. When it finally lands, it will be worth it.
> - Debrief the table gently — a TPK that becomes a cold open needs five minutes of "that was the plan, you magnificent doomed idiots" honesty. Then pack up.
