# Revised Session Flow — Post Session 0.5
*Updated after the actual play on 29 June 2026. The party deviated from the planned route — see [[Session 0.5 — Actual Play]] for what happened.*

> [!warning] SUPERSEDED — 4 July 2026
> This note assumed **three** remaining sessions. The plan is now **four**: see **[[Arc Plan — Four Sessions]]** for the current master plan (tavern cut, Harbinger rematch in S2, finale split into S3 + S4). Kept for the deviation record only.

> [!warning] Key deviation
> Players went to the **church** instead of the Blood of the Vine Tavern. They showed low interest in the tavern. The church siege (originally Session 2 content) is now Session 1 content. Everything shifts forward.

## Where we actually are at the start of Session 1

- The party is **in the church bell tower**, looking out at wolves and undead massing at the cemetery edge.
- **David's PC** is spotted running toward the church through the mist — his arrival is the session cold open.
- **Father Donovich** and ~10 adults upstairs; 4 families with children in the undercroft below.
- **Urwin Martikov** is present (in human guise still — he hasn't revealed his wereraven nature).
- The Harbinger is out there somewhere, barely hurt. The party doesn't know this.
- The Blood of the Vine Tavern is mentioned but unexplored. The party's contract with Urwin is verbal/in-progress.

---

## The Blood of the Vine question

The party showed no interest in exploring the tavern. Options going forward:

### Option A — Skip it entirely *(recommended if time is tight)*
Urwin tells them the morning after the siege: the sisters are beyond saving — he'll send his own people in when it's safe. The party gets the lore through a brief conversation (the sisters were turned, something ancient woke under the tavern, someone did this on purpose). No dungeon crawl.

**What you lose:** the Arik the Barkeep atmosphere, the Ravenkind Shard as a reward from below the tavern, the Sorvina / buried-temple horror.
**How to compensate:** Move the **Ravenkind Shard** — place it in the church undercroft (a relic of the Dawn that Donovich has been protecting), or have Urwin produce it from the Keepers' stores as thanks after the siege. Keep the lore beat (someone woke this deliberately) as something Urwin shares.

### Option B — Compressed tavern (30 min, Session 1 mid-point)
Morning after the siege, the party goes in for a quick look. Run only **one** of the three sister fights (Alenka is the most fun — the stalker on the ground floor). Skip Mirabel and Sorvina unless the players are engaged. The important beat is the **journal** that explains the "woken on purpose" plot — hand that out, then get them out.

**What you lose:** the buried temple and Sorvina entirely.
**How to compensate:** Same Shard move as Option A.

### Option C — Let the players decide
Mention the tavern is still out there after the siege. If they want to go, run it. If they don't, that's the story.

## Revised session-by-session plan

### Session 1 — "The Night the Church Held"
*Picks up in the bell tower. David's PC arrives.*

**Movements:**
1. **David joins** — stumbles out of the mist to the church gate; brief reunion beat, get him up to speed fast.
2. **The siege** — waves of wolves and undead through the night. Braun family in the undercroft. Urwin at the walls. Father Donovich holding the altar. Use the existing [[Session 2 — Run Guide]] siege content for this — it maps directly.
3. **The dawn** — creatures withdraw at first grey light. The cost: count who survived.
4. **Morning after** — Urwin's reveal (wereraven, Keepers of the Feather). The "woken on purpose" truth. He asks them to go to the **old churchyard** to stop whoever is digging. *(Optionally: the tavern as a detour.)*
5. **Level up to 7** at the end of this session (milestone — was originally post-Session 1 tavern).

> [!gmonly] Combat load warning
> The siege was planned for a fresh Session 2 party. Running it as Session 1 with three PCs coming off a Harbinger fight is brutal. Lean on the defensive positioning, not wave after wave. Give them the bell tower advantage and the church's radiant-symbol door as mechanical anchors. David arriving mid-siege (early in the session) evens the odds.

**Key NPCs in play:** Father Donovich, Urwin Martikov (reveal), the Braun family, Sef Martikov (first appearance).

**What to hand out:** The Keeper's Blessing / Boost Kit (Urwin, before the old churchyard), the Ravenkind Shard (moved here from the tavern — Donovich's altar relic, or Urwin's gift).

---

### Session 2 — "Feathers in the Dark" *(or retitled)*
*The old churchyard. Lyssa von Zarovich's operation.*

The original Session 2 content was the church siege — that's now Session 1. Session 2 becomes the **old churchyard investigation**, which was originally the Session 3 open.

**Movements:**
1. Travel to the old churchyard — Ernst Larnak's offer (or Urwin's intelligence).
2. The dig site — cultists, undead, and something wrong under the earth.
3. **Lyssa's entrance** — she watches, taunts, reveals herself as the architect. The arc's emotional pressure point lands on Lucian especially.
4. **The Harbinger rematch** — now the party has the Shard and levels. This is the fight they can actually win (or at least hurt it properly).
5. Lyssa escapes. The party knows who the enemy is.

**Level up to 8** after this session.

No structural changes — this session was always the confrontation. The party arrives with better context now (they've faced Lyssa once, they have the Shard, they know the stakes personally through Lucian's hook).

## Session 1 opener — revised read-aloud

> [!readaloud]
> Last we left you: a campfire on a familiar road — and a fog that rose the wrong way and swallowed it whole. You woke in Barovia — black pines, a bruise-coloured sky, a castle on the mountain that watches and never comes closer. Something tall and patient walked out of the mist toward you with a runed blade, and your steel barely slowed it — but you found the high ground, and it stepped back into the fog. A man named Urwin came out of the dark: grieving, plain, and certain. He made you an offer, and walked you through a village that doesn't come out at night, to the only lit building left — a church with its symbol of the dawn worn almost to nothing, and a priest holding a candle in shaking hands, and children in the undercroft who look like the life is being slowly drained out of them.
>
> You know the wolves are at the cemetery wall. You know the undead are coming. And now — there, through the mist — *your* missing companion is running toward the gate.
>
> *(David's PC joins.)* Someone you know is still standing. For now.
