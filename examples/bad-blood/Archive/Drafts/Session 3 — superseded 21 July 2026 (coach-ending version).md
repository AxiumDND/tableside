# MOVEMENT 1 — The Descent *(guardians on the stairs)*

▸ **Goal:** the warm-up that teaches them how vampires fight, before the real trap.

🖼 ![[Scene - The Green Descent.png|500]]

📖 *(picking up at the sealed stair — green light, the spiral)*

> [!readaloud]
> The stair goes down further than the village is wide. The green light rises to meet you — candles, hundreds of them, burning the colour of pond-ice and giving no heat. And on the last landing, where the stair opens into dressed stone and old heraldry, two figures peel out of the wall-shadows like paint sliding off stone. They were beautiful once. They are *fast* now.

🎲 **2 Vampire Spawn** on the stairs — a tight space they can't spread in. They **Spider-Climb** the walls and ceiling, **grapple-bite** an isolated PC (max-HP drain), and have **no regeneration** (2024). Weaknesses: radiant, running water, a stake while Incapacitated.

🔀 **Radiant is brutal here** — and if the party recovered the **Gem of Brightness** from Lyssa's crates, its Daylight burst is 20 Radiant per turn to any spawn starting in the light, plus Disadvantage (Sunlight Hypersensitivity). Let it sing; it's her own plunder killing her servants.
🔀 **Loud fight?** Lyssa below is **forewarned** — composed, monologue polished. **Fast and quiet?** She's mid-instruction to her servants when they walk in — genuinely surprised for half a second. Reward the subtle approach.

**⚔️ [[Vampire Spawn]] ×2** — *CR 5 · AC 16 · HP 90 · Claw +6 (Grapple DC 13) · Bite +6 (3d6 Necrotic, HP max drain) · Spider Climb · Sunlight Hypersensitivity (20 Radiant/turn in magical sunlight, e.g. the Gem's burst)*

  - 2: Vampire Spawn

# MOVEMENT 2 — The Trap *(the session's spine)*

▸ **Goal:** the villain monologues, unleashes her experiment, and flees. The party survives the spike.

🖼 ![[Scene - The Sanctuary of Green Candles.png|500]] *(the chamber — then swap to her portrait when she turns)* ![[Lyssa von Zarovich.png|300]]

📖

> [!readaloud]
> The stairs open into a vaulted stone chamber lit by the green-burning candles. A pale woman in old, fine clothes turns to greet you as though you were expected for dinner — the first time any of you have seen her, and somehow exactly what the elegant hand promised. Behind her: two great iron doors. And behind *those* — the breathing. Wet, patient, too many lungs.
> "You're not his, are you?" she says, studying you. "No. His dogs have a certain *smell.*" *(her eyes find Lucian, and linger)* "And you've been *busy.* My door, opened with the family's own blood. The old warden, ended on my doorstep — even *he* never managed that. You have no idea what a compliment I'm about to pay you."

🎲 **Let [[Lyssa von Zarovich]] gloat** — she *wants* them to understand what she's built:

> [!readaloud]
> "I've made something new. Something the Count's old tricks can't touch. He's so *proud*, you know — so sure no one could build a better monster than himself." *(a pale hand flat on the iron door)* "Let's see how it feeds."

> [!gmonly] The Lucian beat
> She singles him out mid-fight or mid-monologue — *"Blood owed a throne and given a gutter. We are the* same*, little cousin-of-nothing. Stand aside and I'll give you back your name."* One offer, once, genuinely meant. His refusal (or hesitation) is the arc's emotional pressure point. Don't linger; the doors are already opening.
> **If he wears the Signet of the Quiet Dead** (S2, Room G): she clocks it the instant she sees his hand — *"Oh. You found grandfather's ring. Keep it — it suits the conversation we're about to have."* The offer lands sharper, not safer.

🖼 ![[Scene - The Iron Doors Open.png|500]]

🎲 **The fight.** She opens the doors and looses **2 Vampire Mind Flayers**. She fights too — but **flees the instant she's bloodied (~71 HP)**: mist-form, up the walls, gone, *swearing revenge*. **She escapes. She is never a corpse in this arc.** Let her go.

> [!tip] Mind Burst is the killer — 30-ft Emanation, Int DC 15 or Incapacitated. Two of them can chain-stun a clumped party. Telegraph every time (*"tentacles flare — pressure builds behind your eyes"*) so they spread. Grapple → Drink Sapience (6d6 psychic) on a stunned isolated PC kills fast — pull the safety net *before* a wipe, not after.

> [!gmonly] ★ The experiment seeds detonate here (planted in S2)
> Both mind flayers wear **prisoner tattoos** on their forearms — and if the party read the workshop tally, **one of them carries Grigor's number**: Milos's friend, the one who "came back up and *nodded*." It still moves like it half-remembers being a man — a beat of hesitation before it attacks whoever spoke to Milos kindly. Don't soften the fight with it; just let someone notice mid-combat and feel sick. This is what "the deep change" *is* — prisoners, remade with something from beyond the Mists. The journal in Movement 3 supplies the where-from.

🔀 **Radiant wrecks the mind flayers** — and the **Gem's Daylight burst** (if they have it) triggers Sunlight Hypersensitivity for 20 Radiant a turn. The whole arc has been building to this. Let it pay off completely.

**⚔️ [[Lyssa von Zarovich]]** *(Vampire Nightbringer)* — *CR 8 · AC 16 · HP 142 · **flees at ~71 HP** · Bite +7 (3d6 Necrotic) · Shadow Strike +7 (4d6 Cold) · Shadow Stealth BA · fly 30 ft.*

**⚔️ [[Vampire Mind Flayer]] ×2** — *CR 5 · AC 15 · HP 85 · Mind Burst Recharge 5-6 (30-ft Emanation, Int DC 15 → Incapacitated) · Spider Climb · Sunlight Hypersensitivity (20 Radiant/turn in magical sunlight)*

```encounter
name: Movement 2 — The Trap
players: true
creatures:
  - 1: Vampire Nightbringer (Lyssa von Zarovich)
  - 2: Vampire Mind Flayer
```

> [!gmonly] ⛑ Safety net
> If the party is about to wipe, the **Mists swallow the fight** and they wake in the churchyard, alive, stripped of nothing but certainty. Strahd won't let his instruments die before the job's done — and that *reinforces the twist.* Use once only. Full branches in [[Going Off-Script & Failure States]].

⏭ **Move on** once Lyssa's fled and the servants are down. Let them breathe. Then the crypt.

# MOVEMENT 3 — The Crypt & the Hidden Wall

▸ **Goal:** the Bluetspur payoff and a *choice* — crack the wall or not.

🖼 ![[Scene - The Failed Experiment.png|500]]

📖 *(north chamber)*

> [!readaloud]
> Colder still. On a stone bier lies the bloodless husk of a mind flayer — drained, discarded, an early failure. On its forearm, under the grey skin: a **crude tattooed number, struck through** — the same hand, the same slate-column strike, as the workshop tally upstairs. *(If anyone checks the tally: it's not Grigor's number, and not Vasha's. There were* more*.)* Beside it: a leather journal, its pages crawling with frantic handwriting. Dreams that aren't dreams. A grey city under a green sky. *Something* reaching back through the sleep of everyone who digs here.

🎲 The journal is a **Mist talisman keyed to Bluetspur** — the payoff of the "from beyond the Mists" thread running since the church prototype. Reading too long: DC 13 Wisdom save or a brief shared nightmare (flavour, not a death-trap). **Lykta hook:** if she listens with her whispers, the journal *whispers back* — and for one second, among the alien murmur, a voice she recognises. Don't say whose. If they take it: sequel hook and an alternate way home.

🎲 **The Hidden Chamber:** a PC who takes the **Study action** on the central room (DC 16 Investigation) finds a **bricked-over chamber in the east wall** — newer mortar, careful work. Breaking through: **1 hour** (half with Mason's Tools). **Let them choose.** Don't force it; don't warn them off. The breathing they heard from the stairs has stopped — which is worse.

⏭ Either they crack the wall (→ Movement 4) or they don't (→ Movement 5). *Check the clock before they start hammering.*

# MOVEMENT 4 — The Elder Evil *(optional — fresh parties only)*

▸ **Goal:** a genuine "should we even open this?" decision. For a battered party the right answer is *no* — and that's not a failure.

🖼 ![[Scene - The Elder Evil.png|500]]

📖

> [!readaloud]
> The last brick comes free, and the breath that rolls out is older than the woods, older than the castle on the hill. In the dark beyond sits a single coffin, its lid scored with claw-marks *from the inside* — and something within has just, after a very long time, opened its eyes.

🎲 Opening the coffin releases a **Vampire Nosferatu** (Regeneration 10 — **radiant shuts it off** — Blood Frenzy, a 6d8 **Blood Spew** cone) and a **Swarm of Infesting Insects**. Both fight to the death. In the coffin: a **Gem of Brightness**.

> [!gmonly] Whose coffin this is
> **Strahd buried this thing.** The claw-marks are from *inside*; the wards Lyssa's crew chiselled off the seal upstairs were *his*. If the party works that out (Religion/History DC 15 on the coffin's binding-chains), it lands a quiet, horrible note before the letter does: *the Count uses this valley to bury his problems — and tonight, that's been you, cleaning one up.*

# MOVEMENT 5 — The Door Home *(protect this beat)*

▸ **Goal:** the Mists open; the twist lands in their hands via the letter. This is the whole arc paying off — give it room. **Do not rush it.**

🎲 Lyssa's routed; her lab is dust and green wax. They climb back up past the broken seal, through the ossuary, into the churchyard — and the air is *different*. Show **Castle Ravenloft**. Drop the music to almost-peaceful.

> [!readaloud]
> The job is done. And the Mists, which have hemmed this valley like a wall since the night they took you — *open.*

🖼 ![[Scene - The Black Coach.png|500]]

📖 *(the send-off — recommended **Unseen** version, just the coach)*

> [!readaloud]
> It waits where the Mist still curls, between the leaning headstones: a single black coach, no driver, the door already open. On the seat, two things. A folded square of cloth wrapped around something small and *familiar* — and a letter, sealed in blood-red wax, pressed with a crest you've seen exactly once before: on the night a fog you don't remember walking into swallowed your road.

🎲 **★ Now hand them [[Letter — Strahd's Farewell]] — the physical prop — and go quiet while they read it.** Don't narrate over them. Watch them connect the red "Z" seal to the Invitation — and (if they made the S2 Arcana read) to the *warding script on the Tomb-Warden's blade.* Let the silence do the work.

🔀 *(If you want Strahd seen — the Host option:)*

> [!readaloud]
> A figure stands at the Mist's edge where a heartbeat ago there was none — tall, pale, immaculate, unbothered by the cold. He doesn't approach. "You kept your half of a bargain you never knew you'd made," he says, almost fondly. A glance at the letter on the seat. "The rest, I've written down. Read it on the road." A small, courteous incline of the head — and when you blink, the road home is there, and he is not.

📖 *(as they finish reading)*

> [!readaloud]
> The Mist beyond the coach is thinning, and through it you can see — *impossibly* — your own road home. Behind you, far up the mountain, a single window in a distant castle is lit. As you watch… it goes dark.

> [!example] CLOSE THE ARC
> - **Don't explain the twist.** Let the open door feel like a leash, not a kindness. A sharp table connects the bait talisman (0.5), the blood door and the rune-script (S2), and this — themselves.
> - **What comes home with them:** the **purse in the coach** (2,500 gp — payment, punctual and infuriating, for a contract they never signed), the **Gem of Brightness** (if won), the Rune-Blade fragment, any Dark Gift. The Mists let go; the Dark Powers' bargain does not.
> - **Dangling threads:** Lyssa escaped, swearing revenge. The Bluetspur talisman. The Harbinger (if only driven off). And the Devil of Barovia knows their faces — and found them *useful.*
> - Let the last beat be silence. Then call it. *(Debrief prompts in [[Session 4]] if there's time and appetite.)*
