**Show to players** sends this picture with no pins.

## A1 — Great doors

South steps and the double doors. Wave 1 comes **in** here. Pews already going over as barricades just inside.

> [!gmonly]
> First body hits hard enough to crack the timber. Two Keeper kin take posts by the doors. Jasper has no darkvision — a grabbed candle here matters later.

## A2 — Nave

Central aisle, smashed pews left and right, candles along the walls. Villagers start on the pews; they get moved below before the doors go.

> [!readaloud]
> The church makes itself understood in a glance. Great doors, shuttered windows, pews already going over onto their sides as barricades.

🎲 Ghouls pressure the **floor** — paralysis punishes the front line, they don't climb.

## A3 — Altar

Stone altar in the aisle before the north apse. [[Ravenkind Shard]] lives here until Donovich presses it into a PC's hands.

> [!gmonly]
> If nobody claimed the Shard, Donovich gives it to Jasper. Cut Urwin's briefing the moment the ravens scream.

## A4 — Priest's cell

Southwest room: two simple beds. Donovich's spare cell — not a fight space unless someone hides here.

## A5 — Vestry

Southeast room with the desk. Church papers, a taper box, nothing that solves the siege. Good place to stash a PC who ducks out of the nave.

## A6 — Side chamber

Northwest of the south wing. Debris, a chair. Overflow for villagers before the undercroft is sealed.

## A7 — North shed

Small stone structure north of the apse, outside. Ignore unless someone goes around the building. Churchyard fighting (Sef, Spawn A) is **outside** this floor — use the graveyard map if you cut away.

## Not on this floor

- **Gallery / bell loft** — overwatch, Lykta's natural perch, Lucian's tower stair. Map: `MAP Curse of Strahd - Village of Barovia - Church.webp`
- **Undercroft** — Brauns sealed below, one trapdoor. Map: `MAP Curse of Strahd - Village of Barovia - Church basement.webp`
