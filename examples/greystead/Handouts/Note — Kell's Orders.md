# Note — Kell's Orders

[!gmonly]
**On [[Kell]]** — ridge lookouts, Combat 1. If they skip the search, it is in his inner coat when they bind him, or [[Hobb]] finds it if they drag a body back. This is the bigger plot. Show the picture. Do not name the Warden.
[!/gmonly]

Cheap paper, oil-stained. A clipped silver coin with a bowl-stamp is folded inside.

![[Note — Kell's Orders.png]]

> Kell —
>
> Greystead is the third mouth tonight.
> The girl will walk to you. Do not take her from the inn. The town must say bandits.
> When her blood is in the bowl, do not wait for a sign. Millhollow is already wet.
> Saltmere sings.
>
> If the priest fails, the ridge still owes us a name. There are other Crowes.
>
> — *the Warden*

[!gmonly]
Other Crowes: a cousin at **Thornwick**, an old aunt on the Ford road. Use one if they ask. The clipped silver matches what [[Joss]] was paid.
[!/gmonly]
