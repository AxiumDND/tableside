Battlemap for [[Session 1]]. Gazetteer: [[The Ridge Cave]]. Pins are DM-only.

## A — Mouth

Trip-line: DC 12 Perception; DC 12 Dex or prone and 1d4. Fresh mud. Tin cup.

> [!readaloud]
> Daylight dies two steps in. Twine at ankle height if nobody cut it.

## B — False camp

Stolen crates (Ril’s brand), empty bedrolls, a painted bandit banner. DC 12 Insight or Investigation: nobody slept here. In a crate: [[Scrap — Millhollow Is Wet]].

## C — Choke

Narrow. 10-ft drop (DC 12 Acrobatics or 1d6). Snare: same DC, restrained until an action frees them.

## D — Cells

Lira’s cloak. [[Ril]] in the far cage, alive.

## E — Pale Well

Bowl, salt ring, [[Lira Crowe]] bound, [[Vesper]] mid-chant, three [[Cultist]]s. After: [[Hymn — The Nine Mouths]] in Vesper’s sleeve or under the bowl.

**Stop clean:** drop Vesper, break the salt (not the bowl), or get Lira out of the ring.

**Mess it up:** smash the bowl, finish the chant, or kill Lira. Echo: DC 12 Wis or Frightened and 1d4 psychic, one round, then gone.
