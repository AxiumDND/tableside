## Hire

![[The Grey Mare.webp]] ![[Alden Crowe.webp]]

> [!readaloud]
> Greystead sits on a limestone ridge like a scab. Palisade, mill, one inn. Late morning, and the Grey Mare still smells of last night’s fire. The mayor’s purse is already on the corner table. He has not slept. *“My daughter Lira. Last night. I will pay what I have if you bring her home.”*

[[Alden Crowe]] — **25 gp** now, **25 gp** when she is back, **10 gp** if they flee. He thinks **bandits** took her to a cave on the ridge. Everyone in town says so. He will not hear “cult.”

[[Marta Fenn]] already has bowls down. She wants her house quiet. She will say Lira left angry. She will not name the grey cloak unless asked.

They woke here. Marta already knows their names.

## Village — any two clues

Do not make them collect all six. Any **two** send them to [[The Ridge Road]].

### [[Mayor's House]] — Lira’s room

![[Lira Crowe.webp]] ![[Letter — Come After Last Call.png]]

> [!readaloud]
> A two-storey house, muddy yard, a girl’s room too neat. The bed is made. The cloak is gone. On the sill sits a white flower that does not grow in the street.

- Muddy boots. No broken latch. DC 12 Investigation: she packed. She was not dragged.
- Under the flower: [[Letter — Come After Last Call]]. **Show it.**

### [[The Grey Mare]] — gossip

[[Marta Fenn]] · [[Old Tam]]

> [!readaloud]
> Old Tam has the fire and a cup he has not paid for. *“Grey cloak, two nights back. Asked after the Crowe girl like he already knew the house. Paid in clipped silver. She left after last call — angry, not taken.”*

Marta, if pressed: *“She was angry. Not taken from my floor.”*

### The common — shepherd

> [!readaloud]
> A boy with mud to the knee points at the ridge. *“Lights after midnight. And they were singing. Not drinking. A low drone, like a hive in the stone.”*

### [[The Mill]]

> [!readaloud]
> The wheel still turns. The miller’s boy talks too fast. *“Covered cart at dawn. Two of them. Something under the canvas the size of a person. They went up the ridge track — not the valley road.”*

### [[Shrine of the Hearth]] — [[Brother Pell]]

> [!readaloud]
> The hearth-stone has been scraped raw. Salt in the cracks. Pell’s hands shake when he blesses the water. *“Someone cleaned a circle off my floor last week. I did not do that. I will not go to the cave.”*

He will give one flask of holy water, no charge. He is scared, not guilty.

### [[Joss]] and [[Pellick]] — red herring

> [!readaloud]
> Two men by the palisade gate, trying not to look busy. Joss has new silver in his purse. *“We didn’t take no mayor’s girl. We were paid to stay off the ridge. Eight sp. Three nights. That was the job.”*

They robbed a tinker last month. They did **not** take Lira. They fold if pressed. The coins are **clipped**, each stamped with a shallow bowl — same stamp as [[Note — Kell's Orders]].

## [[Pale Well Caves]]

Open the map. Pins are DM-only. Gazetteer: [[The Ridge Cave]].

![[Pale Well Caves.webp]]

### A — Mouth

> [!readaloud]
> Daylight dies two steps in. Cold air. Lime. Twine at ankle height if nobody cut it.

Trip-line: DC 12 Perception; DC 12 Dex or prone and 1d4, not lethal. Fresh mud. A tin cup. No real watch if Combat 1 already happened.

### B — False camp

> [!readaloud]
> Stolen crates. Empty bedrolls. A painted banner that says *bandits* a little too clearly. Nobody has slept here.

DC 12 Insight or Investigation: the bedrolls are a costume. Crate brand matches [[Ril]]. In the straw: [[Scrap — Millhollow Is Wet]]. **Show it.**

### C — Choke

> [!readaloud]
> The tunnel pinches. A ten-foot drop to one side, wet and narrow. Something waits for an ankle.

Drop: DC 12 Acrobatics or 1d6 bludgeoning. Snare: same DC, restrained until an action frees them.

### D — Cells

> [!readaloud]
> Lira’s cloak on a hook. In the far cage, a man with one eye swollen. He flinches from the light. *“Grey robes. A bowl. She walked until they bound her. I will not go back down there.”*

[[Ril]] is alive. He will not fight. If they missed the room: her satchel has [[Letter — Come After Last Call]]. If they missed B: he saw a courier drop a scrap in the crates.

### E — Pale Well

![[Vesper.webp]]

> [!readaloud]
> A round chamber. A stone bowl set in the floor like a well-mouth, full of still white water. Chalk and salt in a ring. Lira is bound at the far side. A thin man in grey is mid-chant. Three others hold sickles and do not look up until you make them.

## ⚔️ Combat 2 — the Pale Well

**Combatants:** [[Vesper]] · [[Cultist]] ×3 · party

| | |
|---|---|
| **Vesper** | AC **13** · HP **11** · Mace +4 (1d6+2 + 1d4 radiant) · Radiant Flame +4 60 ft. (2d6) |
| **Cultist ×3** | AC **12** · HP **9** · sickle as above |
| **Lira** | Bound, AC 10, 6 HP — stray harm can kill her |

- **3 rounds** of chant from when they enter. Telegraph each round (*the water brightens / the ring hums / the last word is in his mouth*).
- **Stop clean** — any one: drop [[Vesper]]; break the **salt circle** (action, adjacent) **without** hitting the bowl; get Lira out of the ring (action to cut bonds, then move her).
- **Mess it up:** smash the bowl, let the chant finish, or kill Lira.
- Search Vesper / the bowl: [[Hymn — The Nine Mouths]]. **Show it.**

## Endings

### They stop it

> [!readaloud]
> The chant breaks. The water goes dull. Lira is shaking and alive. Outside, Greystead sounds like a village again — a dog, a mill wheel, someone laughing too loud because they can. Alden pays the rest without counting it. Marta puts a meal down and does not ask questions.

If they captured [[Vesper]]: *“The well was always here.”* The papers still say **two other mouths answered tonight**. Do not explain that.

### They mess it up

> [!readaloud]
> White water hits the floor — or the last word lands. For one breath the chamber is too full. Then it is only stone again. Lira’s eyes, if she lives, are white. At dawn the village well tastes of metal, and the birds do not sing.

One round: everyone in E, DC 12 Wisdom or **Frightened** until the end of their next turn, and 1d4 psychic. Then it is gone. Greystead counted. The hymn says the sleeper turned. Do not explain it.

### They flee

> [!readaloud]
> The ridge is still singing when you reach the palisade. In the morning Lira is gone. Alden puts ten gold on the table and does not look at you.

The ridge sings again in a week.

### After any ending

If they have the papers: next town is **Millhollow**, a day down-valley. The Warden rides the valley road at week’s end. See [[Handouts]].
