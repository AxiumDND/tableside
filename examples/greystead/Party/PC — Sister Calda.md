*A hearth-priest who came to bless a wedding and stayed for a missing girl. She wants Lira home and the shrine unsullied.*

## At the table

- **Saves** — WIS +5
- **Skills** — Insight, Medicine
- **Signature tricks** — Bless on the first fight; Cure Wounds on whoever drops

> [!gmonly]
> She will not smash the bowl. If someone else does, she knows something went wrong.

- **Look** — Cropped hair, wooden flame-token
- **Manner** — Calm, then sharp
- **Quotes** — *"Stand up. We are not done."*
