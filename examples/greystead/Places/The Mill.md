[!readaloud]
The wheel turns. A boy with flour in his hair will tell you, if you ask, that a covered cart went up the ridge at dawn. Not down the valley.
[!/readaloud]

## At a glance

- **Notice** — Cart ruts still wet
- **Fantastic** — Nothing
- **Dangerous** — The mill race if someone is shoved

[!gmonly]
The boy is a clue, not a combatant. The cart carried Lira.
[!/gmonly]
